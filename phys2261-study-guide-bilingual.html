What this chapter does

Thermodynamics describes huge numbers of particles with a handful of numbers (\(P\), \(V\), \(T\), \(U\), …). This chapter sets up the vocabulary for that: what a "system" is, what kinds of changes it can undergo, which quantities describe it, and, most importantly, why temperature is a well-defined thing you can measure at all.热力学以少数几个量（\(P\)、\(V\)、\(T\)、\(U\)，……）描述数量庞大的粒子。本章为此建立相应的术语体系：什么是"系统"，系统可以经历哪些类型的变化，用哪些量描述它，以及最重要的一点——温度为何是一个良定义、可测量的量。

After this chapter you should be able to

  • tell extensive from intensive variables;区分广延量与强度量；
  • explain what the Zeroth Law guarantees and how the Kelvin and ideal-gas temperature scales are built.说明第零定律保证了什么，以及开尔文温标与理想气体温标如何建立。

1.1  Systems and their walls1.1 系统及其壁

A thermodynamic system is whatever piece of the world we choose to study: a gas in a cylinder, a cup of coffee, a star. Everything else is the surroundings. The boundary between them is called a wall, and what matters about a wall is what it lets through.热力学系统是我们选择研究的那一部分世界：气缸中的气体、一杯咖啡、一颗恒星。其余一切构成环境。二者之间的边界称为壁，而壁的关键属性在于它允许什么通过。

1.2  Equilibrium and kinds of processes1.2 平衡与过程的类型

A system is in (thermal) equilibrium when its macroscopic properties (pressure, temperature, density, …) stay constant in time and nothing flows inside it or across its walls. Only then are quantities like "the pressure" or "the temperature" of the system well defined. A process takes the system from one equilibrium state to another, and processes come in three kinds:当系统的宏观性质（压强、温度、密度……）随时间保持不变，且其内部及跨壁均无净流动时，系统处于（热）平衡。只有此时，"系统的压强"或"系统的温度"这类量才有良好定义。过程将系统从一个平衡态带到另一个平衡态，过程分为三类：

Spontaneous自发过程
Happens by itself once a constraint is removed, e.g. gas rushing into a vacuum when a partition breaks. In between, the system is out of equilibrium, so \(P\) and \(T\) may not even be defined.约束一旦移除便自行发生，例如隔板破裂时气体冲入真空。在此过程中系统处于非平衡态，故 \(P\) 与 \(T\) 甚至可能没有定义。
Quasi-static准静态过程
Carried out so slowly that the system is essentially in equilibrium at every step. Only these can be drawn as curves on a \(P\)–\(V\) diagram.进行得足够缓慢，使系统在每一步都基本处于平衡。只有这类过程才能在 \(P\)–\(V\) 图上画成曲线。
Reversible可逆过程
Quasi-static and with no dissipation (no friction, no heat flowing across a finite temperature gap). It can be run backwards so that the system and its surroundings both return exactly to where they started. An idealisation, like a frictionless pulley.既是准静态、又无耗散（无摩擦、无热流跨越有限温差）。它可以反向进行，使系统及其环境都精确回到初始状态。这是一种理想化，如同无摩擦滑轮。
Why the distinction matters

The formula for work \(W = -\int P\,dV\) (Chapter 3) only applies to quasi-static processes, because otherwise \(P\) isn't defined. The formula for entropy \(\Delta S = \int \text{đ}Q/T\) (Chapter 4) only applies along reversible paths. Many exam questions turn on noticing that a process is not quasi-static (sudden expansion, a released piston, a weight dropped on a piston).功的公式 \(W = -\int P\,dV\)（第 3 章）仅适用于准静态过程，否则 \(P\) 没有定义。熵的公式 \(\Delta S = \int \text{đ}Q/T\)（第 4 章）仅沿可逆路径适用。许多考题的关键在于识别某过程并非准静态（突然膨胀、活塞释放、重物落在活塞上）。

1.3  State variables: extensive and intensive1.3 状态参量：广延量与强度量

State variables describe an equilibrium state. A quick test sorts them into two kinds: cut the system in half and see what happens to the variable.状态参量描述平衡态。一个快速的判据可将其分为两类：把系统一分为二，观察该参量如何变化。

A minimal set of variables that fixes the equilibrium state completely is called the set of thermodynamic coordinates. All other state variables are functions of them. The coordinates are taken to be extensive: the internal energy \(U\) plus "work coordinates" such as \(V\) (or a magnetisation \(M\) for a magnet, an area for a film).一组能够完全确定平衡态的最少变量称为热力学坐标。所有其他状态参量都是它们的函数。这些坐标取为广延量：内能 \(U\) 加上"功坐标"如 \(V\)（或磁体的磁化强度 \(M\)、薄膜的面积）。

Why not just use \(T\) and \(P\)? Take three containers of water at 0 °C and 1 atm: all ice, half ice and half water, all water. Same \(T\), same \(P\), but clearly different states with different internal energies. \(T\) and \(P\) can't tell them apart; an extensive variable like \(U\) can.

1.4  The Zeroth Law: why temperature exists1.4 第零定律：温度为何存在

The Zeroth Law

If A is in thermal equilibrium with C, and B is in thermal equilibrium with C, then A is in thermal equilibrium with B.若 A 与 C 处于热平衡，且 B 与 C 处于热平衡，则 A 与 B 处于热平衡。

It sounds obvious, but it is exactly what makes a thermometer work. C is the thermometer: if it reads the same in A and in B, we can conclude that A and B would be in equilibrium with each other without ever putting them in contact. Mathematically, the Zeroth Law guarantees that every system has a function of its own coordinates, \(\Theta\), that takes the same value for all systems in mutual equilibrium:这看似显然，却正是温度计得以工作的根本。C 即温度计：若它在 A 与 B 中读数相同，我们便可断定 A 与 B 彼此处于平衡，而无需使它们接触。数学上，第零定律保证每个系统都存在其自身坐标的函数 \(\Theta\)，且对所有相互平衡的系统取相同值：

Consequence

\[ \Theta_A(A_1, A_2, \ldots) = \Theta_B(B_1, B_2, \ldots) \quad\text{whenever A and B are in equilibrium} \]

That function is the temperature. The Zeroth Law tells us it exists; it does not yet tell us what numbers to assign. That is the job of a temperature scale.该函数即温度。第零定律告诉我们它存在，但尚未告诉我们应赋予何种数值。这是温标的工作。

Proof: from the Zeroth Law to a temperature function证明：从第零定律到温度函数

Equilibrium between A and C imposes one condition linking their coordinates, \(f_{AC}(A_1, A_2, \ldots; C_1, C_2, \ldots) = 0\); similarly \(f_{BC}(B_1, \ldots; C_1, \ldots) = 0\). Solve both for \(C_1\):A 与 C 的平衡施加一个联系二者坐标的条件 \(f_{AC}(A_1, A_2, \ldots; C_1, C_2, \ldots) = 0\)；同理 \(f_{BC}(B_1, \ldots; C_1, \ldots) = 0\)。将两式分别解出 \(C_1\)：

\[ C_1 = F_{AC}(A_1, A_2, \ldots; C_2, \ldots) = F_{BC}(B_1, B_2, \ldots; C_2, \ldots) \]

The Zeroth Law says A and B are then also in equilibrium, \(f_{AB}(A_1, \ldots; B_1, \ldots) = 0\), a condition with no C-coordinates in it. So the C-coordinates must cancel out of the equation above, leaving \(\Theta_A(A_1, A_2, \ldots) = \Theta_B(B_1, B_2, \ldots)\).第零定律指出 A 与 B 也随之平衡，即 \(f_{AB}(A_1, \ldots; B_1, \ldots) = 0\)，而该条件不含任何 C 的坐标。因此上式中 C 的坐标必须相消，留下 \(\Theta_A(A_1, A_2, \ldots) = \Theta_B(B_1, B_2, \ldots)\)。

1.5  Temperature scales1.5 温标

To put numbers on temperature, pick a thermometric property \(X\) that changes with hotness (the length of a mercury column, the pressure of a gas at fixed volume, a wire's resistance) and assume a linear rule \(\Theta = aX + b\), with \(a\), \(b\) fixed by reference points.要为温度赋予数值，需选取一个随冷热变化的测温性质 \(X\)（水银柱长度、定容气体的压强、导线的电阻），并假定线性关系 \(\Theta = aX + b\)，其中 \(a\)、\(b\) 由参考点确定。

Celsius, absolute zero, Kelvin摄氏、绝对零度、开尔文

Celsius uses two reference points, ice melting (0 °C) and water boiling at 1 atm (100 °C), and interpolates linearly between them. Now measure the volume of a dilute gas at fixed pressure at several temperatures and extrapolate the straight line to \(V = 0\). Whatever the gas and whatever the pressure, the line hits zero at \(-273.15\ \text{°C}\). Since a volume can't be negative, nothing can be colder: that is absolute zero. The Kelvin scale keeps the Celsius degree size but starts there:摄氏温标使用两个参考点：冰融化（0 °C）与 1 atm 下水沸腾（100 °C），并在其间线性插值。随后在若干温度下测量定压稀薄气体的体积，并将直线外推至 \(V = 0\)。无论何种气体、何种压强，直线都在 \(-273.15\ \text{°C}\) 处与零相交。由于体积不能为负，故不存在更低的温度：这就是绝对零度。开尔文温标保留摄氏度的度大小，但以此为起点：

Conversions

\[ T\ (\text{K}) = \theta\ (\text{°C}) + 273.15, \qquad \theta\ (\text{°C}) = \tfrac{5}{9}\,(\theta_F - 32) \]

Always use kelvin in formulas. Ratios like \(T_2/T_1\), \(\ln(T_2/T_1)\) or \(1 - T_C/T_H\) are meaningless in Celsius. Temperature differences are the same in both.

The ideal-gas temperature scale理想气体温标

Different thermometers (different substances, different amounts of gas) disagree slightly with each other. But as a gas thermometer is made more and more dilute, all gases converge on the same reading. That limit defines a universal scale, anchored at the triple point of water (273.16 K, where ice, water and vapour coexist):不同温度计（不同物质、不同气体量）彼此略有差异。但当气体温度计越来越稀薄时，所有气体都收敛到同一读数。该极限定义一个普适温标，以水的三相点（273.16 K，冰、水与蒸汽共存）为锚点：

Ideal-gas temperature (constant-volume gas thermometer)

\[ T = 273.16\ \text{K}\times\lim_{P_{\text{tr}}\to0}\frac{P}{P_{\text{tr}}} \]

\(P_{\text{tr}}\) is the gas pressure when the bulb sits at the triple point; \(P\) is the pressure at the unknown temperature. Chapter 4 shows that this scale coincides with the "thermodynamic" temperature defined by heat engines, so it is not tied to any particular substance.\(P_{\text{tr}}\) 是球泡处于三相点时的气体压强；\(P\) 是未知温度下的压强。第 4 章表明该温标与热机定义的"热力学"温度一致，故不依赖于任何特定物质。

2.1  The ideal gas law2.1 理想气体定律

An equation of state is a relation \(f(P, V, T) = 0\) among the state variables of a substance in equilibrium. Thermodynamics does not predict it; it comes from experiment or from a microscopic model. The simplest, and the limit every real gas approaches at low density, is状态方程是平衡态物质各状态参量之间的关系 \(f(P, V, T) = 0\)。热力学本身并不预测它；它来自实验或微观模型。最简单、也是所有实际气体在低密度下趋近的极限，是

Ideal gas law

\[ PV = nRT = Nk_BT, \qquad R = 8.314\ \text{J/(mol·K)}, \quad k_B = R/N_A = 1.381\times10^{-23}\ \text{J/K} \]

It is really the ideal-gas temperature scale of §1.5 rewritten: that scale is defined so that \(PV/n\) of a dilute gas is proportional to \(T\), and \(R\) is the proportionality constant. Useful forms: density \(\rho = PM/(RT)\) with \(M\) the molar mass; and \(1\ \text{atm·L} = 101.3\ \text{J}\).它实际上是 §1.5 的理想气体温标的重写：该温标的定义使稀薄气体的 \(PV/n\) 正比于 \(T\)，\(R\) 为比例常数。常用形式：密度 \(\rho = PM/(RT)\)（\(M\) 为摩尔质量）；以及 \(1\ \text{atm·L} = 101.3\ \text{J}\)。

2.2  Real gases2.2 实际气体

Real molecules have a size and attract each other, so at high density (high pressure, low temperature) the ideal gas law fails. Two standard corrections:实际分子具有体积并相互吸引，故在高密度（高压、低温）下理想气体定律失效。两项标准修正：

Van der Waals equation

\[ \left(P + \frac{an^2}{V^2}\right)(V - nb) = nRT \]

  • \(-nb\): molecules take up space. The volume available to move in is less than the container volume.\(-nb\)：分子占据空间。可供运动的体积小于容器体积。
  • \(+an^2/V^2\): molecules attract. A molecule about to hit the wall is pulled back by its neighbours, so it hits more softly and the measured \(P\) is lower than it would otherwise be. The effect grows with the square of the density.\(+an^2/V^2\)：分子相互吸引。即将撞击壁的分子被相邻分子拉回，故撞击更缓和，测得的 \(P\) 低于其本应具有的值。该效应随密度的平方增长。
Virial expansion (a systematic series in density) \[ PV = nRT\left(1 + \frac{B(T)}{V/n} + \frac{C(T)}{(V/n)^2} + \cdots\right) \]

At low density every correction vanishes and \(PV = nRT\) returns.低密度下所有修正项消失，回到 \(PV = nRT\)。

2.3  Kinetic theory: pressure from molecules2.3 分子动理论：由分子推导压强

Model the gas as many tiny hard spheres in random motion, colliding elastically with each other and the walls, with no forces between collisions and no preferred direction. Pressure is then just the momentum delivered to the walls per unit area per unit time. Working this out gives将气体建模为大量做无规则运动的小硬球，彼此及与器壁弹性碰撞，碰撞之间无作用力，亦无优先方向。于是压强就是单位时间单位面积传递给器壁的动量。计算可得

Pressure from molecular motion

\[ PV = \tfrac{1}{3}Nm\langle v^2\rangle = \tfrac{2}{3}K, \qquad K = N\cdot\tfrac12 m\langle v^2\rangle \]

The macroscopic pressure is set by the total translational kinetic energy \(K\) of the molecules.宏观压强由分子的总平动动能 \(K\) 决定。

Proof: momentum transfer to a wall证明：向器壁的动量传递

A molecule with \(x\)-velocity \(v_x\) bounces off the right wall and reverses, transferring momentum \(2mv_x\). In time \(dt\), the molecules that reach a wall of area \(A\) are those within distance \(v_x\,dt\) moving towards it: \(\tfrac12(N/V)Av_x\,dt\) of them (the \(\tfrac12\) because half move the other way). Force = momentum per time:一个 \(x\) 方向速度为 \(v_x\) 的分子从右壁弹回并反向，传递动量 \(2mv_x\)。在时间 \(dt\) 内，到达面积 \(A\) 器壁的分子是那些位于距离 \(v_x\,dt\) 以内、朝其运动的分子：共 \(\tfrac12(N/V)Av_x\,dt\) 个（\(\tfrac12\) 是因为一半朝相反方向运动）。力 = 单位时间的动量：

\[ F = \tfrac12\frac{N}{V}Av_x\cdot 2mv_x \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; P = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{N}{V}m\langle v_x^2\rangle \]

No direction is special, so \(\langle v_x^2\rangle = \tfrac13\langle v^2\rangle\), giving \(PV = \tfrac13 Nm\langle v^2\rangle\).没有哪个方向特殊，故 \(\langle v_x^2\rangle = \tfrac13\langle v^2\rangle\)，给出 \(PV = \tfrac13 Nm\langle v^2\rangle\)。

2.4  Temperature as kinetic energy: equipartition2.4 温度作为动能：能量均分

Put \(PV = \tfrac23 K\) next to \(PV = Nk_BT\): they agree only if \(K = \tfrac32 Nk_BT\). So temperature measures the average kinetic energy per molecule, \(\tfrac12m\langle v^2\rangle = \tfrac32k_BT\), shared equally among the three directions of motion. The general rule:将 \(PV = \tfrac23 K\) 与 \(PV = Nk_BT\) 并列：仅当 \(K = \tfrac32 Nk_BT\) 时二者一致。故温度衡量每个分子的平均动能 \(\tfrac12m\langle v^2\rangle = \tfrac32k_BT\)，并在三个运动方向上均分。一般规则：

Equipartition theorem

\[ U = \frac{f}{2}Nk_BT = \frac{f}{2}nRT \]

2.5  The Maxwell speed distribution2.5 麦克斯韦速率分布

Molecules don't all move at \(v_{\text{rms}}\); their speeds spread out according to分子并非都以 \(v_{\text{rms}}\) 运动；其速率按如下分布展开

Maxwell speed distribution

\[ f(v) = 4\pi\left(\frac{m}{2\pi k_BT}\right)^{3/2}v^2\,e^{-mv^2/2k_BT}, \qquad \int_0^\infty f(v)\,dv = 1 \]

\(f(v)\,dv\) is the fraction of molecules with speeds between \(v\) and \(v + dv\). Two competing factors shape it: \(v^2\) (there are more ways to have a large velocity vector — a bigger sphere in velocity space) and \(e^{-mv^2/2k_BT}\) (high energies are exponentially rare).\(f(v)\,dv\) 是速率介于 \(v\) 与 \(v + dv\) 之间的分子分数。两个相互竞争的因素塑造了它：\(v^2\)（具有大速度矢量的方式更多——速度空间中的球面更大）与 \(e^{-mv^2/2k_BT}\)（高能量呈指数稀有）。

\(v_{\text{mp}}\) is the peak of the curve; the long tail at high speed pulls the mean and the rms above it.\(v_{\text{mp}}\) 是曲线的峰值；高速处的长尾将平均值与方均根值拉至其上方。

3.1  Internal energy and the First Law3.1 内能与第一定律

Experiments show that if you take a thermally insulated system from state 1 to state 2, the work needed is always the same, whether you stir it, compress it or pass a current through it. A quantity whose change depends only on the end states is a state function; this one is the internal energy \(U\). If heat can also flow, the same change of state can be produced by any mix of work and heat:实验表明，若将绝热系统从状态 1 带到状态 2，无论采用搅拌、压缩还是通电，所需功总是相同。变化只依赖于初末状态的量称为状态函数；此处即为内能 \(U\)。若热也可流动，同一状态变化可由功与热的任意组合实现：

First Law of Thermodynamics

\[ \Delta U = W + Q, \qquad dU = \text{đ}W + \text{đ}Q \]

\(W\) = work done on the system, \(Q\) = heat put into the system.\(W\) = 对系统做的功，\(Q\) = 输入系统的热。

Sign conventions. This course uses "work done on the system". Some textbooks use work done by the system and write \(\Delta U = Q - W\). Before plugging into any formula, decide whose work it is. In engines we usually quote the work the engine delivers, which is \(-W\).

\(U\) is a state function; \(W\) and \(Q\) are not. The same \(\Delta U\) can be reached with lots of work and little heat or the other way round, which is why small amounts of them are written \(\text{đ}W\), \(\text{đ}Q\).\(U\) 是状态函数；\(W\) 与 \(Q\) 不是。同一 \(\Delta U\) 可由大量功与少量热实现，也可反之，因此它们的微小量写作 \(\text{đ}W\)、\(\text{đ}Q\)。

3.2  Work done on a gas3.2 对气体做的功

Push a piston of area \(A\) in by \(dx\) against a gas at pressure \(P\): the work done on the gas is \(PA\,dx = -P\,dV\) (volume decreases, \(dV \lt 0\), work done on the gas is positive).将面积 \(A\) 的活塞向内推 \(dx\)，对抗压强为 \(P\) 的气体：对气体做的功为 \(PA\,dx = -P\,dV\)（体积减小，\(dV \lt 0\)，对气体做的功为正）。

Quasi-static work

\[ \text{đ}W = -P\,dV, \qquad W = -\int_{V_1}^{V_2}P\,dV \]

On a \(P\)–\(V\) diagram, \(|W|\) is the area under the path. Different paths between the same two states enclose different areas, which is another way of seeing that \(W\) depends on the path.在 \(P\)–\(V\) 图上，\(|W|\) 是路径下方的面积。相同两状态之间的不同路径围出不同面积，这是 \(W\) 依赖于路径的另一种体现。

Sudden processes. If the gas is not in equilibrium (a weight dropped onto a piston, a piston released), its own pressure isn't defined during the process and \(-\int P\,dV\) can't be used. But if the external pressure is constant, the work done on the gas is simply突变过程。若气体不处于平衡（重物落在活塞上、活塞被释放），过程中气体自身的压强没有定义，\(-\int P\,dV\) 不可用。但若外压恒定，对气体做的功简为

\[ W = -P_{\text{ext}}\,\Delta V \]

3.7  Heat engines and the Carnot cycle3.7 热机与卡诺循环

A heat engine runs in a cycle: it absorbs heat \(Q_H\) from a hot reservoir, delivers work \(W\), and dumps heat \(Q_C\) into a cold reservoir. Over a full cycle the working gas returns to its starting state, so \(\Delta U = 0\) and \(W = Q_H - Q_C\) (all quantities as magnitudes). A refrigerator runs the same cycle backwards, using work to pump heat from cold to hot.热机循环运行：从高温热库吸收热 \(Q_H\)，输出功 \(W\)，并向低温热库排出热 \(Q_C\)。整个循环后工作气体回到初始态，故 \(\Delta U = 0\)、\(W = Q_H - Q_C\)（各量取大小）。制冷机则反向运行同一循环，用功将热从冷处泵至热处。

Figures of merit

\[ \eta = \frac{W}{Q_H} = 1 - \frac{Q_C}{Q_H} \quad(\text{engine}), \qquad \text{COP} = \frac{Q_C}{W} = \frac{Q_C}{Q_H - Q_C} \quad(\text{refrigerator}) \]

The Carnot cycle is the ideal reversible engine: two isotherms joined by two adiabats.卡诺循环是理想的可逆热机：两条等温线由两条绝热线连接。

\[ \eta_{\text{Carnot}} = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}, \qquad \frac{Q_C}{Q_H} = \frac{T_C}{T_H}, \qquad \text{COP}_{\text{Carnot}} = \frac{T_C}{T_H - T_C} \]

Chapter 4

Entropy and the Second Law熵与第二定律

What this chapter does

The First Law would allow a hot cup of tea to get hotter by drawing heat from a cold room, as long as energy is conserved. That never happens. The Second Law captures which way things actually go, and this chapter turns it into a number, the entropy \(S\): in any process that happens by itself, the total entropy goes up. 4.1  Reversible processes and reservoirs4.1 可逆过程与热库

Two idealisations make the theory work, in the way frictionless planes make mechanics work:两个理想化使理论得以运作，正如无摩擦斜面之于力学：

  • A reversible process is quasi-static with no dissipation (§1.2). Run it backwards and both the system and the surroundings return to exactly where they were. Real processes only approximate this.可逆过程是准静态且无耗散的（§1.2）。反向运行它，系统与环境都精确回到原处。真实过程只能近似如此。
  • A heat reservoir has such a large heat capacity that taking heat from it or adding heat to it doesn't change its temperature (the atmosphere, a lake, a big water bath). Heat exchange is always reversible from the reservoir's point of view.热库具有足够大的热容，从中取热或向其加热都不改变其温度（大气、湖泊、大浴槽）。从热库的角度看，热交换总是可逆的。

4.2  The Second Law4.2 第二定律

Two everyday observations, stated carefully, turn out to be the same law:两个日常观察，仔细表述后，实为同一条定律：

Kelvin statement

No process can have as its only effect the complete conversion of heat into work.没有任何过程可以以其唯一效果是将热完全转化为功。

Clausius statement

Heat cannot flow from a colder body to a hotter one with no other effect.热不可能在没有其他影响的情况下从较冷物体流向较热物体。

The words "only" and "no other effect" matter. An ideal gas expanding isothermally converts heat fully into work, but its volume has changed (another effect), so it can't be repeated in a cycle. A refrigerator moves heat from cold to hot, but it consumes work. What is forbidden is a cyclic machine that turns heat from a single reservoir entirely into work, or heat flowing uphill by itself."唯一"与"没有其他影响"至关重要。理想气体等温膨胀将热完全转化为功，但其体积改变了（另一种影响），故不能在循环中重复。制冷机将热从冷处搬到热处，但消耗功。被禁止的是仅利用单一热库的热、在循环中将其完全转化为功的机器，或热自行上坡流动。

Proof: the two statements are equivalent证明：两种表述等价

Violate Clausius ⇒ violate Kelvin. If heat could flow from cold to hot by itself, run a Carnot engine between the reservoirs and send the heat it dumps into the cold reservoir straight back up to the hot one. Net effect: heat taken from the hot reservoir and turned entirely into work. Kelvin is violated.违反克劳修斯 ⇒ 违反开尔文。若热可自行从冷流向热，则让一台卡诺热机在两热库之间工作，并将其排入低温库的热直接送回高温库。净效果：从高温库取热并完全转化为功。开尔文表述被违反。

Violate Kelvin ⇒ violate Clausius. If a perfect engine could turn heat fully into work, use that work to run a refrigerator. Net effect: heat moved from cold to hot with nothing else changed. Clausius is violated.违反开尔文 ⇒ 违反克劳修斯。若一台完美热机可将热完全转化为功，则用该功运行制冷机。净效果：热从冷处搬到热处，其他一切不变。克劳修斯表述被违反。

4.3  Carnot's theorem and absolute temperature4.3 卡诺定理与绝对温度

Carnot's theorem

No engine working between two reservoirs is more efficient than a reversible one, and all reversible engines between the same two temperatures have the same efficiency:在两个热库之间工作的任何热机，其效率都不高于可逆热机；且同一两个温度之间的所有可逆热机效率相同：

\[ \eta \le \eta_{\text{rev}} = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H} \]

This is the ultimate speed limit for turning heat into work, and it doesn't depend on the working substance (gas, steam, rubber band) or the details of the engine. Because it is universal, it can be used to define temperature without reference to any material: the thermodynamic temperature is defined by \(Q_C/Q_H = T_C/T_H\) for a reversible engine. It agrees with the ideal-gas scale of §1.5 (both give the Carnot result).这是将热转化为功的终极速度极限，且不依赖于工作物质（气体、蒸汽、橡皮筋）或热机的细节。由于它是普适的，可用于在不参考任何材料的情况下定义温度：热力学温度由可逆热机的 \(Q_C/Q_H = T_C/T_H\) 定义。它与 §1.5 的理想气体温标一致（两者均给出卡诺结果）。

Proof sketch证明梗概

Suppose an engine X beat a reversible engine R between the same reservoirs. Run R backwards as a refrigerator, driven by part of X's work, and let it put back into the hot reservoir the heat X took out. Because X is more efficient, there is work left over, and the only net effect is heat taken from the cold reservoir and turned into work, or heat moved from cold to hot. That contradicts the Second Law. Applying this argument both ways to two reversible engines shows their efficiencies must be equal.假设热机 X 在同一对热库之间胜过可逆热机 R。让 R 反向作为制冷机运行，由 X 的一部分功驱动，并让它把 X 取出的热送回高温库。由于 X 效率更高，会剩下功，而唯一的净效果是从低温库取热并转化为功，或把热从冷处搬到热处。这违反第二定律。对两台可逆热机双向应用该论证，表明它们的效率必须相等。

For the temperature scale: if \(Q_C/Q_H = f(\theta_C, \theta_H)\) for every reversible engine, chaining two engines through an intermediate reservoir forces \(f(\theta_C, \theta_H) = g(\theta_C)/g(\theta_H)\). Choosing \(g(\theta) = \theta\) defines the absolute scale.关于温标：若对每台可逆热机 \(Q_C/Q_H = f(\theta_C, \theta_H)\)，通过一个中间热库串联两台热机可推出 \(f(\theta_C, \theta_H) = g(\theta_C)/g(\theta_H)\)。选择 \(g(\theta) = \theta\) 即定义绝对温标。

4.4  Entropy4.4 熵

For a Carnot cycle, \(Q_H/T_H = Q_C/T_C\); with signs (heat absorbed positive), \(Q_H/T_H + Q_C/T_C = 0\). This generalises to any reversible cycle broken into many small heat exchanges: \(\oint\text{đ}Q_{\text{rev}}/T = 0\). An integral that vanishes around every closed loop doesn't depend on the path between two points, so it defines a state function:对卡诺循环，\(Q_H/T_H = Q_C/T_C\)；带符号（吸热为正），\(Q_H/T_H + Q_C/T_C = 0\)。这可推广到分解为许多小热交换的任意可逆循环：\(\oint\text{đ}Q_{\text{rev}}/T = 0\)。绕任意闭合回路积分为零的积分不依赖于两点之间的路径，故它定义一个状态函数：

Definition of entropy

\[ \Delta S = S_B - S_A = \int_A^B\frac{\text{đ}Q_{\text{rev}}}{T} \]

Two things to hold on to:需牢记两点：

  1. The integral must be taken along a reversible path. For a real (irreversible) process, \(\int\text{đ}Q/T\) is not \(\Delta S\).积分必须沿可逆路径进行。对真实（不可逆）过程，\(\int\text{đ}Q/T\) 不等于 \(\Delta S\)。
  2. But \(\Delta S\) itself only depends on the end states. So for an irreversible process, invent any convenient reversible path between the same start and end, and integrate along that. This is the single most important trick in the chapter.但 \(\Delta S\) 本身只依赖于初末状态。故对不可逆过程，在相同的初末之间构造任意方便的可逆路径，并沿其积分。这是本章最重要的单一技巧。
Proof: Clausius's theorem \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)证明：克劳修斯定理 \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)

Let a system go round any cycle, exchanging heat \(\text{đ}Q\) at temperatures \(T\). Supply each bit of heat with a small Carnot engine running from a single big reservoir at \(T_0\); each engine draws \(T_0\,\text{đ}Q/T\) from that reservoir. After one cycle the system and all the small engines are back to their start, and the net heat drawn from the single reservoir, \(T_0\oint\text{đ}Q/T\), has become work. Kelvin forbids this from being positive, so \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\), with equality for a reversible cycle (run it backwards and the inequality flips).让系统绕任意循环，在温度 \(T\) 下交换热 \(\text{đ}Q\)。为每一小份热配一台小卡诺热机，它们均从温度为 \(T_0\) 的单一热库运行；每台热机从该热库取 \(T_0\,\text{đ}Q/T\)。一个循环后，系统与所有小热机均回到起点，而从单一热库取出的净热 \(T_0\oint\text{đ}Q/T\) 已变为功。开尔文禁止其为正，故 \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)，对可逆循环取等号（反向运行则不等号翻转）。

4.5  The entropy principle4.5 熵原理

Compare a real process with a reversible one between the same states and Clausius's theorem gives将真实过程与相同状态之间的可逆过程比较，克劳修斯定理给出

Clausius inequality and the entropy principle

\[ dS \ge \frac{\text{đ}Q}{T} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \Delta S_{\text{isolated}} \ge 0, \qquad \Delta S_{\text{univ}} = \Delta S_{\text{sys}} + \Delta S_{\text{surr}} \ge 0 \]

Equality holds only for reversible processes. In words: the entropy of an isolated system never decreases; treating "system + surroundings" as one isolated whole, the entropy of the universe never decreases. This gives a mechanical test:仅对可逆过程取等号。换言之：孤立系统的熵永不减少；将"系统 + 环境"视为一个孤立整体，宇宙的熵永不减少。这给出一个机械判据：

Reversibility test: compute \(\Delta S_{\text{sys}}\) and \(\Delta S_{\text{surr}}\) separately and add them. \(\Delta S_{\text{univ}} = 0\) ⇒ reversible; \(\gt 0\) ⇒ irreversible; \(\lt 0\) ⇒ impossible (check your signs).
Proof证明

Go from A to B by an irreversible path I, and come back by a reversible path II. For the cycle, \(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T + \int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\). The return leg is reversible, so \(\int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T = S_A - S_B\). Hence \(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T \le S_B - S_A\). For an isolated system \(\text{đ}Q = 0\), so \(S_B \ge S_A\).沿不可逆路径 I 从 A 到 B，再沿可逆路径 II 返回。对循环，\(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T + \int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)。返回段可逆，故 \(\int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T = S_A - S_B\)。因此 \(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T \le S_B - S_A\)。对孤立系统 \(\text{đ}Q = 0\)，故 \(S_B \ge S_A\)。

4.8  What entropy measures: \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\)4.8 熵衡量什么：\(S = k_B\ln\Omega\)

Flip 100 coins. A microstate is the exact sequence (there are \(2^{100}\)); a macrostate is what you'd actually report, say "\(n\) heads". The number of microstates in a macrostate is the multiplicity \(\Omega(N,n) = \binom{N}{n}\). "All heads" has \(\Omega = 1\); "50 heads" has \(\Omega \approx 10^{29}\). If the coins are shaken randomly, you will essentially always see something near 50, simply because overwhelmingly more microstates look like that.抛 100 枚硬币。微观态是精确的序列（共有 \(2^{100}\) 种）；宏观态是你实际会报告的，比如"\(n\) 个正面"。一个宏观态中微观态的数目为多重度 \(\Omega(N,n) = \binom{N}{n}\)。"全正面"有 \(\Omega = 1\) 种；"50 个正面"有 \(\Omega \approx 10^{29}\) 种。若随机摇动硬币，你几乎总会看到接近 50 的结果，仅因为看起来像那样的微观态多得多。

Equilibrium is the macrostate with the largest \(\Omega\). Where Sackur–Tetrode comes from (sketch)萨克–特策罗德公式的来源（概述）

5.1  Natural variables: what a differential tells you5.1 自然变量：微分所提供的信息

Look at the structure of \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\): each term on the right is "some quantity × d(another quantity)". The quantities after the \(d\), here \(S\) and \(V\), are the natural variables of \(U\). Treat \(U\) as \(U(S,V)\), compare with the total differential \(dU = (\partial U/\partial S)_V dS + (\partial U/\partial V)_S dV\), and read off:观察 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\) 的结构：右边每一项都是"某个量 × d(另一个量)"。\(d\) 后面的量——此处为 \(S\) 与 \(V\)——即 \(U\) 的自然变量。将 \(U\) 视为 \(U(S,V)\)，与全微分 \(dU = (\partial U/\partial S)_V dS + (\partial U/\partial V)_S dV\) 比较，读出：

Reading \(T\) and \(P\) off \(U(S,V)\)

\[ T = \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial S}\right)_{\!V}, \qquad P = -\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{\!S} \]

This says something important: if you know \(U\) as a function of its natural variables \(S\) and \(V\), you can find the temperature, the pressure and hence the equation of state. All the thermodynamics of the system is contained in that one function. That is where the word "potential" comes from: just as differentiating a potential energy gives a force, differentiating a thermodynamic potential gives \(T\), \(P\) and so on.这说明了重要的一点：若知道 \(U\) 作为其自然变量 \(S\) 与 \(V\) 的函数，即可求出温度、压强，从而得到状态方程。系统的全部热力学都包含在这一个函数中。"势"一词由此而来：正如对势能求导得合力，对热力学势求导得到 \(T\)、\(P\) 等。

Show solution显示解答

\[ T = \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial S}\right)_{\!V} = \frac{2}{3Nk_B}\,U \quad\Longrightarrow\quad U = \tfrac{3}{2}Nk_BT \]

\[ P = -\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{\!S} = \frac{2}{3}\,\frac{U}{V} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad PV = \tfrac{2}{3}U = Nk_BT \]

5.2  Changing variables: enthalpy, Helmholtz and Gibbs free energies5.2 变量变换：焓、亥姆霍兹与吉布斯自由能

To swap the natural variable \(S\) for \(T\), there is a standard trick using the product rule \(d(TS) = T\,dS + S\,dT\). Subtract \(d(TS)\) from \(dU\):要将自然变量 \(S\) 换成 \(T\)，有一个使用乘积法则 \(d(TS) = T\,dS + S\,dT\) 的标准技巧。从 \(dU\) 中减去 \(d(TS)\)：

\[ d(U - TS) = T\,dS - P\,dV - T\,dS - S\,dT = -S\,dT - P\,dV \]

The \(T\,dS\) term cancels and is replaced by \(-S\,dT\). The new function \(U - TS\) has natural variables \(T\) and \(V\). In the same way, adding \(d(PV)\) swaps \(V\) for \(P\). This gives four functions in total (the operation is called a Legendre transformation):\(T\,dS\) 项相消并被 \(-S\,dT\) 取代。新函数 \(U - TS\) 的自然变量为 \(T\) 与 \(V\)。同样地，加上 \(d(PV)\) 将 \(V\) 换成 \(P\)。这样共得到四个函数（该运算称为勒让德变换）：

The four thermodynamic potentials

\[ \begin{aligned} &U &&dU = T\,dS - P\,dV &&\text{natural variables } S, V \\ &H = U + PV &&dH = T\,dS + V\,dP &&\text{natural variables } S, P \\ &F = U - TS &&dF = -S\,dT - P\,dV &&\text{natural variables } T, V \\ &G = U - TS + PV &&dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP &&\text{natural variables } T, P \end{aligned} \]

No need to memorise four differentials, just the pattern: adding \(PV\) turns \(-P\,dV\) into \(+V\,dP\); subtracting \(TS\) turns \(T\,dS\) into \(-S\,dT\).无需记住四个微分，只需记住模式：加上 \(PV\) 将 \(-P\,dV\) 变为 \(+V\,dP\)；减去 \(TS\) 将 \(T\,dS\) 变为 \(-S\,dT\)。

Reading off coefficients works for every potential:读出系数适用于每个势：

\[ \begin{aligned} &T = \left(\frac{\partial H}{\partial S}\right)_P, &&V = \left(\frac{\partial H}{\partial P}\right)_S \\ &S = -\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial T}\right)_V, &&P = -\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial V}\right)_T \\ &S = -\left(\frac{\partial G}{\partial T}\right)_P, &&V = \left(\frac{\partial G}{\partial P}\right)_T \end{aligned} \]

The last line gets used constantly in Chapters 7 and 8: the \(T\)-derivative of \(G\) is \(-S\), the \(P\)-derivative is \(V\).最后一行在第 7、8 章中频繁使用：\(G\) 对 \(T\) 的导数为 \(-S\)，对 \(P\) 的导数为 \(V\)。

  • Each potential's natural variables are the two corners next to it (\(F\): \(V\) and \(T\)).每个势的自然变量是紧邻它的两个角（\(F\)：\(V\) 与 \(T\)）。
  • In its differential, each natural variable is multiplied by the corner diagonally opposite.在其微分中，每个自然变量乘以对角相对的角。

If this feels fiddly, the "add \(PV\) / subtract \(TS\)" rule above is more direct.以 \(G\) 验证：自然变量 \(T, P\)。对角 \(T\) 为 \(S\)，箭头指向 \(T\)，给出 \(-S\,dT\)。对角 \(P\) 为 \(V\)，箭头背离 \(P\)，给出 \(+V\,dP\)。若觉得繁琐，上述"加 \(PV\) / 减 \(TS\)"规则更直接。

5.3  What the new functions mean physically5.3 新函数的物理含义

\(H\), \(F\) and \(G\) are not just a change of variables; each one is the useful quantity in a particular experimental situation.\(H\)、\(F\) 与 \(G\) 不仅是变量变换；每一个都是特定实验情境中有用的量。

Enthalpy \(H\): the heat absorbed at constant pressure焓 \(H\)：恒压下吸收的热

At constant pressure \(dP = 0\), so \(dH = T\,dS = \text{đ}Q\):在恒压 \(dP = 0\) 下，故 \(dH = T\,dS = \text{đ}Q\)：

Heat at constant pressure

\[ Q_P = \Delta H \]

Chemical reactions, melting and boiling almost always happen at atmospheric pressure, so the "heat of reaction" or "latent heat" you find in tables is really a \(\Delta H\).化学反应、融化与沸腾几乎总在大气压下发生，故表中查到的"反应热"或"潜热"实际上是 \(\Delta H\)。

Where it's used later

The latent heat \(L = \Delta H = T\Delta S\) in Chapter 7; the reaction heat \(\Delta h_0\) in the van 't Hoff equation of Chapter 8; and throttling (Joule–Thomson, Chapter 3) is a constant-\(H\) process.第 7 章的潜热 \(L = \Delta H = T\Delta S\)；第 8 章范特霍夫方程中的反应热 \(\Delta h_0\)；节流（焦耳–汤姆孙，第 3 章）为恒 \(H\) 过程。

Helmholtz free energy \(F\): the maximum work at constant temperature亥姆霍兹自由能 \(F\)：恒温下的最大功

For a system in contact with a reservoir at temperature \(T\), the work it can deliver is at most \(-\Delta F\) (proved in Chapter 6). In a reversible isothermal process, the work done on the system equals \(\Delta F\) exactly:对与温度为 \(T\) 的热库接触的系统，其能输出的功至多为 \(-\Delta F\)（第 6 章证明）。在可逆等温过程中，对系统做的功恰等于 \(\Delta F\)：

Reversible isothermal process

\[ W_{\text{on}} = \Delta F, \qquad W_{\text{by, max}} = -\Delta F \]

Why not \(-\Delta U\)? Because during an isothermal process the system also exchanges heat \(T\Delta S\) with the reservoir. Only the part \(F = U - TS\) is "free" to become work; the rest, \(TS\), can only be exchanged as heat.为何不是 \(-\Delta U\)？因为在等温过程中系统还与热库交换热 \(T\Delta S\)。只有 \(F = U - TS\) 这部分是"自由"的、可转化为功的；其余 \(TS\) 只能以热的形式交换。

Example · Isothermal expansion of an ideal gas

\(n\) mol of ideal gas expands reversibly and isothermally at temperature \(T\) from \(V_1\) to \(V_2\). Find \(\Delta F\) and compare it with the work done by the gas.\(n\) mol 理想气体在温度 \(T\) 下从 \(V_1\) 可逆等温膨胀到 \(V_2\)。求 \(\Delta F\)，并与气体做的功比较。

Show solution显示解答

For an ideal gas \(U\) depends only on \(T\), so \(\Delta U = 0\); the entropy change is \(\Delta S = nR\ln(V_2/V_1)\). Hence对理想气体 \(U\) 只依赖于 \(T\)，故 \(\Delta U = 0\)；熵变为 \(\Delta S = nR\ln(V_2/V_1)\)。因此

\[ \Delta F = \Delta U - T\Delta S = -nRT\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1} \]

The work done by the gas is \(\int P\,dV = nRT\ln(V_2/V_1) = -\Delta F\). They match: every joule of work came from heat drawn from the reservoir. The gas's \(U\) is unchanged, but its \(F\) went down.气体做的功为 \(\int P\,dV = nRT\ln(V_2/V_1) = -\Delta F\)。二者相等：每一焦耳的功都来自从热库吸收的热。气体的 \(U\) 不变，但其 \(F\) 下降。

Gibbs free energy \(G\): which way a process goes at constant \(T\) and \(P\)吉布斯自由能 \(G\)：在恒定 \(T\) 与 \(P\) 下过程的方向

Most real processes (reactions, phase changes, dissolving) take place at constant temperature and pressure. Chapter 6 shows that under these conditions a process can only go in the direction that lowers \(G\), and equilibrium is the minimum of \(G\). That makes \(G\) the central quantity of Chapters 6–8.大多数真实过程（反应、相变、溶解）在恒温恒压下进行。第 6 章表明在这些条件下过程只能朝降低 \(G\) 的方向进行，平衡是 \(G\) 的极小值。这使 \(G\) 成为第 6–8 章的核心量。

Why it exists

Questions like "does ice melt at −5 °C?" or "does this reaction run by itself?" are answered by the sign of \(\Delta G\). The form \(G = H - TS\) shows the competition: lowering \(H\) (releasing heat) favours one direction, raising \(S\) (more disorder) may favour the other, and the temperature \(T\) sets which one wins.如"冰在 −5 °C 会融化吗？"或"该反应会自发进行吗？"之类的问题，由 \(\Delta G\) 的符号回答。形式 \(G = H - TS\) 展示了竞争：降低 \(H\)（放热）有利于一个方向，升高 \(S\)（更无序）可能有利于另一个方向，而温度 \(T\) 决定哪一方胜出。

An isolated system at fixed \(U\) has maximum \(S\). 5.4  Maxwell relations: trading entropy for measurable quantities5.4 麦克斯韦关系：以可测量替换熵

The problem: many questions ask for an entropy change, e.g. "how much does the entropy of a copper block change when it is compressed at constant temperature?" That needs \((\partial S/\partial P)_T\), but there is no "entropy meter". The Maxwell relations replace such entropy derivatives by derivatives of \(P, V, T\), which come from the equation of state or from tabulated data.问题在于：许多问题问的是熵变，例如"铜块在恒温下被压缩时熵变多少？"这需要 \((\partial S/\partial P)_T\)，但不存在"熵表"。麦克斯韦关系将这类熵的导数替换为 \(P, V, T\) 的导数，后者来自状态方程或表格数据。

Where they come from is simple. Each potential is a state function, so its mixed second derivative does not depend on the order of differentiation (the toolkit in §2.6). Take \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\): the coefficients are \(-S\) and \(V\), and cross-differentiating gives其来源很简单。每个势都是状态函数，故其混合二阶导数不依赖于求导顺序（§2.6 的工具箱）。取 \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\)：系数为 \(-S\) 与 \(V\)，交叉求导得

\[ \left.\frac{\partial(-S)}{\partial P}\right|_T = \left.\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right|_P \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} = -\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} \]

Doing this once for each potential:对每个势做一次：

Maxwell relations

\[ \begin{aligned} \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial V}\right)_{\!S} &= -\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial S}\right)_{\!V} &&(U) \\ \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_{\!S} &= \left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial S}\right)_{\!P} &&(H) \\ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T} &= \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V} &&(F) \\ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} &= -\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} &&(G) \end{aligned} \]

The ones you will actually use are the last two (from \(F\) and \(G\)): their left sides ask "how does entropy change with \(V\) or \(P\) at constant \(T\)?", and their right sides come straight from the equation of state. You don't need to memorise them: write the relevant differential, cross-differentiate, and the signs take care of themselves.实际使用最多的是最后两个（来自 \(F\) 与 \(G\)）：其左边问"恒定 \(T\) 下熵如何随 \(V\) 或 \(P\) 变化？"，右边直接来自状态方程。无需记忆：写出相应的微分，交叉求导，符号自会正确。

Example 1 · Sanity check: isothermal expansion of an ideal gas

Show solution显示解答

At constant \(T\), \(dS = (\partial S/\partial V)_T\,dV\). \[ \Delta S = \int_{V_1}^{V_2}\frac{nR}{V}\,dV = nR\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1} \]

Same answer as Chapter 4's \(\int\text{đ}Q/T\), but without ever asking how much heat flowed.与第 4 章的 \(\int\text{đ}Q/T\) 答案相同，但完全无需询问流过了多少热。

Example 2 · Compressing a litre of water

5.5  The relation between \(C_P\) and \(C_V\)5.5 \(C_P\) 与 \(C_V\) 的关系

For solids and liquids, heating at constant volume is practically impossible: the material wants to expand, and holding its volume fixed takes enormous pressure. So experiments measure \(C_P\). When theory needs \(C_V\), it is calculated from \(C_P\), \(\alpha\) and \(\kappa_T\) using:对固体与液体，恒容加热实际上不可能：材料想要膨胀，保持其体积固定需要巨大压强。故实验测量的是 \(C_P\)。当理论需要 \(C_V\) 时，由 \(C_P\)、\(\alpha\) 与 \(\kappa_T\) 计算：

\(C_P - C_V\)

\[ C_P - C_V = \frac{TV\alpha^2}{\kappa_T} \]

  • Every factor on the right is \(\geq 0\) (\(\kappa_T \gt 0\) is the stability condition of Chapter 6), so always \(C_P \geq C_V\): at constant pressure some of the heat goes into expansion work.右边的每个因子都是 \(\geq 0\)（\(\kappa_T \gt 0\) 是第 6 章的稳定性条件），故总有 \(C_P \geq C_V\)：恒压下部分热用于膨胀功。
  • The two are equal when \(\alpha = 0\), for example water at 4 °C.当 \(\alpha = 0\) 时二者相等，例如 4 °C 的水。
  • Check: for an ideal gas \(\alpha = 1/T\) and \(\kappa_T = 1/P\), giving \(TV\cdot\frac{1}{T^2}\cdot P = \frac{PV}{T} = nR\), the familiar \(C_P - C_V = nR\).验证：对理想气体 \(\alpha = 1/T\) 与 \(\kappa_T = 1/P\)，给出 \(TV\cdot\frac{1}{T^2}\cdot P = \frac{PV}{T} = nR\)，即熟悉的 \(C_P - C_V = nR\)。
Proof证明

Treat \(S\) as \(S(T,V)\): \(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V\,dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T\,dV\). Divide by \(dT\) at constant \(P\):将 \(S\) 视为 \(S(T,V)\)：\(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V\,dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T\,dV\)。在恒定 \(P\) 下除以 \(dT\)：

\[ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} = \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V} + \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} \]

Multiply by \(T\): \(C_P = C_V + T(\partial S/\partial V)_T(\partial V/\partial T)_P\). Use the Maxwell relation \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\), then the cyclic relation \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P/(\partial V/\partial P)_T = \alpha/\kappa_T\), and \((\partial V/\partial T)_P = V\alpha\):乘以 \(T\)：\(C_P = C_V + T(\partial S/\partial V)_T(\partial V/\partial T)_P\)。使用麦克斯韦关系 \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\)，再用循环关系 \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P/(\partial V/\partial P)_T = \alpha/\kappa_T\)，以及 \((\partial V/\partial T)_P = V\alpha\)：

\[ C_P - C_V = T\cdot\frac{\alpha}{\kappa_T}\cdot V\alpha = \frac{TV\alpha^2}{\kappa_T} \]

Example · \(C_P - C_V\) for water

At 20 °C water has \(\alpha = 2.07\times10^{-4}\ \text{K}^{-1}\), \(\kappa_T = 4.6\times10^{-10}\ \text{Pa}^{-1}\), molar volume \(V_m = 1.8\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^3\) and \(C_P = 75.3\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\). \[ C_P - C_V = \frac{(293)(1.8\times10^{-5})(2.07\times10^{-4})^2}{4.6\times10^{-10}} \approx 0.49\ \text{J/(mol·K)} \]

So \(C_V \approx 74.8\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\). 5.6  Maxwell relations at work5.6 麦克斯韦关系的应用

The following results appear in homework and in later chapters. Each uses the same three steps: write the derivative you want → use a Maxwell relation to eliminate \(\partial S\) → substitute the equation of state.以下结果出现在作业与后续章节中。每一个都使用相同的三步：写出所需的导数 → 用麦克斯韦关系消去 \(\partial S\) → 代入状态方程。

(a) Entropy change of any substance(a) 任何物质的熵变

Two general entropy differentials

\[ dS = \frac{C_V}{T}\,dT + \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V}dV, \qquad dS = \frac{C_P}{T}\,dT - \left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P}dP \]

Given an equation of state and a heat capacity, you can now find the entropy change of any substance between any two states: integrate along a convenient path (change \(T\) at fixed \(V\) or \(P\), then change \(V\) or \(P\) at fixed \(T\)). For an ideal gas this reproduces Chapter 4's \(\Delta S = nC_V\ln(T_2/T_1) + nR\ln(V_2/V_1)\).给定状态方程与热容，现在可以求任何物质在任意两个状态之间的熵变：沿一条方便的路径积分（在固定 \(V\) 或 \(P\) 下改变 \(T\)，然后在固定 \(T\) 下改变 \(V\) 或 \(P\)）。对理想气体，这重现第 4 章的 \(\Delta S = nC_V\ln(T_2/T_1) + nR\ln(V_2/V_1)\)。

Proof证明

The total differential of \(S(T,V)\) is \(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T dV\). The first coefficient is \(C_V/T\) by definition of heat capacity; the second becomes \((\partial P/\partial T)_V\) by the \(F\) relation. The second formula is the same argument with \(S(T,P)\) and the \(G\) relation.\(S(T,V)\) 的全微分为 \(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T dV\)。第一个系数由热容的定义为 \(C_V/T\)；第二个由 \(F\) 关系变为 \((\partial P/\partial T)_V\)。第二个公式是同样的论证，使用 \(S(T,P)\) 与 \(G\) 关系。

(b) How internal energy depends on volume(b) 内能如何依赖于体积

Energy equation

\[ \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T} = T\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V} - P \]

This settles a question left open in Chapter 3. For an ideal gas, \(P = nRT/V\) gives \(T\cdot nR/V - P = 0\). So the ideal gas's \(U\) depends only on \(T\): it is a consequence, not an extra assumption.这解决了第 3 章遗留的问题。对理想气体，\(P = nRT/V\) 给出 \(T\cdot nR/V - P = 0\)。故理想气体的 \(U\) 只依赖于 \(T\)：这是推论，而非额外假设。

Proof证明

Divide \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\) by \(dV\) at constant \(T\): \((\partial U/\partial V)_T = T(\partial S/\partial V)_T - P\). Then use \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\).在恒定 \(T\) 下将 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\) 除以 \(dV\)：\((\partial U/\partial V)_T = T(\partial S/\partial V)_T - P\)。然后使用 \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\)。

Example · A van der Waals gas cools in free expansion

\((\partial P/\partial T)_V = nR/(V - nb)\), so \((\partial U/\partial V)_T = \frac{nRT}{V-nb} - P = \frac{an^2}{V^2}\). \[ \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial H}\right)_{\!S} = \frac{aV}{C_H\,T}\,H \gt 0 \]

5.7  When particle number changes: the chemical potential5.7 粒子数变化时：化学势

So far the particle number \(N\) has been fixed. But in a phase change (water molecules leaving the liquid for the vapour), a chemical reaction, or dissolving, the number of particles in each part changes. The fundamental relation then needs one more term:到目前为止粒子数 \(N\) 固定。但在相变（水分子离开液体进入蒸汽）、化学反应或溶解中，各部分的粒子数会变化。此时基本关系式需要增加一项：

Fundamental relation for open systems

\[ dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN, \qquad dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP + \mu\,dN \]

\(\mu\) is the chemical potential. Reading off coefficients:\(\mu\) 是化学势。读出系数：

\[ \mu = \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial N}\right)_{\!S,V} = \left(\frac{\partial G}{\partial N}\right)_{\!T,P} \]

In words: how much \(G\) increases when one particle is added at fixed \(T\) and \(P\).换言之：在固定 \(T\) 与 \(P\) 下加入一个粒子时 \(G\) 增加多少。

Why it exists · where it's used later

\(T\) decides which way heat flows (hot to cold). \(P\) decides which side expands (high to low pressure). \(\mu\) decides which way particles move: from high \(\mu\) to low \(\mu\) (proved in Chapter 6). So:\(T\) 决定热流方向（热→冷）。\(P\) 决定哪一侧膨胀（高压→低压）。\(\mu\) 决定粒子运动方向：从高 \(\mu\) 到低 \(\mu\)（第 6 章证明）。因此：

  • Chapter 7: two phases coexist ⟺ their chemical potentials are equal. Phase diagrams and the Clausius–Clapeyron equation follow from this single statement.第 7 章：两相共存 ⟺ 其化学势相等。相图与克劳修斯–克拉珀龙方程均由这一陈述推出。
  • Chapter 8: chemical equilibrium, solutions and mixtures are all "set the \(\mu\)'s equal".第 8 章：化学平衡、溶液与混合物均为"令 \(\mu\) 相等"。

\(G = \mu N\): the chemical potential is the Gibbs energy per particle\(G = \mu N\)：化学势即每个粒子的吉布斯能量

\(G\) is extensive (doubling the system doubles \(G\)), but its natural variables \(T\) and \(P\) do not change with system size. So at fixed \(T, P\), \(G\) can only be proportional to \(N\), and the proportionality constant is \(\mu\):\(G\) 是广延量（系统加倍则 \(G\) 加倍），但其自然变量 \(T\) 与 \(P\) 不随系统大小改变。故在固定 \(T, P\) 下，\(G\) 只能正比于 \(N\)，比例常数为 \(\mu\)：

Euler relation

\[ G = \mu N \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mu = \frac{G}{N} \equiv g(T,P) \]

For a single substance, \(\mu\) is just the Gibbs energy per molecule (or per mole). Dividing \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\) by \(N\) gives the most important formula of Chapter 7:对单一物质，\(\mu\) 就是每个分子（或每摩尔）的吉布斯能量。将 \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\) 除以 \(N\) 得到第 7 章最重要的公式：

\(d\mu\) for a single substance

\[ d\mu = -s\,dT + v\,dP \qquad (s = S/N,\ v = V/N) \]

Proof: the Euler and Gibbs–Duhem relations证明：欧拉关系与吉布斯–杜安关系

Scale the system by a factor \(\lambda\): \(U(\lambda S, \lambda V, \lambda N) = \lambda U(S, V, N)\). Differentiate with respect to \(\lambda\) and set \(\lambda = 1\):将系统放大 \(\lambda\) 倍：\(U(\lambda S, \lambda V, \lambda N) = \lambda U(S, V, N)\)。对 \(\lambda\) 求导并令 \(\lambda = 1\)：

\[ U = TS - PV + \mu N \]

Hence \(G = U - TS + PV = \mu N\), and similarly \(H = TS + \mu N\), \(F = -PV + \mu N\).因此 \(G = U - TS + PV = \mu N\)，同理 \(H = TS + \mu N\)、\(F = -PV + \mu N\)。

Take the total differential of \(U = TS - PV + \mu N\) and subtract \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\). What remains is the Gibbs–Duhem relation:取 \(U = TS - PV + \mu N\) 的全微分并减去 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\)。剩下的即吉布斯–杜安关系：

\[ S\,dT - V\,dP + N\,d\mu = 0 \]

It says \(T\), \(P\) and \(\mu\) cannot all vary independently: fix two and the third is determined. With several components, \(S\,dT - V\,dP + \sum_i N_i\,d\mu_i = 0\); this is the origin of the Gibbs phase rule in Chapter 7.它表明 \(T\)、\(P\) 与 \(\mu\) 不能全部独立变化：固定其中两个，第三个即确定。对多组分，\(S\,dT - V\,dP + \sum_i N_i\,d\mu_i = 0\)；这就是第 7 章吉布斯相律的来源。

Chemical potential of an ideal gas理想气体的化学势

At constant temperature \(d\mu = v\,dP = \frac{k_BT}{P}\,dP\). Integrate from a reference pressure \(P_0\) to \(P\):在恒温 \(d\mu = v\,dP = \frac{k_BT}{P}\,dP\) 下。从参考压强 \(P_0\) 积分到 \(P\)：

Ideal-gas chemical potential

\[ \mu(T,P) = \mu_0(T) + k_BT\ln\frac{P}{P_0} \qquad(\text{per mole: } RT\ln\tfrac{P}{P_0}) \]

Higher pressure means higher chemical potential: the molecules are more "eager" to leave. In a gas mixture, each component uses its own partial pressure \(P_i\). This formula is the starting point for chemical equilibrium constants in Chapter 8.压强越高，化学势越高：分子越"急于"离开。在气体混合物中，每个组分使用其自身分压 \(P_i\)。该公式是第 8 章化学平衡常数的出发点。

6.1  Which way does a process go?6.1 过程朝哪个方向进行？

For an isolated system the Second Law already answers this: entropy goes up, and equilibrium is maximum entropy. But a beaker on a lab bench is not isolated; it exchanges heat with the room. Tracking the room's entropy as well is tedious. What we want is a rule stated only in terms of the system. The potentials provide exactly that:对孤立系统，第二定律已给出答案：熵增加，平衡是最大熵。但实验台上的烧杯并非孤立；它与房间交换热。同时追踪房间的熵十分繁琐。我们需要的是仅用系统本身表述的规则。势恰好提供了这一点：

Direction of spontaneous change and equilibrium

\[ \begin{aligned} &\text{fixed } T, V: && dF \le 0, &&\text{equilibrium at minimum } F \\ &\text{fixed } T, P: && dG \le 0, &&\text{equilibrium at minimum } G \end{aligned} \]

The constant-\(T\), constant-\(P\) case is by far the most common (anything open to the air at room temperature), so the working rule for the rest of the course is: a process at fixed \(T\) and \(P\) happens by itself only if it lowers \(G\).恒定 \(T\)、恒定 \(P\) 的情形最为常见（任何在室温下向空气敞开者），故本课程余下的工作规则是：在固定 \(T\) 与 \(P\) 下，过程只有当其降低 \(G\) 时才自发进行。

Proof: where the minimum principles come from证明：极小值原理从何而来

Combine the Clausius inequality \(dS \geq \text{đ}Q/T\) with the First Law \(\text{đ}Q = dU - \text{đ}W\):将克劳修斯不等式 \(dS \geq \text{đ}Q/T\) 与第一定律 \(\text{đ}Q = dU - \text{đ}W\) 结合：

\[ T\,dS \geq dU - \text{đ}W \]

Fixed \(T\). Let \(\text{đ}W' = -\text{đ}W\) be the work done by the system. Then \(\text{đ}W' \le -dU + T\,dS = -d(U - TS) = -dF\), using \(T\,dS = d(TS)\) at constant \(T\). If no work is done (fixed \(V\)), \(dF \le 0\).固定 \(T\)。设 \(\text{đ}W' = -\text{đ}W\) 为系统做的功。则 \(\text{đ}W' \le -dU + T\,dS = -d(U - TS) = -dF\)，使用恒定 \(T\) 下的 \(T\,dS = d(TS)\)。若不做功（固定 \(V\)），\(dF \le 0\)。

Fixed \(T\) and \(P\). The system must at least push back the surroundings, \(\text{đ}W' = P\,dV\) if that is its only work. Then \(P\,dV \le -dF\), and since \(P\) is constant, \(d(F + PV) = dG \le 0\).固定 \(T\) 与 \(P\)。系统至少须推开环境，\(\text{đ}W' = P\,dV\)（若这是其唯一的功）。则 \(P\,dV \le -dF\)，且由于 \(P\) 恒定，\(d(F + PV) = dG \le 0\)。

Equality holds for reversible changes. At equilibrium nothing can lower the potential further, so it sits at a minimum: \(dF = 0, d^2F \gt 0\) or \(dG = 0, d^2G \gt 0\).对可逆变化取等号。平衡时没有东西能进一步降低势，故它处于极小值：\(dF = 0, d^2F \gt 0\) 或 \(dG = 0, d^2G \gt 0\)。

Example · Does ice melt at −10 °C? At +10 °C?

For melting ice, \(\Delta H = 6.01\ \text{kJ/mol}\), and at 0 °C the process is in equilibrium. Assuming \(\Delta H\) and \(\Delta S\) don't change much with temperature, find the sign of \(\Delta G\) for melting at 263 K and at 283 K.对融冰，\(\Delta H = 6.01\ \text{kJ/mol}\)，在 0 °C 时过程处于平衡。假设 \(\Delta H\) 与 \(\Delta S\) 随温度变化不大，求 263 K 与 283 K 下融化的 \(\Delta G\) 的符号。

Show solution显示解答

At the melting point \(\Delta G = 0\), so \(\Delta S = \Delta H/T_m = 6010/273.15 = 22.0\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\). Then \(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\):在熔点处 \(\Delta G = 0\)，故 \(\Delta S = \Delta H/T_m = 6010/273.15 = 22.0\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\)。于是 \(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\)：

\[ T = 263\ \text{K}: \ \Delta G = 6010 - 263(22.0) \approx +220\ \text{J/mol} \gt 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{ice does not melt} \]

\[ T = 283\ \text{K}: \ \Delta G = 6010 - 283(22.0) \approx -220\ \text{J/mol} \lt 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{ice melts} \]

Melting costs energy (\(\Delta H \gt 0\)) but gains entropy (\(\Delta S \gt 0\)). The \(-T\Delta S\) term grows with temperature, so above 0 °C entropy wins and below it energy wins. That tug of war inside \(G = H - TS\) is the reason phase transitions happen at a definite temperature.融化消耗能量（\(\Delta H \gt 0\)）但获得熵（\(\Delta S \gt 0\)）。\(-T\Delta S\) 项随温度增长，故高于 0 °C 时熵占优，低于 0 °C 时能量占优。\(G = H - TS\) 内部的这种竞争正是相变发生在确定温度的原因。

Maximum useful work最大有用功

The proof above also shows that at fixed \(T\) the work a system can deliver is at most \(-\Delta F\), and at fixed \(T\) and \(P\) the work beyond pushing back the atmosphere (e.g. electrical work) is at most \(-\Delta G\).上述证明还表明，固定 \(T\) 时系统能输出的功至多为 \(-\Delta F\)；在固定 \(T\) 与 \(P\) 时，超出推开大气的那部分功（例如电功）至多为 \(-\Delta G\)。

Example · A hydrogen fuel cell

For \(\mathrm{H_2} + \tfrac12\mathrm{O_2} \to \mathrm{H_2O(l)}\) at 298 K and 1 bar: \(\Delta H = -286\ \text{kJ/mol}\), \(\Delta G = -237\ \text{kJ/mol}\). How much electrical work can a fuel cell deliver per mole of water, and where does the rest of the energy go?对 298 K、1 bar 下的 \(\mathrm{H_2} + \tfrac12\mathrm{O_2} \to \mathrm{H_2O(l)}\)：\(\Delta H = -286\ \text{kJ/mol}\)、\(\Delta G = -237\ \text{kJ/mol}\)。燃料电池每摩尔水能输出多少电功，其余能量去了哪里？

Show solution显示解答

Maximum non-expansion work \(= -\Delta G = 237\ \text{kJ}\). Burning the hydrogen would release \(-\Delta H = 286\ \text{kJ}\) of heat, but even a perfect fuel cell can turn only 237 kJ into electrical work. The remaining \(\Delta H - \Delta G = T\Delta S = -49\ \text{kJ}\) must be released as heat, because the products have lower entropy than the reactants (three molecules of gas become one of liquid), and that entropy has to be dumped into the surroundings.最大非膨胀功 \(= -\Delta G = 237\ \text{kJ}\)。燃烧氢会释放 \(-\Delta H = 286\ \text{kJ}\) 的热，但即使完美的燃料电池也只能将 237 kJ 转化为电功。其余 \(\Delta H - \Delta G = T\Delta S = -49\ \text{kJ}\) 必须以热的形式释放，因为产物的熵低于反应物（三分子气体变为一分子液体），该熵必须排入环境。

6.2  Equilibrium between two parts: equal \(T\), \(P\), \(\mu\)6.2 两部分之间的平衡：\(T\)、\(P\)、\(\mu\) 相等

Now split a system into two parts that can exchange something. The rule is easy to state:现在将系统分为可以交换物质的两部分。规则易于陈述：

Equilibrium conditions

Whatever the two parts can exchange, the matching intensive variable must be equal on both sides:无论两部分能交换什么，相应的强度量在两侧必须相等：

\[ \text{energy} \Rightarrow T_1 = T_2, \qquad \text{volume} \Rightarrow P_1 = P_2, \qquad \text{particles} \Rightarrow \mu_1 = \mu_2 \]

When a condition is not met, the system moves to increase its total entropy, which fixes the direction of flow:当条件不满足时，系统移动以增加总熵，这确定了流动方向：

If the parts can exchange若两部分能交换Equilibrium needs平衡需要Otherwise否则
Energy (heat)能量（热）\(T_1 = T_2\)Heat flows from high \(T\) to low \(T\)热从高 \(T\) 流向低 \(T\)
Volume (movable wall)体积（可动壁）\(P_1 = P_2\)The high-\(P\) side expands\(P\) 高的一侧膨胀
Particles (permeable wall)粒子（可渗透壁）\(\mu_1 = \mu_2\)Particles move from high \(\mu\) to low \(\mu\)粒子从高 \(\mu\) 流向低 \(\mu\)

The first two rows are familiar. The third is the new one, and it is what Chapters 7 and 8 run on: whenever particles can move between two places (two phases, a solution and pure solvent, reactants and products), equilibrium means their chemical potentials are equal.前两行已熟悉。第三行是新的，第 7、8 章正依赖于此：只要粒子能在两处之间移动（两个相、溶液与纯溶剂、反应物与产物），平衡即意味着它们的化学势相等。

Proof: maximise the total entropy证明：最大化总熵

Entropy is extensive, so \(S = S_1(U_1, V_1, N_1) + S_2(U_2, V_2, N_2)\). The whole system is isolated, so \(dU_2 = -dU_1\), \(dV_2 = -dV_1\), \(dN_2 = -dN_1\). From \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\), the entropy derivatives are \(\partial S/\partial U = 1/T\), \(\partial S/\partial V = P/T\), \(\partial S/\partial N = -\mu/T\). So熵是广延量，故 \(S = S_1(U_1, V_1, N_1) + S_2(U_2, V_2, N_2)\)。整个系统孤立，故 \(dU_2 = -dU_1\)、\(dV_2 = -dV_1\)、\(dN_2 = -dN_1\)。由 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\)，熵的导数为 \(\partial S/\partial U = 1/T\)、\(\partial S/\partial V = P/T\)、\(\partial S/\partial N = -\mu/T\)。故

\[ dS = \left(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2}\right)dU_1 + \left(\frac{P_1}{T_1} - \frac{P_2}{T_2}\right)dV_1 - \left(\frac{\mu_1}{T_1} - \frac{\mu_2}{T_2}\right)dN_1 \]

At equilibrium \(dS = 0\) for every allowed \(dU_1, dV_1, dN_1\), so each bracket vanishes: \(T_1 = T_2\), then \(P_1 = P_2\), then \(\mu_1 = \mu_2\).平衡时对每个允许的 \(dU_1, dV_1, dN_1\) 有 \(dS = 0\)，故每个方括号为零：\(T_1 = T_2\)，然后 \(P_1 = P_2\)，然后 \(\mu_1 = \mu_2\)。

Away from equilibrium a spontaneous change has \(dS \gt 0\). Take just the first term: if \(T_1 \lt T_2\) the bracket is positive, so \(dU_1 \gt 0\): energy flows into the colder part. The other two rows of the table follow the same way.偏离平衡时，自发变化有 \(dS \gt 0\)。仅看第一项：若 \(T_1 \lt T_2\) 方括号为正，则 \(dU_1 \gt 0\)：能量流入较冷的部分。表格另外两行同理。

6.3  Stability: will it stay there?6.3 稳定性：它是否会保持在该状态？

"Minimum of \(G\)" (or maximum of \(S\)) contains two pieces of information. The first derivative being zero gives the equilibrium conditions above. The second derivative having the right sign says the state is a true minimum, not a maximum or a saddle, so that small random fluctuations get pushed back. That requirement constrains the material itself:"\(G\) 取极小"（或 \(S\) 取极大）包含两层信息。一阶导数为零给出上述平衡条件。二阶导数具有正确符号说明该状态是真正的极小值，而非极大值或鞍点，从而使小的随机涨落被推回。这一要求约束材料本身：

Stability conditions

\[ C_V \gt 0, \qquad \kappa_T = -\frac{1}{V}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} \gt 0 \]

Both conditions have a direct physical meaning:两个条件都有直接的物理含义：

  • \(C_V \gt 0\) (thermal stability). If a small region randomly gains some energy, its temperature rises, so heat flows back out of it. With a negative heat capacity, gaining energy would make it colder; it would pull in even more heat and the fluctuation would run away.\(C_V \gt 0\)（热稳定性）。若某小区域偶然获得能量，其温度升高，故热从其中流出。若热容为负，获得能量会使其变冷；它会吸收更多热，涨落将失控。
  • \(\kappa_T \gt 0\) (mechanical stability). If a small region gets squeezed, its pressure goes up and it pushes back. If squeezing lowered its pressure, it would keep collapsing.\(\kappa_T \gt 0\)（力学稳定性）。若某小区域被挤压，其压强升高并反推。若挤压降低其压强，它将持续坍缩。
Where it's used later
  • Chapter 7: below the critical temperature the van der Waals equation has a stretch with \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\), i.e. \(\kappa_T \lt 0\). That uniform fluid is unstable and splits into liquid + gas; this is the liquid–gas transition.第 7 章：在临界温度以下，范德瓦尔斯方程有一段满足 \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\)，即 \(\kappa_T \lt 0\)。该均匀流体不稳定并分裂为液 + 气；这就是液–气相变。
  • Chapter 8: a mixture whose \(G(x)\) curves the wrong way is unstable in the same sense and separates into two phases (oil and water).第 8 章：其 \(G(x)\) 朝错误方向弯曲的混合物以同样方式不稳定，并分离为两个相（油和水）。
  • Chapter 5: \(\kappa_T \gt 0\) is why \(C_P - C_V = TV\alpha^2/\kappa_T \geq 0\).第 5 章：\(\kappa_T \gt 0\) 正是 \(C_P - C_V = TV\alpha^2/\kappa_T \geq 0\) 的原因。
Proof: the second variation of entropy证明：熵的二阶变分

At fixed \(N\), let the energy and volume fluctuate by \(dU\), \(dV\). The second-order change of \(S(U,V)\) is在固定 \(N\) 下，令能量与体积涨落 \(dU\)、\(dV\)。\(S(U,V)\) 的二阶变化为

\[ d^2S = \tfrac{1}{2}\left[S_{UU}(dU)^2 + 2S_{UV}\,dU\,dV + S_{VV}(dV)^2\right] \]

The bracket equals \(d(S_U)\,dU + d(S_V)\,dV = d(1/T)\,dU + d(P/T)\,dV\). Now write everything in terms of \(dT\) and \(dV\), using \(d(1/T) = -dT/T^2\), \(dU = C_V\,dT + [T(\partial P/\partial T)_V - P]\,dV\), \(d(P/T) = dP/T - P\,dT/T^2\) and \(dP = (\partial P/\partial T)_V dT + (\partial P/\partial V)_T dV\). Expanding, all cross terms \(dT\,dV\) cancel, leaving方括号等于 \(d(S_U)\,dU + d(S_V)\,dV = d(1/T)\,dU + d(P/T)\,dV\)。现将一切用 \(dT\) 与 \(dV\) 表示，使用 \(d(1/T) = -dT/T^2\)、\(dU = C_V\,dT + [T(\partial P/\partial T)_V - P]\,dV\)、\(d(P/T) = dP/T - P\,dT/T^2\) 与 \(dP = (\partial P/\partial T)_V dT + (\partial P/\partial V)_T dV\)。展开后所有交叉项 \(dT\,dV\) 相消，剩下

\[ d^2S = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{C_V}{T^2}(dT)^2 + \frac{1}{2T}\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T}(dV)^2 \]

For this to be negative for every \(dT\) and \(dV\), each coefficient must be negative: \(C_V \gt 0\) and \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \lt 0\), i.e. \(\kappa_T \gt 0\).要使其对任意 \(dT\) 与 \(dV\) 为负，每个系数必须为负：\(C_V \gt 0\) 与 \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \lt 0\)，即 \(\kappa_T \gt 0\)。

Example · Constraints on a model entropy (exam-style)

A substance has entropy \(S = a\,U^bV^cN^d\) with \(a \gt 0\). What constraints apply to \(b\), \(c\), \(d\)?某物质的熵为 \(S = a\,U^bV^cN^d\)，其中 \(a \gt 0\)。对 \(b\)、\(c\)、\(d\) 有哪些约束？

Show solution显示解答

Extensivity: doubling the system must double \(S\). \(S(\lambda U, \lambda V, \lambda N) = \lambda^{b+c+d}S\) must equal \(\lambda S\), so \(b + c + d = 1\).广延性：系统加倍必须使 \(S\) 加倍。\(S(\lambda U, \lambda V, \lambda N) = \lambda^{b+c+d}S\) 必须等于 \(\lambda S\)，故 \(b + c + d = 1\)。

Stability: \(S\) must curve downward in each variable:稳定性：\(S\) 必须沿每个变量向下弯曲：

\[ \frac{\partial^2 S}{\partial U^2} = ab(b-1)\,U^{b-2}V^cN^d \lt 0 \;\Rightarrow\; 0 \lt b \lt 1 \]

6.4  Le Chatelier's principle6.4 勒夏特列原理

The stability conditions have a qualitative version that needs no calculation:稳定性条件有一个无需计算的定性版本：

Le Chatelier's principle

When a system in equilibrium is disturbed (temperature, pressure, volume or concentration changed), it shifts to a new equilibrium in the direction that partly undoes the disturbance.当处于平衡的系统受到扰动（温度、压强、体积或浓度改变）时，它向部分抵消该扰动的方向移动到新的平衡。

This is the same logic as \(C_V \gt 0\) and \(\kappa_T \gt 0\): a stable equilibrium pushes back. If it amplified the disturbance instead, it would not have been stable to begin with.这与 \(C_V \gt 0\) 与 \(\kappa_T \gt 0\) 的逻辑相同：稳定的平衡会反推。若它反而放大扰动，则它原本就不稳定。

7.1  The key picture: lowest \(\mu\) wins7.1 关键图景：\(\mu\) 最低者胜

A phase is a region of matter that is uniform throughout: same density, composition and so on everywhere. Ice, liquid water and steam are three phases of one substance. (A uniform mix of He and N₂ gas is one phase; ice cubes in water are two.)相是处处均匀的物质区域：密度、组成等处处相同。冰、液态水与蒸汽是同一物质的三个相。（He 与 N₂ 气体的均匀混合物是一个相；水中的冰块是两个相。）

At given \(T\) and \(P\), each phase has its own chemical potential \(\mu(T,P)\), the Gibbs energy per molecule. Molecules drift from high \(\mu\) to low \(\mu\) (§6.2), so at any \((T,P)\) the substance ends up entirely in the phase with the lowest \(\mu\). Now recall from §5.7:在给定 \(T\) 与 \(P\) 下，每个相各有其化学势 \(\mu(T,P)\)，即每个分子的吉布斯能量。分子从高 \(\mu\) 漂向低 \(\mu\)（§6.2），故在任何 \((T,P)\) 下，物质最终完全处于 \(\mu\) 最低的相。现在回顾 §5.7：

\[ \left(\frac{\partial\mu}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} = -s, \qquad \left(\frac{\partial\mu}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} = v \]

So on a plot of \(\mu\) against \(T\), each phase is a line sloping down with slope \(-s\). A gas has much more entropy per molecule than a liquid, which has more than a solid. So the gas line is the steepest and the solid line the flattest:故在 \(\mu\) 对 \(T\) 的图上，每个相是一条斜率为 \(-s\) 的向下倾斜直线。气相每分子的熵远大于液相，液相大于固相。故气相线最陡，固相线最平：

Read the picture from left to right:从左至右读图：

  • At low \(T\) the solid has the lowest \(\mu\): it is stable.在低的 \(T\) 下，固相的 \(\mu\) 最低：它稳定。
  • The steeper liquid line overtakes it at \(T_m\): the substance melts.更陡的液相线在 \(T_m\) 处超过它：物质融化。
  • The even steeper gas line overtakes the liquid at \(T_b\): it boils.更陡的气相线在 \(T_b\) 处超过液相：物质沸腾。

The transitions are sharp because the lowest line switches abruptly at a crossing. At the crossing itself the two \(\mu\) are equal and both phases can coexist:转变是突变的，因为最低的线在交点处突然切换。在交点处两个 \(\mu\) 相等，两相可以共存：

Two phases coexist when

\[ T^{\text{I}} = T^{\text{II}}, \qquad P^{\text{I}} = P^{\text{II}}, \qquad \mu^{\text{I}}(T,P) = \mu^{\text{II}}(T,P) \]

The picture also explains pressure: raising \(P\) raises each \(\mu\) by \(v\,\Delta P\). The gas has by far the largest \(v\), so its line rises most and its crossing with the liquid moves to higher temperature. That is why water boils above 100 °C in a pressure cooker. §7.3 makes this quantitative.该图也解释了压强：升高 \(P\) 使每条 \(\mu\) 升高 \(v\,\Delta P\)。气相的 \(v\) 远最大，故其线抬升最多，与液相的交点移至更高温度。这就是高压锅中水在 100 °C 以上沸腾的原因。§7.3 将把这一点定量化。

Why a uniform phase can split in two (the entropy argument)均匀相为何会一分为二（熵的论证）

Suppose the entropy of a uniform substance, as a function of its energy, has a stretch where \(S(U)\) curves upward. Split the system into two halves with energies \(U + \Delta U\) and \(U - \Delta U\). The total energy is the same, but the average entropy is假设均匀物质的熵作为其能量的函数，存在一段 \(S(U)\) 向上弯曲。将系统分成两半，能量分别为 \(U + \Delta U\) 与 \(U - \Delta U\)。总能量相同，但平均熵为

\[ \tfrac{1}{2}\left[S(U + \Delta U) + S(U - \Delta U)\right] \gt S(U) \]

because a chord lies above an upward-curving curve. Splitting raises the entropy, so the uniform state is not the equilibrium state: the system separates into two parts with different energy densities, i.e. two phases. This is exactly the violation of the stability condition \(\partial^2S/\partial U^2 \lt 0\) from §6.3.因为弦位于向上弯曲的曲线之上。分裂提高了熵，故均匀态不是平衡态：系统分离成能量密度不同的两部分，即两个相。这正是 §6.3 中稳定性条件 \(\partial^2S/\partial U^2 \lt 0\) 的失效。

\[ f = K + 2 - P \]

7.3  The Clausius–Clapeyron equation7.3 克劳修斯–克拉珀龙方程

A coexistence line has a definite slope, and we can predict it from two measurable numbers: the latent heat and the volume change.共存线具有确定的斜率，且可由两个可测量的数预测：潜热与体积变化。

Clausius–Clapeyron equation

\[ \frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta v} = \frac{L}{T\,\Delta v} \]

Here \(L = T\Delta s\) is the latent heat (heat absorbed going from phase I to phase II) and \(\Delta v\) the change in volume, both for the same amount of substance (per molecule, per mole, or per kilogram).这里 \(L = T\Delta s\) 为潜热（从相 I 到相 II 吸收的热），\(\Delta v\) 为体积变化，二者均指同一数量的物质（每分子、每摩尔或每千克）。

What it tells you
  • Boiling: \(L \gt 0\) and the gas takes up far more volume (\(\Delta v \gt 0\)), so \(dP/dT \gt 0\): higher pressure → higher boiling point.沸腾：\(L \gt 0\)，且气体占据大得多的体积（\(\Delta v \gt 0\)），故 \(dP/dT \gt 0\)：压强越高 → 沸点越高。
  • Melting of ice: \(L \gt 0\), but liquid water is denser than ice, so \(\Delta v \lt 0\) and \(dP/dT \lt 0\). Squeezing ice lowers its melting point. Almost every other substance behaves the opposite way.冰的融化：\(L \gt 0\)，但液态水比冰更致密，故 \(\Delta v \lt 0\)、\(dP/dT \lt 0\)。挤压冰会降低其熔点。几乎所有其他物质的行为相反。
Proof (three lines)证明（三行）

Along the coexistence line, \(\mu^{\text{I}} = \mu^{\text{II}}\) at every point, so moving along the line their changes are equal: \(d\mu^{\text{I}} = d\mu^{\text{II}}\). With \(d\mu = -s\,dT + v\,dP\):沿共存线，每一点 \(\mu^{\text{I}} = \mu^{\text{II}}\)，故沿线移动时二者变化相等：\(d\mu^{\text{I}} = d\mu^{\text{II}}\)。取 \(d\mu = -s\,dT + v\,dP\)：

\[ -s^{\text{I}}\,dT + v^{\text{I}}\,dP = -s^{\text{II}}\,dT + v^{\text{II}}\,dP \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{s^{\text{II}} - s^{\text{I}}}{v^{\text{II}} - v^{\text{I}}} \]

The phase change happens at constant \(T\) and is reversible, so the heat absorbed is \(L = T(s^{\text{II}} - s^{\text{I}})\).相变在恒定 \(T\) 下发生且可逆，故吸收的热为 \(L = T(s^{\text{II}} - s^{\text{I}})\)。

Vapour pressure: the integrated form蒸气压：积分形式

For liquid → gas (or solid → gas), three approximations make the equation easy to integrate: the gas volume dominates (\(\Delta V \approx V_g\)); the vapour is ideal (\(V_g = RT/P\) per mole); and \(L\) is roughly constant. Then \(dP/dT = LP/(RT^2)\), which integrates to对液 → 气（或固 → 气），三个近似使方程易于积分：气体体积占主导（\(\Delta V \approx V_g\)）；蒸汽为理想气体（每摩尔 \(V_g = RT/P\)）；且 \(L\) 大致为常数。则 \(dP/dT = LP/(RT^2)\)，积分为

Vapour-pressure curve

\[ \ln P = -\frac{L}{RT} + \text{const}, \qquad \ln\frac{P_2}{P_1} = -\frac{L}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}\right) \]

(\(L\) per mole.) Plotting \(\ln P\) against \(1/T\) gives a straight line of slope \(-L/R\); reading \(L\) off such a slope is a standard exam step.（每摩尔 \(L\)。）将 \(\ln P\) 对 \(1/T\) 作图得斜率为 \(-L/R\) 的直线；从该斜率读出 \(L\) 是标准的考试步骤。

7.4  The van der Waals fluid: where the liquid–gas transition comes from7.4 范德瓦尔斯流体：液–气相变从何而来

So far we have taken the existence of liquid and gas for granted. The van der Waals equation shows how a single equation of state produces both. Per molecule (\(v = V/N\)):到目前为止我们将液体与气体的存在视为理所当然。范德瓦尔斯方程展示了一个状态方程如何同时产生二者。按每个分子（\(v = V/N\)）：

\[ P = \frac{k_BT}{v - b} - \frac{a}{v^2} \]

\(b\) accounts for the finite size of molecules, \(a\) for their mutual attraction. At high temperature the isotherms look like an ideal gas. Below a critical temperature \(T_c\) each isotherm develops a wiggle containing a stretch where \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\), i.e. \(\kappa_T \lt 0\). By §6.3 that is unstable: a uniform fluid there splits into two phases, liquid and gas.\(b\) 计入分子的有限体积，\(a\) 计入其相互吸引。在高温下等温线形似理想气体。在某一临界温度 \(T_c\) 以下，每条等温线出现一个弯曲，其中含有一段满足 \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\)，即 \(\kappa_T \lt 0\)。由 §6.3，那是不稳定的：该处的均匀流体分裂为两个相，液相与气相。

Van der Waals critical point

\[ v_c = 3b, \qquad k_BT_c = \frac{8a}{27b}, \qquad P_c = \frac{a}{27b^2}, \qquad \frac{P_cv_c}{k_BT_c} = \frac{3}{8} \]

At \(T_c\) the wiggle shrinks to a single point where the isotherm is momentarily flat and has an inflection; above \(T_c\) liquid and gas can no longer be told apart.在 \(T_c\) 处弯曲收缩为一点，等温线在该点瞬间变平并具有拐点；在 \(T_c\) 以上，液体与气体不再能区分。

Proof: critical constants证明：临界常数

Require \((\partial P/\partial v)_T = 0\) and \((\partial^2 P/\partial v^2)_T = 0\):要求 \((\partial P/\partial v)_T = 0\) 与 \((\partial^2 P/\partial v^2)_T = 0\)：

\[ -\frac{k_BT}{(v-b)^2} + \frac{2a}{v^3} = 0, \qquad \frac{2k_BT}{(v-b)^3} - \frac{6a}{v^4} = 0 \]

7.5  First-order and continuous transitions7.5 一级相变与连续相变

In melting and boiling, \(\mu\) is continuous across the transition (the two lines cross), but its slopes jump: \(\partial\mu/\partial T = -s\) and \(\partial\mu/\partial P = v\) are different for the two phases. In some transitions the two phases become identical at the transition point: \(\Delta s = 0\), \(\Delta v = 0\), no latent heat. 8.1  The entropy of mixing8.1 混合熵

Put gas A in the left half of a box and gas B in the right half, at the same \(T\) and \(P\). Remove the partition. The gases mix and never unmix by themselves: mixing is irreversible, so it must create entropy. For ideal gases nothing else changes (no interactions, so \(\Delta U = 0\) and \(T\) stays the same); each gas simply spreads into a larger volume, like a free expansion.将气体 A 置于盒子左半、气体 B 置于右半，温度 \(T\) 与压强 \(P\) 相同。抽去隔板。两种气体混合，且永不自行分离：混合是不可逆的，故必然产生熵。对理想气体，其他一切不变（无相互作用，故 \(\Delta U = 0\) 与 \(T\) 保持不变）；每种气体只是扩散到更大的体积，如同自由膨胀。

Entropy of mixing (ideal)

\[ \Delta S_{\text{mix}} = -Nk_B\sum_i x_i\ln x_i \;\gt\; 0, \qquad x_i = \frac{N_i}{N} \]

Since every \(x_i\) is between 0 and 1, every \(\ln x_i \lt 0\) and the result is positive. Equal amounts of two gases (\(x = \tfrac12\)) give \(Nk_B\ln 2\), where \(N\) is the total number of molecules.由于每个 \(x_i\) 介于 0 与 1 之间，每个 \(\ln x_i \lt 0\)，结果为正。两种气体各取同等量（\(x = \tfrac12\)）给出 \(Nk_B\ln 2\)，其中 \(N\) 为分子总数。

Why this matters for the rest of the chapter

Mixing adds \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}}\) to \(G\), which lowers it. Differentiating that term gives each component a chemical potential lowered by a \(k_BT\ln x\) term:混合给 \(G\) 加上 \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}}\) 项，从而降低它。对该项求导，即得每个组分的化学势被一个 \(k_BT\ln x\) 项降低：

\[ \mu_i = \mu_i^{0} + k_BT\ln x_i \]

where \(\mu_i^0\) is the pure substance's value. This single fact drives everything below: a solvent with something dissolved in it has a lower \(\mu\) than the pure solvent, so water "prefers" to be in the solution.其中 \(\mu_i^0\) 为纯物质的值。这一事实支撑了以下全部内容：溶有物质的溶剂，其 \(\mu\) 低于纯溶剂，故水"偏好"存在于溶液中。

Proof of the formula, and the Gibbs paradox公式的证明与吉布斯佯谬

Let species \(i\) start with \(N_i\) molecules in its own compartment \(V_i\), all at the same density, so \(V_i/V = x_i\). An ideal gas's entropy depends on volume through \(Nk_B\ln V\). Before mixing: \(k_B\sum_i N_i\ln V_i = k_BN\sum_i x_i\ln x_i + k_BN\ln V\). After mixing every species fills \(V\): \(k_BN\ln V\). The difference is \(-Nk_B\sum_i x_i\ln x_i\).令物种 \(i\) 初始有 \(N_i\) 个分子位于其自身隔间 \(V_i\) 中，密度均相同，故 \(V_i/V = x_i\)。理想气体的熵通过 \(Nk_B\ln V\) 依赖于体积。混合前：\(k_B\sum_i N_i\ln V_i = k_BN\sum_i x_i\ln x_i + k_BN\ln V\)。混合后每个物种充满 \(V\)：\(k_BN\ln V\)。差值为 \(-Nk_B\sum_i x_i\ln x_i\)。

Gibbs paradox. Repeat with the same gas on both sides. The formula would still predict \(2Nk_B\ln 2\), yet nothing physical has happened; reinserting the partition restores the original state. The resolution is that identical molecules are indistinguishable: the correct volume dependence is \(Nk_B\ln(V/N)\) (as in the Sackur–Tetrode equation), and joining two identical gases at the same density leaves \(V/N\) unchanged, so \(\Delta S = 0\). For different gases, each species' own \(V/N_i\) still grows, so the mixing entropy survives.吉布斯佯谬。换成同种气体在两侧重复。公式仍会预测 \(2Nk_B\ln 2\)，但物理上什么都没变；插回隔板即恢复原状。解决之道在于全同分子不可分辨：正确的体积依赖为 \(Nk_B\ln(V/N)\)（如萨克–特策罗德方程所示），将两份等密度的同种气体合并使 \(V/N\) 不变，故 \(\Delta S = 0\)。对不同的气体，每个物种自身的 \(V/N_i\) 仍然增大，故混合熵依然存在。

8.2  Chemical equilibrium: the law of mass action8.2 化学平衡：质量作用定律

Take \(\mathrm{N_2} + 3\,\mathrm{H_2} \rightleftharpoons 2\,\mathrm{NH_3}\) at fixed \(T\) and \(P\). If the reaction advances a little, N₂ drops by \(\varepsilon\), H₂ by \(3\varepsilon\), and NH₃ rises by \(2\varepsilon\). At equilibrium \(G\) is a minimum, so \(dG = \sum\mu_i\,dN_i = 0\) for this change:取 \(\mathrm{N_2} + 3\,\mathrm{H_2} \rightleftharpoons 2\,\mathrm{NH_3}\)，固定 \(T\) 与 \(P\)。若反应推进少许，N₂ 减少 \(\varepsilon\)，H₂ 减少 \(3\varepsilon\)，NH₃ 增加 \(2\varepsilon\)。平衡时 \(G\) 取极小，故对该变化 \(dG = \sum\mu_i\,dN_i = 0\)：

Condition for chemical equilibrium

\[ \mu_{\mathrm{N_2}} + 3\,\mu_{\mathrm{H_2}} = 2\,\mu_{\mathrm{NH_3}} \]

The chemical potentials balance with the same coefficients as the reaction equation. Now substitute the ideal-gas form \(\mu_i = g_i^0 + RT\ln(P_i/P_0)\) per mole (§5.7), with \(P_i\) the partial pressure:化学势以与反应式相同的系数平衡。现在代入理想气体形式 \(\mu_i = g_i^0 + RT\ln(P_i/P_0)\)（每摩尔，§5.7），其中 \(P_i\) 为分压：

Law of mass action

\[ \frac{(P_{\mathrm{NH_3}}/P_0)^2}{(P_{\mathrm{N_2}}/P_0)(P_{\mathrm{H_2}}/P_0)^3} = K(T), \qquad K(T) = e^{-\Delta g_0/RT} \]

\(\Delta g_0 = 2g^0_{\mathrm{NH_3}} - g^0_{\mathrm{N_2}} - 3g^0_{\mathrm{H_2}}\) (products minus reactants, at the reference pressure \(P_0\)). The equilibrium constant \(K\) depends only on temperature: whatever amounts you start with, the partial pressures adjust until this ratio equals \(K(T)\). Large \(K\) (negative \(\Delta g_0\)) means mostly products.\(\Delta g_0 = 2g^0_{\mathrm{NH_3}} - g^0_{\mathrm{N_2}} - 3g^0_{\mathrm{H_2}}\)（产物减反应物，在参考压强 \(P_0\) 下）。平衡常数 \(K\) 只依赖于温度：无论初始量如何，分压都会调整至该比值等于 \(K(T)\)。\(K\) 很大（\(\Delta g_0\) 为负）意味着产物占主导。

Proof: from equal \(\mu\)'s to \(K\)证明：从 \(\mu\) 相等推出 \(K\)

\[ g^0_{\mathrm{N_2}} + RT\ln\tfrac{P_{\mathrm{N_2}}}{P_0} + 3g^0_{\mathrm{H_2}} + 3RT\ln\tfrac{P_{\mathrm{H_2}}}{P_0} = 2g^0_{\mathrm{NH_3}} + 2RT\ln\tfrac{P_{\mathrm{NH_3}}}{P_0} \]

Move the \(g^0\) terms to one side, combine the logarithms, and divide by \(RT\): \(\ln[\ldots] = -\Delta g_0/RT\). Exponentiate.将 \(g^0\) 项移到一边，合并对数，再除以 \(RT\)：\(\ln[\ldots] = -\Delta g_0/RT\)。取指数。

Pressure effect (Le Chatelier, quantitatively). With mole fractions \(x_i\) and total pressure \(P\), \(P_i = x_iP\), so \(\frac{x_{\mathrm{NH_3}}^2}{x_{\mathrm{N_2}}x_{\mathrm{H_2}}^3} = K(T)\,(P/P_0)^2\). The power 2 is (gas moles on the left) − (gas moles on the right). Raising \(P\) increases the fraction of NH₃, as §6.4 predicted.

How \(K\) changes with temperature\(K\) 如何随温度变化

van 't Hoff equation

\[ \frac{d\ln K}{dT} = \frac{\Delta h_0}{RT^2} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \ln\frac{K_2}{K_1} = -\frac{\Delta h_0}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}\right) \]

\(\Delta h_0\) is the heat absorbed by the reaction. Endothermic (\(\Delta h_0 \gt 0\)): \(K\) grows with \(T\). Exothermic (\(\Delta h_0 \lt 0\)): \(K\) shrinks with \(T\). This is the quantitative form of Le Chatelier. Notice it has exactly the same shape as the vapour-pressure law \(d\ln P/dT = L/RT^2\) from §7.3, because evaporation is just the "reaction" liquid ⇌ vapour.\(\Delta h_0\) 是反应吸收的热。吸热（\(\Delta h_0 \gt 0\)）：\(K\) 随 \(T\) 增大。放热（\(\Delta h_0 \lt 0\)）：\(K\) 随 \(T\) 减小。这是勒夏特列原理的定量形式。注意其形式与 §7.3 的蒸气压定律 \(d\ln P/dT = L/RT^2\) 完全相同，因为蒸发不过是"反应" 液 ⇌ 气。

Proof证明

\(\frac{d}{dT}\left(-\frac{\Delta g_0}{RT}\right) = -\frac{1}{R}\,\frac{T\,(d\Delta g_0/dT) - \Delta g_0}{T^2}\). Use \(d\Delta g_0/dT = -\Delta s_0\) and \(\Delta g_0 = \Delta h_0 - T\Delta s_0\): the numerator becomes \(-T\Delta s_0 - \Delta h_0 + T\Delta s_0 = -\Delta h_0\).\(\frac{d}{dT}\left(-\frac{\Delta g_0}{RT}\right) = -\frac{1}{R}\,\frac{T\,(d\Delta g_0/dT) - \Delta g_0}{T^2}\)。使用 \(d\Delta g_0/dT = -\Delta s_0\) 与 \(\Delta g_0 = \Delta h_0 - T\Delta s_0\)：分子变为 \(-T\Delta s_0 - \Delta h_0 + T\Delta s_0 = -\Delta h_0\)。

8.3  Dilute solutions8.3 稀溶液

A dilute solution has a little solute B (salt, sugar) dissolved in a lot of solvent A (water): \(N_B \ll N_A\). Solute molecules are far apart, so they interact only with the solvent, never with each other. Write \(x = N_B/N_A\) for the (small) solute fraction. The one result we need is how dissolving the solute changes the solvent's chemical potential:稀溶液中，少量溶质 B（盐、糖）溶解于大量溶剂 A（水）中：\(N_B \ll N_A\)。溶质分子相距很远，故只与溶剂相互作用，彼此不相互作用。以 \(x = N_B/N_A\) 表示（很小的）溶质分数。我们所需的唯一结果是：溶解溶质如何改变溶剂的化学势：

Solvent and solute in a dilute solution

\[ \mu_A = \mu_0(T,P) - x\,k_BT, \qquad \mu_B = f(T,P) + k_BT\ln x \]

\(\mu_0\) is the chemical potential of the pure solvent; \(f\) describes solute–solvent interactions and never appears in the results. The first formula is the important one: dissolving anything lowers the solvent's \(\mu\) by \(xk_BT\), regardless of what the solute is. It is the \(k_BT\ln x_A\) term from §8.1 with \(\ln(1-x) \approx -x\).\(\mu_0\) 为纯溶剂的化学势；\(f\) 描述溶质–溶剂相互作用，在结果中从不出现。第一个公式最为重要：无论溶质为何物，溶解任何物质都会使溶剂的 \(\mu\) 降低 \(xk_BT\)。它就是 §8.1 中的 \(k_BT\ln x_A\) 项，取 \(\ln(1-x) \approx -x\)。

Why this matters

Because only the number of dissolved particles enters (not their identity), the effects in §8.4 are called colligative properties. One mole of sugar and one mole of urea shift the freezing point of water by the same amount. A salt like NaCl splits into two ions, so it counts double.由于只涉及溶解粒子的数目（而非其身份），§8.4 中的效应称为依数性。一摩尔糖与一摩尔尿素使水的凝固点降低相同的量。而 NaCl 这类盐会解离为两个离子，故计为双份。

Proof: the Gibbs energy of a dilute solution证明：稀溶液的吉布斯能量

Start from pure solvent, \(G = N_A\mu_0\). Adding \(N_B\) solute molecules adds an interaction term \(N_Bf(T,P)\) (proportional to \(N_B\), since solute molecules don't meet each other) and the mixing term \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}} = k_BT[N_B\ln(N_B/N) + N_A\ln(N_A/N)]\). For \(N_B \ll N_A\), \(N \approx N_A\) and \(N_A\ln(N_A/N) \approx -N_B\), so从纯溶剂出发，\(G = N_A\mu_0\)。加入 \(N_B\) 个溶质分子，增加一个相互作用项 \(N_Bf(T,P)\)（正比于 \(N_B\)，因为溶质分子彼此不相遇）以及混合项 \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}} = k_BT[N_B\ln(N_B/N) + N_A\ln(N_A/N)]\)。对 \(N_B \ll N_A\)，\(N \approx N_A\) 与 \(N_A\ln(N_A/N) \approx -N_B\)，故

\[ G \approx N_A\mu_0 + N_Bf - N_Bk_BT\ln N_A + N_Bk_BT\ln N_B - N_Bk_BT \]

Differentiating: \(\mu_A = \partial G/\partial N_A = \mu_0 - N_Bk_BT/N_A\) and \(\mu_B = \partial G/\partial N_B = f + k_BT\ln(N_B/N_A)\).求导：\(\mu_A = \partial G/\partial N_A = \mu_0 - N_Bk_BT/N_A\) 与 \(\mu_B = \partial G/\partial N_B = f + k_BT\ln(N_B/N_A)\)。

8.4  Colligative properties8.4 依数性

All four results below come from the three-step recipe in the chapter introduction. The pure solvent's own equilibrium point (its boiling point, freezing point, or vapour pressure) is the point we expand around.以下四个结论均来自章首的三步套路。纯溶剂自身的平衡点（其沸点、凝固点或蒸气压）即展开点。

Raoult's law: vapour pressure is lowered拉乌尔定律：蒸气压下降

Raoult's law

\[ P \approx P_0(1 - x) \]

\(P_0\) is the vapour pressure of the pure solvent. With solute dissolved, the solvent's \(\mu\) in the liquid is lower, so fewer molecules "want" to escape and the equilibrium vapour pressure drops by the fraction \(x\).\(P_0\) 为纯溶剂的蒸气压。溶有溶质时，液相中溶剂的 \(\mu\) 较低，故愿意逸出的分子更少，平衡蒸气压按比例 \(x\) 下降。

Proof证明

Step 2: the vapour is pure solvent, so \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P) = \mu_0(T,P) - xk_BT\). For pure solvent, \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P_0) = \mu_0(T,P_0)\).第 2 步：气相为纯溶剂，故 \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P) = \mu_0(T,P) - xk_BT\)。对纯溶剂，\(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P_0) = \mu_0(T,P_0)\)。

Step 3: expand both sides about \(P_0\) using \(\partial\mu/\partial P = v\): \(v_{\text{gas}}(P - P_0) = v_{\text{liq}}(P - P_0) - xk_BT\). Drop \(v_{\text{liq}} \ll v_{\text{gas}}\) and use \(v_{\text{gas}} = k_BT/P_0\): \(P - P_0 = -xP_0\).第 3 步：用 \(\partial\mu/\partial P = v\) 将两边在 \(P_0\) 附近展开：\(v_{\text{gas}}(P - P_0) = v_{\text{liq}}(P - P_0) - xk_BT\)。舍去 \(v_{\text{liq}} \ll v_{\text{gas}}\) 并使用 \(v_{\text{gas}} = k_BT/P_0\)：\(P - P_0 = -xP_0\)。

Boiling point goes up, freezing point goes down沸点升高，凝固点降低

Boiling-point elevation and freezing-point depression

\[ \Delta T_b \approx +\frac{x\,RT_b^2}{L_{\text{vap}}}, \qquad \Delta T_m \approx -\frac{x\,RT_m^2}{L_{\text{fus}}} \]

(\(L\) per mole; per molecule replace \(R\) by \(k_B\).) The solute stays in the liquid; the vapour and the ice are pure solvent. Dissolving lowers \(\mu\) of the liquid only. In the \(\mu(T)\) picture of §7.1, the liquid line moves down, so it crosses the gas line later (higher \(T_b\)) and the solid line earlier (lower \(T_m\)). The liquid range widens at both ends. This is why salted roads don't ice over and why antifreeze is added to car radiators.（每摩尔 \(L\)；按每分子计则将 \(R\) 换为 \(k_B\)。）溶质留在液体中；蒸气与冰均为纯溶剂。溶解只降低液相的 \(\mu\)。在 §7.1 的 \(\mu(T)\) 图中，液相线下移，故它与气相线相交更晚（沸点更高 \(T_b\)）、与固相线相交更早（凝固点更低 \(T_m\)）。液相存在的温度范围两端均变宽。这就是撒盐的道路不易结冰、汽车散热器中需加防冻液的原因。

Proof (boiling; freezing is identical)证明（沸腾；凝固完全相同）

At fixed \(P\), the solution boils where \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T) = \mu_0(T) - xk_BT\). Pure solvent: \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T_b) = \mu_0(T_b)\). Expand about \(T_b\) with \(\partial\mu/\partial T = -s\):在固定 \(P\) 下，溶液在 \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T) = \mu_0(T) - xk_BT\) 处沸腾。纯溶剂为 \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T_b) = \mu_0(T_b)\)。用 \(\partial\mu/\partial T = -s\) 在 \(T_b\) 附近展开：

\[ -s_{\text{gas}}\,\Delta T = -s_{\text{liq}}\,\Delta T - xk_BT \;\Rightarrow\; \Delta T = \frac{xk_BT_b}{s_{\text{gas}} - s_{\text{liq}}} = \frac{xk_BT_b^2}{L_{\text{vap}}} \]

For freezing, replace the gas by ice: \((s_{\text{ice}} - s_{\text{liq}})\Delta T = xk_BT\), and since \(s_{\text{ice}} \lt s_{\text{liq}}\) the shift is negative: \(\Delta T = -xk_BT_m^2/L_{\text{fus}}\).对凝固，将气相换为冰：\((s_{\text{ice}} - s_{\text{liq}})\Delta T = xk_BT\)，且由于 \(s_{\text{ice}} \lt s_{\text{liq}}\)，偏移为负：\(\Delta T = -xk_BT_m^2/L_{\text{fus}}\)。

Osmotic pressure渗透压

A semi-permeable membrane lets water through but not the solute. Put pure water on one side and solution on the other. Water flows into the solution (its \(\mu\) is lower there) until the solution side is at a higher pressure that stops the flow, e.g. a column of height \(h\) with \(\Pi = \rho gh\).半透膜允许水通过而不允许溶质通过。一侧为纯水，另一侧为溶液。水流入溶液（该处水的 \(\mu\) 较低），直到溶液侧压强足够高而阻止流动，例如高度为 \(h\) 的液柱，\(\Pi = \rho gh\)。

Osmotic pressure

\[ \Pi = P_2 - P_1 = \frac{N_Bk_BT}{V} = \frac{n_BRT}{V} \]

It has exactly the form of the ideal gas law: the dissolved particles push as if they were a gas filling the solution's volume.其形式与理想气体定律完全相同：溶解粒子施加的压强，如同它们是充满溶液体积的气体。

Proof证明

Water's \(\mu\) must match across the membrane: \(\mu_0(T,P_1) = \mu_0(T,P_2) - xk_BT\). Expand \(\mu_0(T,P_2) \approx \mu_0(T,P_1) + v_w(P_2 - P_1)\) with \(v_w\) the volume per water molecule: \(v_w(P_2 - P_1) = xk_BT = N_Bk_BT/N_A\). Since \(N_Av_w \approx V\), \(P_2 - P_1 = N_Bk_BT/V\).膜两侧水的 \(\mu\) 必须相等：\(\mu_0(T,P_1) = \mu_0(T,P_2) - xk_BT\)。用 \(v_w\)（每个水分子的体积）展开 \(\mu_0(T,P_2) \approx \mu_0(T,P_1) + v_w(P_2 - P_1)\)：\(v_w(P_2 - P_1) = xk_BT = N_Bk_BT/N_A\)。由于 \(N_Av_w \approx V\)，\(P_2 - P_1 = N_Bk_BT/V\)。

8.5  Binary mixtures: mix or separate?8.5 二元混合物：混合还是分离？

Now drop "dilute": mix two components A and B in any proportion, \(x = N_B/(N_A + N_B)\) from 0 (pure A) to 1 (pure B). Will they form one uniform mixture, or separate like oil and water? Compare Gibbs energies.现在去掉"稀"的假设：两种组分 A 与 B 以任意比例混合，\(x = N_B/(N_A + N_B)\) 从 0（纯 A）到 1（纯 B）。它们会形成均匀混合物，还是像油和水那样分离？比较吉布斯能量。

Unmixed, \(G\) is just a weighted average of the pure substances — a straight line in \(x\). Mixing ideal components adds only the mixing entropy:未混合时，\(G\) 只是纯物质的加权平均——在 \(x\) 中为一条直线。理想组分混合只增加混合熵：

Ideal mixture

\[ G = (1-x)G_A^0 + xG_B^0 + Nk_BT\left[(1-x)\ln(1-x) + x\ln x\right] \]

The bracket is negative and curves upward, so \(G(x)\) sags below the straight line everywhere: ideal components always mix, in any proportion. (In fact the slope of \(x\ln x\) is infinite at \(x \to 0\), so adding the first trace of B always lowers \(G\): no substance in contact with another stays perfectly pure.)方括号为负且向上弯曲，故 \(G(x)\) 处处位于直线之下：理想组分总是混合，任意比例皆然。（实际上 \(x\ln x\) 在 \(x \to 0\) 处的斜率为无穷大，故加入微量 B 总会使 \(G\) 降低：任何物质与另一物质接触都不会保持绝对纯净。）

When mixing costs energy当混合需要能量时

In real mixtures, unlike molecules may attract each other less than like ones (oil and water), so mixing raises the energy. Now two terms compete: the energy term makes \(G(x)\) bulge upward in the middle, while the entropy term pulls it down. At low temperature the energy wins and \(G(x)\) develops a stretch that curves the wrong way. A uniform mixture there is unstable and lowers its \(G\) by splitting into an A-rich phase and a B-rich phase.真实混合物中，异种分子之间的吸引可能弱于同种分子（油与水），故混合升高能量。此时两项竞争：能量项使 \(G(x)\) 在中部上凸，熵项将其向下拉。低温时能量项占优，\(G(x)\) 出现一段弯曲方向错误的区域。该处的均匀混合物不稳定，通过分裂为富 A 相与富 B 相来降低 \(G\)。

Compositions between \(x_a\) and \(x_b\) are a solubility gap: the two components are immiscible there. Raising \(T\) strengthens the entropy term, the gap narrows, and above a certain temperature it disappears. Plotting \(x_a\) and \(x_b\) against \(T\) gives a dome, with two phases inside and one uniform mixture outside, just like the liquid–gas dome in §7.4.\(x_a\) 与 \(x_b\) 之间的组分构成溶解度间隙：该区间内两种组分不互溶。升高 \(T\) 增强熵项，间隙变窄，高于某一温度时消失。将 \(x_a\) 与 \(x_b\) 对 \(T\) 作图得一个穹顶，内部为两相，外部为均匀混合物，与 §7.4 的液–气穹顶相同。

8.6  The common-tangent rule and the lever rule8.6 公切线法则与杠杆定则

Two practical tools for reading binary phase diagrams:读取二元相图的两个实用工具：

Common-tangent rule

Two phases \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) coexist at the compositions where a single straight line is tangent to both \(G(x)\) curves (or both parts of one curve).两个相 \(\alpha\) 与 \(\beta\) 在一条直线同时与两条 \(G(x)\) 曲线（或一条曲线的两段）相切的组分处共存。

Why: equilibrium needs \(\mu_A\) equal in both phases and \(\mu_B\) equal in both phases. The tangent line to \(g(x) = G/N\) at composition \(x\) hits the \(x = 0\) axis at \(\mu_A\) and the \(x = 1\) axis at \(\mu_B\). Equal \(\mu\)'s in two phases means equal intercepts, which means the same tangent line.原因：平衡要求两相中 \(\mu_A\) 相等、\(\mu_B\) 相等。组分 \(x\) 处 \(g(x) = G/N\) 的切线在 \(x = 0\) 轴上为 \(\mu_A\)，在 \(x = 1\) 轴上为 \(\mu_B\)。两相中 \(\mu\) 相等即截距相等，即同一条切线。

Proof: chemical potentials are tangent intercepts证明：化学势即切线截距

With \(G = Ng(x)\), \(N = N_A + N_B\), \(x = N_B/N\): \(\partial x/\partial N_A = -x/N\), \(\partial x/\partial N_B = (1-x)/N\). So有 \(G = Ng(x)\)、\(N = N_A + N_B\)、\(x = N_B/N\)：\(\partial x/\partial N_A = -x/N\)、\(\partial x/\partial N_B = (1-x)/N\)。故

\[ \mu_A = \frac{\partial G}{\partial N_A} = g - x\,g'(x), \qquad \mu_B = \frac{\partial G}{\partial N_B} = g + (1-x)\,g'(x) \]

The tangent at \(x\) is \(\ell(y) = g(x) + g'(x)(y - x)\); \(\ell(0) = \mu_A\) and \(\ell(1) = \mu_B\).在 \(x\) 处的切线为 \(\ell(y) = g(x) + g'(x)(y - x)\)；\(\ell(0) = \mu_A\) 与 \(\ell(1) = \mu_B\)。

Once the coexisting compositions \(x^\alpha\) and \(x^\beta\) are known, conservation of B fixes how much of each phase there is. If a fraction \(\mathcal{P}\) of the particles is in phase \(\beta\) and the overall composition is \(x_0\), then \((1-\mathcal{P})x^\alpha + \mathcal{P}x^\beta = x_0\):共存组分 \(x^\alpha\) 与 \(x^\beta\) 已知后，B 的守恒确定各相的量。若粒子中有比例 \(\mathcal{P}\) 在相 \(\beta\) 中，总组分为 \(x_0\)，则 \((1-\mathcal{P})x^\alpha + \mathcal{P}x^\beta = x_0\)：

Lever rule

\[ \mathcal{P}_\beta = \frac{x_0 - x^\alpha}{x^\beta - x^\alpha}, \qquad \mathcal{P}_\alpha = \frac{x^\beta - x_0}{x^\beta - x^\alpha} \]

On the diagram: the amount of each phase is proportional to the length of the segment on the opposite side of \(x_0\). If \(x_0\) is close to \(x^\alpha\), most of the system is phase \(\alpha\).图上：各相的量正比于 \(x_0\) 另一侧的线段长度。若 \(x_0\) 接近 \(x^\alpha\)，系统大部分为相 \(\alpha\)。

Building a phase diagram构建相图

Given \(G(x)\) curves for, say, the liquid and gas at many temperatures: wherever the curves cross, draw the common tangent and mark its two touching points; then plot those compositions against \(T\). For two liquids with boiling points \(T_A \lt T_B\) this gives a lens-shaped two-phase region between \(T_A\) and \(T_B\). The gas is always richer in the more volatile component (the lower boiling point).给定许多温度下例如液相与气相的 \(G(x)\) 曲线：在曲线相交处画公切线并标出两个切点；然后将这些组分对 \(T\) 作图。沸点为 \(T_A \lt T_B\) 的两种液体，给出 \(T_A\) 与 \(T_B\) 之间的透镜状两相区。气相总是更富含更易挥发（沸点更低）的组分。

Eutectic mixtures共晶混合物

Some pairs mix completely as liquids but form two different solid phases: \(\alpha\) (mostly A) and \(\beta\) (mostly B). On cooling, solid \(\alpha\) or \(\beta\) starts to separate out, and the melting curves fall from the pure melting points \(T_A\) and \(T_B\) towards each other. They meet at the eutectic point, the composition with the lowest melting temperature of all, lower than either pure substance. This is freezing-point depression pushed to large concentrations. Chapter 9

The Third Law of Thermodynamics热力学第三定律

What this chapter does

The First and Second Laws only ever talk about changes, \(\Delta U\) and \(\Delta S\). The Third Law is about the bottom of the temperature scale: it gives entropy an absolute zero, and it explains why 0 K can be approached but never reached. It is a short chapter that reuses tools from Chapters 4, 5 and 7.第一与第二定律只涉及变化，\(\Delta U\) 与 \(\Delta S\)。第三定律关乎温标的底端：它给熵一个绝对零点，并解释为何 0 K 可以逼近却永远无法达到。本章较短，复用第 4、5、7 章的工具。

After this chapter you should be able to
  • state the Third Law (Nernst and Planck forms) and explain it with \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\);陈述第三定律（能斯特与普朗克形式），并用 \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\) 解释；
  • compute an absolute entropy from heat capacities and latent heats;由热容与潜热计算绝对熵；
  • derive the consequences \(C \to 0\), \(\alpha \to 0\), flat phase boundaries, and the unattainability of 0 K.推出推论 \(C \to 0\)、\(\alpha \to 0\)、相界变平以及 0 K 不可达到。

9.1  Why worry about absolute zero?9.1 为何关注绝对零度？

A reversible refrigerator removing heat \(Q_C\) from a cold body at \(T_C\) and dumping it at \(T_H\) needs work (§3.9, §4.4)一台可逆制冷机从温度为 \(T_C\) 的冷物体中取出热 \(Q_C\)，并在 \(T_H\) 处排出，所需功为（§3.9、§4.4）

\[ W = Q_C\left(\frac{T_H}{T_C} - 1\right) \]

As \(T_C \to 0\) this blows up: extracting even a little heat from something near 0 K costs enormous work. So refrigeration alone cannot reach absolute zero. The Third Law turns this observation into a general principle (§9.4).当 \(T_C \to 0\) 时该式发散：从接近 0 K 的物体中取出哪怕少量热，也需巨大的功。故仅靠制冷无法达到绝对零度。第三定律将该观察提升为普遍原理（§9.4）。

9.2  Statements of the Third Law9.2 第三定律的表述

Nernst statement

For any isothermal process between equilibrium (condensed) states, \(\displaystyle\lim_{T\to0}\Delta S = 0\).对任意在两个平衡（凝聚）态之间的等温过程，\(\displaystyle\lim_{T\to0}\Delta S = 0\)。

Planck statement

The entropy of a perfect crystal is zero at absolute zero: \(S(T = 0) = 0\).完美晶体在绝对零度时熵为零：\(S(T = 0) = 0\)。

Nernst says all entropy differences vanish at 0 K, so every state of a substance approaches the same entropy there. Planck sets that common value to zero. Unlike energy, whose zero is a convention, entropy then has an absolute value.能斯特指出 0 K 时所有熵差消失，故物质的每个状态在该处趋于同一个熵。普朗克将该共同值取为零。与能量不同——能量的零点只是一种约定——熵因此具有绝对值。

The microscopic reason is \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\) (§4.11). At \(T = 0\) a perfect crystal sits in its single lowest-energy arrangement: \(\Omega = 1\), so \(S = 0\). There is no remaining uncertainty about the microstate.微观原因在于 \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\)（§4.11）。在 \(T = 0\)，完美晶体处于其唯一的能量最低排列：\(\Omega = 1\)，故 \(S = 0\)。微观态不存在任何不确定性。

Residual entropy. If the lowest-energy state is not unique, \(\Omega_0 \gt 1\) and \(S(0) = k_B\ln\Omega_0 \gt 0\). In a crystal of CO, each molecule can point either way (CO or OC) at almost the same energy. If the orientations freeze in at random, \(\Omega_0 = 2^N\), and the crystal keeps \(Nk_B\ln 2\), i.e. \(R\ln 2\) per mole.
How Nernst's statement shows up in chemistry data能斯特表述如何体现在化学数据中

For an isothermal reaction, \(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\), so \(\Delta S = (\Delta H - \Delta G)/T\). At \(T = 0\), \(\Delta G = \Delta H\), and the limit is \(0/0\). By L'Hôpital's rule, \(\lim\Delta S = (\partial\Delta H/\partial T)_0 - (\partial\Delta G/\partial T)_0\). Measured \(\Delta G(T)\) and \(\Delta H(T)\) curves meet at \(T = 0\) and both become flat there: \(\partial\Delta G/\partial T = -\Delta S \to 0\) and \(\partial\Delta H/\partial T = \Delta C_P \to 0\).对等温反应，\(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\)，故 \(\Delta S = (\Delta H - \Delta G)/T\)。在 \(T = 0\) 处，\(\Delta G = \Delta H\)，极限为 \(0/0\) 型。由洛必达法则，\(\lim\Delta S = (\partial\Delta H/\partial T)_0 - (\partial\Delta G/\partial T)_0\)。实测的 \(\Delta G(T)\) 与 \(\Delta H(T)\) 曲线在 \(T = 0\) 处相交，且二者在该处均变平：\(\partial\Delta G/\partial T = -\Delta S \to 0\) 与 \(\partial\Delta H/\partial T = \Delta C_P \to 0\)。

9.3  Absolute entropy9.3 绝对熵

With \(S(0) = 0\) as the reference, the entropy at any temperature is obtained by adding up \(\text{đ}Q/T\) for all the heat needed to bring the substance up from 0 K:以 \(S(0) = 0\) 为参考，任何温度下的熵通过累加将物质从 0 K 升至该温度所需全部热的 \(\text{đ}Q/T\) 得到：

Absolute (Third-Law) entropy

\[ S(T) = \int_0^T\frac{C(T')}{T'}\,dT' + \sum_{\text{transitions}}\frac{\Delta H_{\text{trans}}}{T_{\text{trans}}} \]

The integral uses each phase's own heat capacity; each melting or boiling on the way adds its latent heat divided by its temperature. Tabulated entropies (like the diamond and graphite values in Chapter 7) are made this way. In problems, though, you usually need entropy changes, and the same two pieces (\(\int C\,dT/T\) and \(L/T\)) are what you add up.积分时使用各相自身的热容；途中每次熔化或沸腾加上其潜热除以温度。表格熵（如第 7 章金刚石与石墨的值）即由此得到。不过解题时通常需要的是熵变，而累加的仍是那两项（\(\int C\,dT/T\) 与 \(L/T\)）。

Example · An ice cube melting in the kitchen (course example)

A 30 g ice cube at 0 °C melts on a table in a 25 °C kitchen and warms to 25 °C. Find the entropy change of (a) the ice/water, (b) the kitchen, (c) the universe. (\(L = 333\ \text{J/g}\), \(c = 4.186\ \text{J/(g·K)}\).)一块 30 g、0 °C 的冰在 25 °C 厨房的桌上融化并升温至 25 °C。求 (a) 冰/水、(b) 厨房、(c) 宇宙的熵变。（\(L = 333\ \text{J/g}\)、\(c = 4.186\ \text{J/(g·K)}\)。）

Show solution显示解答

(a) Melting at 273 K: \(\Delta S_1 = mL/T = (30)(333)/273 = 36.6\ \text{J/K}\). Warming 273 → 298 K: \(\Delta S_2 = mc\ln(T_f/T_i) = (30)(4.186)\ln(298/273) = 11.0\ \text{J/K}\). Total \(47.6\ \text{J/K}\).(a) 273 K 下融化：\(\Delta S_1 = mL/T = (30)(333)/273 = 36.6\ \text{J/K}\)。273 → 298 K 升温：\(\Delta S_2 = mc\ln(T_f/T_i) = (30)(4.186)\ln(298/273) = 11.0\ \text{J/K}\)。总计 \(47.6\ \text{J/K}\)。

(b) The kitchen is a reservoir at 298 K giving up all that heat: \(Q = (30)(333) + (30)(4.186)(25) = 13\,130\ \text{J}\), so \(\Delta S = -13\,130/298 = -44.1\ \text{J/K}\).(b) 厨房是 298 K 的热库，放出全部该热量：\(Q = (30)(333) + (30)(4.186)(25) = 13\,130\ \text{J}\)，故 \(\Delta S = -13\,130/298 = -44.1\ \text{J/K}\)。

(c) \(47.6 - 44.1 = +3.5\ \text{J/K} \gt 0\): irreversible, as heat flowed across a finite temperature difference. The ice gains more entropy than the kitchen loses because it receives the heat at a lower temperature, and \(\Delta S = \int\text{đ}Q/T\).(c) \(47.6 - 44.1 = +3.5\ \text{J/K} \gt 0\)：不可逆，因为热跨越有限温差。冰获得的熵多于厨房失去的，因为它是在更低温度下"接收"热量，且 \(\Delta S = \int\text{đ}Q/T\)。

9.4  Consequences of the Third Law9.4 第三定律的推论

Each consequence below uses one tool from earlier chapters together with "\(\Delta S \to 0\) as \(T \to 0\)".以下每条推论均使用前面章节的一个工具，并配上"\(\Delta S \to 0\) 当 \(T \to 0\)"。

(i) Heat capacities vanish(i) 热容趋于零

As \(T \to 0\)

\[ C_P \to 0, \qquad C_V \to 0 \]

Reason: \(S(T) = \int_0^T C\,dT'/T'\) must be finite. If \(C\) stayed at a nonzero value, \(\int dT'/T'\) would diverge logarithmically. So the classical ideal gas, with constant \(C_V = \tfrac32Nk_B\), must fail at very low temperature; real matter is quantum there, and its heat capacity falls to zero (for example as \(T^3\) for vibrations in a solid).理由：\(S(T) = \int_0^T C\,dT'/T'\) 必须有限。若 \(C\) 保持非零值，\(\int dT'/T'\) 将对数发散。故热容为常数 \(C_V = \tfrac32Nk_B\) 的经典理想气体在极低温必然失效；真实物质在低温下是量子的，其热容趋于零（例如固体振动按 \(T^3\) 下降）。

(ii) Thermal expansion vanishes(ii) 热膨胀趋于零

As \(T \to 0\)

\[ \alpha = \frac{1}{V}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} \to 0 \]

Reason: by Nernst, compressing isothermally at \(T \to 0\) changes no entropy, \((\partial S/\partial P)_T \to 0\). The Maxwell relation \((\partial S/\partial P)_T = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P\) (§5.4) then gives \(V\alpha \to 0\). Likewise \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = (\partial S/\partial V)_T \to 0\).理由：由能斯特，在 \(T \to 0\) 下等温压缩不改变熵，\((\partial S/\partial P)_T \to 0\)。麦克斯韦关系 \((\partial S/\partial P)_T = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P\)（§5.4）随后给出 \(V\alpha \to 0\)。同理 \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = (\partial S/\partial V)_T \to 0\)。

(iii) Phase boundaries become flat(iii) 相界变平

As \(T \to 0\)

\[ \frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta v} \to 0 \]

Reason: Clausius–Clapeyron (§7.3) with \(\Delta s \to 0\) and \(\Delta v\) finite. Any coexistence line that reaches 0 K must meet the axis horizontally, as the melting curve of helium does.理由：克劳修斯–克拉珀龙方程（§7.3），其中 \(\Delta s \to 0\) 与 \(\Delta v\) 均有限。任何延伸至 0 K 的共存线必须以水平方式与轴相接，如氦的熔化曲线所示。

(iv) Absolute zero cannot be reached(iv) 绝对零度不可达到

Cooling is done in alternating steps between two values of a control parameter (volume, pressure, or magnetic field as in adiabatic demagnetisation, §5.6). An isothermal step (in contact with the coldest reservoir available) lowers \(S\) at fixed \(T\); an isentropic step (isolated) then lowers \(T\) at fixed \(S\). The final step can't be isothermal, since that would need a reservoir already at 0 K.制冷在两个控制参数（体积、压强或磁场，如绝热去磁，§5.6）取值之间交替进行。等温步骤（与可用的最冷热库接触）在固定 \(T\) 下降低 \(S\)；随后绝热步骤（孤立）在固定 \(S\) 下降低 \(T\)。最后一步不可能是等温的，因为那需要一个已处于 0 K 的热库。

Because \(\Delta S \to 0\), the two curves meet at \(T = 0\). Each vertical drop gets smaller, and so does each move to the left. (In the figure each cycle exactly halves \(T\).) If the curves did not meet, a single isentropic step from a low enough temperature could land exactly on 0 K.因为 \(\Delta S \to 0\)，两条曲线在 \(T = 0\) 处相交。每次垂直下降的幅度变小，每次向左移动的幅度也变小。（图中每个循环恰使 \(T\) 减半。）若两条曲线不相交，从足够低的温度出发，一次绝热步骤即可恰好落在 0 K。

Unattainability statement

Absolute zero cannot be reached by any process in a finite number of steps or in a finite time.任何过程都无法在有限步数或有限时间内达到绝对零度。
