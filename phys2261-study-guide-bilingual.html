Chapter 1

What this chapter does

Thermodynamics describes huge numbers of particles with a handful of numbers (\(P\), \(V\), \(T\), \(U\), …). This chapter sets up the vocabulary for that: what a "system" is, what kinds of changes it can undergo, which quantities describe it, and, most importantly, why temperature is a well-defined thing you can measure at all.热力学厉害的地方在于：面对一大坨粒子，它只用几个数（\(P\)、\(V\)、\(T\)、\(U\)……）就能把话说清楚。这一章先把词汇备好：什么叫"系统"，它能怎么变，用哪些量描述它，还有最关键的一点——温度为啥是个货真价实、能测的量。

After this chapter you should be able to

  • tell extensive from intensive variables;一眼看出广延量和强度量；
  • explain what the Zeroth Law guarantees and how the Kelvin and ideal-gas temperature scales are built.讲明白第零定律保证了什么，以及开尔文温标和理想气体温标是怎么搭起来的。

1.1  Systems and their walls1.1 系统，还有隔在中间的"墙"

A thermodynamic system is whatever piece of the world we choose to study: a gas in a cylinder, a cup of coffee, a star. Everything else is the surroundings. The boundary between them is called a wall, and what matters about a wall is what it lets through.热力学系统，说白了就是你挑出来研究的那一块世界：气缸里的气体、一杯咖啡、一颗恒星。其它统统叫环境。两者之间的分界叫"墙"，这堵墙的本事就在于——它到底放什么过去。

1.2  Equilibrium and kinds of processes1.2 平衡，以及过程有哪些花样

A system is in (thermal) equilibrium when its macroscopic properties (pressure, temperature, density, …) stay constant in time and nothing flows inside it or across its walls. Only then are quantities like "the pressure" or "the temperature" of the system well defined. A process takes the system from one equilibrium state to another, and processes come in three kinds:什么时候算（热）平衡？系统的宏观性质（压强、温度、密度……）不随时间变，里面没有净流动，也不跟墙外换东西。只有这时候，"这个系统的压强""这个系统的温度"才说得通。过程就是把系统从一个平衡态搬到另一个平衡态，常见的有三类：

Spontaneous自发过程
Happens by itself once a constraint is removed, e.g. gas rushing into a vacuum when a partition breaks. In between, the system is out of equilibrium, so \(P\) and \(T\) may not even be defined.约束一撤就自己发生，比如隔板一破，气体"呼"地冲进真空。这中间系统根本不在平衡，所以 \(P\) 和 \(T\) 很可能压根没有定义。
Quasi-static准静态过程
Carried out so slowly that the system is essentially in equilibrium at every step. Only these can be drawn as curves on a \(P\)–\(V\) diagram.慢到每一步系统都基本处于平衡。只有这种过程才能在 \(P\)–\(V\) 图上画成一条曲线。
Reversible可逆过程
Quasi-static and with no dissipation (no friction, no heat flowing across a finite temperature gap). It can be run backwards so that the system and its surroundings both return exactly to where they started. An idealisation, like a frictionless pulley.既准静态、又不耗散（没摩擦、没热流过有限温差）。它能原路倒回去，系统和环境都精确回到出发点。这是理想化，跟"无摩擦滑轮"一个性质。
Why the distinction matters

The formula for work \(W = -\int P\,dV\) (Chapter 3) only applies to quasi-static processes, because otherwise \(P\) isn't defined. The formula for entropy \(\Delta S = \int \text{đ}Q/T\) (Chapter 4) only applies along reversible paths. Many exam questions turn on noticing that a process is not quasi-static (sudden expansion, a released piston, a weight dropped on a piston).功的公式 \(W = -\int P\,dV\)（第 3 章）只对准静态过程成立，不然 \(P\) 根本没定义。熵的公式 \(\Delta S = \int \text{đ}Q/T\)（第 4 章）只沿可逆路径走。很多考题的坑就在这：一眼看出它不是准静态（突然膨胀、活塞一松、重物砸下来）。

State variables describe an equilibrium state. A quick test sorts them into two kinds: cut the system in half and see what happens to the variable.状态参量描述的是平衡态。想快速分类？把系统劈成两半，看这个量怎么变就行。

A minimal set of variables that fixes the equilibrium state completely is called the set of thermodynamic coordinates. All other state variables are functions of them. The coordinates are taken to be extensive: the internal energy \(U\) plus "work coordinates" such as \(V\) (or a magnetisation \(M\) for a magnet, an area for a film).能完全钉死平衡态的一组最少变量，叫热力学坐标。其它状态参量都是它们的函数。这组坐标一般取广延量：内能 \(U\)，再加"功坐标"比如 \(V\)（磁体就是磁化强度 \(M\)，薄膜就是面积）。

Why not just use \(T\) and \(P\)? Take three containers of water at 0 °C and 1 atm: all ice, half ice and half water, all water. Same \(T\), same \(P\), but clearly different states with different internal energies. \(T\) and \(P\) can't tell them apart; an extensive variable like \(U\) can.

1.4  The Zeroth Law: why temperature exists1.4 第零定律：温度凭什么存在

The Zeroth Law

If A is in thermal equilibrium with C, and B is in thermal equilibrium with C, then A is in thermal equilibrium with B.A 跟 C 热平衡，B 也跟 C 热平衡，那 A 和 B 就热平衡。

It sounds obvious, but it is exactly what makes a thermometer work. C is the thermometer: if it reads the same in A and in B, we can conclude that A and B would be in equilibrium with each other without ever putting them in contact. Mathematically, the Zeroth Law guarantees that every system has a function of its own coordinates, \(\Theta\), that takes the same value for all systems in mutual equilibrium:听着像废话，但温度计能干活的全部秘密就在这。C 就是温度计：它在 A 里读数和在 B 里一样，我们就能断定 A、B 之间也平衡，压根不用让它们碰到一起。数学上，第零定律保证每个系统都有个"自家坐标的函数"\(\Theta\)，所有互相平衡的系统这个值都一样：

Consequence

\[ \Theta_A(A_1, A_2, \ldots) = \Theta_B(B_1, B_2, \ldots) \quad\text{whenever A and B are in equilibrium} \]

That function is the temperature. The Zeroth Law tells us it exists; it does not yet tell us what numbers to assign. That is the job of a temperature scale.这个函数就是温度。第零定律只保证它存在，还没说读数该怎么定——那是温标的活儿。

Proof: from the Zeroth Law to a temperature function证明：从第零定律到温度函数

Equilibrium between A and C imposes one condition linking their coordinates, \(f_{AC}(A_1, A_2, \ldots; C_1, C_2, \ldots) = 0\); similarly \(f_{BC}(B_1, \ldots; C_1, \ldots) = 0\). Solve both for \(C_1\):A 和 C 平衡，等于给它们的坐标加了一个约束 \(f_{AC}(A_1, A_2, \ldots; C_1, C_2, \ldots) = 0\)；B 和 C 也一样 \(f_{BC}(B_1, \ldots; C_1, \ldots) = 0\)。两个式子都解出 \(C_1\)：

\[ C_1 = F_{AC}(A_1, A_2, \ldots; C_2, \ldots) = F_{BC}(B_1, B_2, \ldots; C_2, \ldots) \]

The Zeroth Law says A and B are then also in equilibrium, \(f_{AB}(A_1, \ldots; B_1, \ldots) = 0\), a condition with no C-coordinates in it. So the C-coordinates must cancel out of the equation above, leaving \(\Theta_A(A_1, A_2, \ldots) = \Theta_B(B_1, B_2, \ldots)\).第零定律说 A、B 也平衡，即 \(f_{AB}(A_1, \ldots; B_1, \ldots) = 0\)，而这个式子里根本没有 C 的坐标。所以上面的式子必须能把 C 的坐标消干净，剩下 \(\Theta_A(A_1, A_2, \ldots) = \Theta_B(B_1, B_2, \ldots)\)。

1.5  Temperature scales1.5 温标那点事

To put numbers on temperature, pick a thermometric property \(X\) that changes with hotness (the length of a mercury column, the pressure of a gas at fixed volume, a wire's resistance) and assume a linear rule \(\Theta = aX + b\), with \(a\), \(b\) fixed by reference points.要给温度标上数字，先挑一个会随冷热变化的"测温性质" \(X\)（水银柱长度、定容气体压强、电阻丝电阻），再假定它是线性的 \(\Theta = aX + b\)，其中 \(a\)、\(b\) 由参考点定下来。

Celsius, absolute zero, Kelvin摄氏、绝对零度、开尔文

Celsius uses two reference points, ice melting (0 °C) and water boiling at 1 atm (100 °C), and interpolates linearly between them. Now measure the volume of a dilute gas at fixed pressure at several temperatures and extrapolate the straight line to \(V = 0\). Whatever the gas and whatever the pressure, the line hits zero at \(-273.15\ \text{°C}\). Since a volume can't be negative, nothing can be colder: that is absolute zero. The Kelvin scale keeps the Celsius degree size but starts there:摄氏温标用两个参考点：冰水混合（0 °C）和 1 atm 下水的沸点（100 °C），中间线性插值。接着在几个温度下量一下定压稀薄气体的体积，把直线外推看看它在哪儿归零：\(V = 0\)。不管什么气体、什么压强，直线都在 \(-273.15\ \text{°C}\) 处碰零。体积不可能为负，那自然就没有比这更冷的了——这就是绝对零度。开尔文温标沿用了摄氏度"一度"的大小，只是把零点挪到了那儿：

Conversions

\[ T\ (\text{K}) = \theta\ (\text{°C}) + 273.15, \qquad \theta\ (\text{°C}) = \tfrac{5}{9}\,(\theta_F - 32) \]

Always use kelvin in formulas. Ratios like \(T_2/T_1\), \(\ln(T_2/T_1)\) or \(1 - T_C/T_H\) are meaningless in Celsius. Temperature differences are the same in both.

The ideal-gas temperature scale理想气体温标

Different thermometers (different substances, different amounts of gas) disagree slightly with each other. But as a gas thermometer is made more and more dilute, all gases converge on the same reading. That limit defines a universal scale, anchored at the triple point of water (273.16 K, where ice, water and vapour coexist):不同温度计（不同物质、不同气体量）读数会有点出入。但气体越稀薄，各种气体的读数就越往一块儿靠。取这个极限，就得到一个普适温标，锚点用水三相点（273.16 K，冰、水、蒸汽共存）：

Ideal-gas temperature (constant-volume gas thermometer)

\[ T = 273.16\ \text{K}\times\lim_{P_{\text{tr}}\to0}\frac{P}{P_{\text{tr}}} \]

\(P_{\text{tr}}\) is the gas pressure when the bulb sits at the triple point; \(P\) is the pressure at the unknown temperature. Chapter 4 shows that this scale coincides with the "thermodynamic" temperature defined by heat engines, so it is not tied to any particular substance.\(P_{\text{tr}}\) 是球泡放在三相点时的压强；\(P\) 是待测温度下的压强。第 4 章会证明，这个温标跟热机定义出来的"热力学温度"是一回事，所以它不绑死在任何一种物质上。

2.1  The ideal gas law2.1 理想气体定律

An equation of state is a relation \(f(P, V, T) = 0\) among the state variables of a substance in equilibrium. Thermodynamics does not predict it; it comes from experiment or from a microscopic model. The simplest, and the limit every real gas approaches at low density, is状态方程，就是平衡态下物质各状态参量之间的关系 \(f(P, V, T) = 0\)。热力学本身推不出它，得靠实验或微观模型。最简单、也是所有实际气体在低密度下都会趋近的那个，是

Ideal gas law

\[ PV = nRT = Nk_BT, \qquad R = 8.314\ \text{J/(mol·K)}, \quad k_B = R/N_A = 1.381\times10^{-23}\ \text{J/K} \]

It is really the ideal-gas temperature scale of §1.5 rewritten: that scale is defined so that \(PV/n\) of a dilute gas is proportional to \(T\), and \(R\) is the proportionality constant. Useful forms: density \(\rho = PM/(RT)\) with \(M\) the molar mass; and \(1\ \text{atm·L} = 101.3\ \text{J}\).其实就是把 §1.5 的理想气体温标换个写法：那个温标的定义就是让稀薄气体的 \(PV/n\) 正比于 \(T\)，\(R\) 是比例系数。几个好用的变形：密度 \(\rho = PM/(RT)\)（\(M\) 是摩尔质量），还有 \(1\ \text{atm·L} = 101.3\ \text{J}\)。

2.2  Real gases2.2 实际气体没那么听话

Real molecules have a size and attract each other, so at high density (high pressure, low temperature) the ideal gas law fails. Two standard corrections:真实分子有体积、还互相吸引，所以一到高密度（高压、低温），理想气体定律就开始不灵了。两项经典修正：

Van der Waals equation

\[ \left(P + \frac{an^2}{V^2}\right)(V - nb) = nRT \]

  • \(-nb\): molecules take up space. The volume available to move in is less than the container volume.\(-nb\)：分子自己占地方，能自由活动的体积比容器小。
  • \(+an^2/V^2\): molecules attract. A molecule about to hit the wall is pulled back by its neighbours, so it hits more softly and the measured \(P\) is lower than it would otherwise be. The effect grows with the square of the density.\(+an^2/V^2\)：分子互相吸引。快要撞墙的分子会被旁边的伙伴拽回来，撞得就没那么狠，测到的 \(P\) 偏小。这个效应跟密度的平方成正比。
Virial expansion (a systematic series in density) \[ PV = nRT\left(1 + \frac{B(T)}{V/n} + \frac{C(T)}{(V/n)^2} + \cdots\right) \]

At low density every correction vanishes and \(PV = nRT\) returns.密度一低，两项修正都没了，\(PV = nRT\) 又回来了。

Where it's used later

The van der Waals gas is the course's standard "non-ideal" example: its entropy (Chapter 4), why it cools in a free expansion (Chapter 5), and how it produces the liquid–gas transition and a critical point (Chapter 7).范德瓦尔斯气体是这门课标准的"非理想"代表：它的熵（第 4 章）、为什么自由膨胀会变冷（第 5 章）、以及液–气相变和临界点怎么来的（第 7 章），全拿它当例子。

2.3  Kinetic theory: pressure from molecules2.3 分子动理论：压强其实是分子撞出来的

Model the gas as many tiny hard spheres in random motion, colliding elastically with each other and the walls, with no forces between collisions and no preferred direction. Pressure is then just the momentum delivered to the walls per unit area per unit time. Working this out gives把气体看成一大堆乱飞的小硬球，彼此、跟器壁都弹性碰撞，除了碰撞没别的力，也没有哪个方向更特殊。这么一来，压强就是"单位时间、单位面积上，分子揍墙揍出的动量"。算一算就得到

Pressure from molecular motion

\[ PV = \tfrac{1}{3}Nm\langle v^2\rangle = \tfrac{2}{3}K, \qquad K = N\cdot\tfrac12 m\langle v^2\rangle \]

The macroscopic pressure is set by the total translational kinetic energy \(K\) of the molecules.宏观的压强，完全由分子总平动动能 \(K\) 决定。

Proof: momentum transfer to a wall证明：撞墙时的动量传递

A molecule with \(x\)-velocity \(v_x\) bounces off the right wall and reverses, transferring momentum \(2mv_x\). In time \(dt\), the molecules that reach a wall of area \(A\) are those within distance \(v_x\,dt\) moving towards it: \(\tfrac12(N/V)Av_x\,dt\) of them (the \(\tfrac12\) because half move the other way). Force = momentum per time:一个 \(x\) 方向速度为 \(v_x\) 的分子，撞上右壁后原速弹回，动量变了 \(2mv_x\)。在 \(dt\) 这么长的时间里，能撞到面积 \(A\) 的墙上的，就是那些离墙 \(v_x\,dt\) 以内、朝它飞的分子，一共 \(\tfrac12(N/V)Av_x\,dt\) 个（\(\tfrac12\) 是因为有一半在朝反方向飞）。力 = 单位时间的动量：

\[ F = \tfrac12\frac{N}{V}Av_x\cdot 2mv_x \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; P = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{N}{V}m\langle v_x^2\rangle \]

No direction is special, so \(\langle v_x^2\rangle = \tfrac13\langle v^2\rangle\), giving \(PV = \tfrac13 Nm\langle v^2\rangle\).哪个方向都不特殊，所以 \(\langle v_x^2\rangle = \tfrac13\langle v^2\rangle\)，代入得 \(PV = \tfrac13 Nm\langle v^2\rangle\)。

2.4  Temperature as kinetic energy: equipartition2.4 温度就是动能：能量均分

Put \(PV = \tfrac23 K\) next to \(PV = Nk_BT\): they agree only if \(K = \tfrac32 Nk_BT\). So temperature measures the average kinetic energy per molecule, \(\tfrac12m\langle v^2\rangle = \tfrac32k_BT\), shared equally among the three directions of motion. The general rule:把 \(PV = \tfrac23 K\) 和 \(PV = Nk_BT\) 摆一块儿，会发现只有 \(K = \tfrac32 Nk_BT\) 时两者才对得上。所以温度量的就是每个分子的平均动能 \(\tfrac12m\langle v^2\rangle = \tfrac32k_BT\)，而且它在三个方向上平分。推广成一条规则：

Equipartition theorem

Every independent quadratic term in a molecule's energy (\(\tfrac12mv_x^2\), \(\tfrac12I\omega^2\), \(\tfrac12kx^2\), …) carries on average \(\tfrac12k_BT\). With \(f\) such terms:分子能量里每一个独立的二次项（\(\tfrac12mv_x^2\)、\(\tfrac12I\omega^2\)、\(\tfrac12kx^2\)……）平均都摊到 \(\tfrac12k_BT\)。有 \(f\) 个这样的项，就乘上去：

\[ U = \frac{f}{2}Nk_BT = \frac{f}{2}nRT \]

2.5  The Maxwell speed distribution2.5 麦克斯韦速率分布

Molecules don't all move at \(v_{\text{rms}}\); their speeds spread out according to分子可不是都以 \(v_{\text{rms}}\) 在跑；它们的速率是这么铺开的：

Maxwell speed distribution

\[ f(v) = 4\pi\left(\frac{m}{2\pi k_BT}\right)^{3/2}v^2\,e^{-mv^2/2k_BT}, \qquad \int_0^\infty f(v)\,dv = 1 \]

\(f(v)\,dv\) is the fraction of molecules with speeds between \(v\) and \(v + dv\). 3.1  Internal energy and the First Law3.1 内能与第一定律

Experiments show that if you take a thermally insulated system from state 1 to state 2, the work needed is always the same, whether you stir it, compress it or pass a current through it. A quantity whose change depends only on the end states is a state function; this one is the internal energy \(U\). If heat can also flow, the same change of state can be produced by any mix of work and heat:实验发现，把一个绝热系统从状态 1 推到状态 2，不管你用的是搅拌、压缩还是通电，需要做的功总是一模一样。这种"变化只跟初末有关"的量叫状态函数，这里这个就叫内能 \(U\)。要是还能传热，同一个状态变化就可以用"功 + 热"的任意搭配来完成：

First Law of Thermodynamics

\[ \Delta U = W + Q, \qquad dU = \text{đ}W + \text{đ}Q \]

\(W\) = work done on the system, \(Q\) = heat put into the system.\(W\) = 外界对系统做的功，\(Q\) = 送进系统的热。

Sign conventions. This course uses "work done on the system". Some textbooks use work done by the system and write \(\Delta U = Q - W\). Before plugging into any formula, decide whose work it is. In engines we usually quote the work the engine delivers, which is \(-W\).

\(U\) is a state function; \(W\) and \(Q\) are not. The same \(\Delta U\) can be reached with lots of work and little heat or the other way round, which is why small amounts of them are written \(\text{đ}W\), \(\text{đ}Q\).\(U\) 是状态函数；\(W\) 和 \(Q\) 不是。同一个 \(\Delta U\) 可以靠"多功少热"或"少功多热"达到，所以它们的微小量要写成 \(\text{đ}W\)、\(\text{đ}Q\)，而不是 d。

3.2  Work done on a gas3.2 对气体做的功

Push a piston of area \(A\) in by \(dx\) against a gas at pressure \(P\): the work done on the gas is \(PA\,dx = -P\,dV\) (volume decreases, \(dV \lt 0\), work done on the gas is positive).把面积 \(A\) 的活塞往里推 \(dx\)，顶着压强 \(P\) 的气体：外界对气体做的功是 \(PA\,dx = -P\,dV\)（体积变小，\(dV \lt 0\)，对气体做功为正）。

Quasi-static work

\[ \text{đ}W = -P\,dV, \qquad W = -\int_{V_1}^{V_2}P\,dV \]

On a \(P\)–\(V\) diagram, \(|W|\) is the area under the path. Different paths between the same two states enclose different areas, which is another way of seeing that \(W\) depends on the path.在 \(P\)–\(V\) 图上，\(|W|\) 就是路径下面的面积。同样两个状态，走不同路径围出来的面积不一样——这就是"\(W\) 跟路径有关"的另一种说法。

Sudden processes. If the gas is not in equilibrium (a weight dropped onto a piston, a piston released), its own pressure isn't defined during the process and \(-\int P\,dV\) can't be used. But if the external pressure is constant, the work done on the gas is simply突变过程。要是气体压根不在平衡（重物砸在活塞上、活塞一松），过程中它自己的压强根本没定义，\(-\int P\,dV\) 用不了。但外压恒定时，对气体做的功就简单是

\[ W = -P_{\text{ext}}\,\Delta V \]

3.3  Heat capacity and enthalpy3.3 热容与焓

How much heat it takes to warm something by 1 K depends on what else is happening, so there are two heat capacities:要让某样东西升 1 K，需要多少热？这得看同时还在发生什么，于是有两种热容：

  • Constant volume: no work is done, so all the heat goes into \(U\): \(C_V = (\partial U/\partial T)_V\).定容：不做功，所以热全进了 \(U\)：\(C_V = (\partial U/\partial T)_V\)。
  • Constant pressure: the system expands and does work on the surroundings, so more heat is needed. Write \(U(T,V)\): \(dU = C_V\,dT + (\partial U/\partial V)_T\,dV\). Then \(\text{đ}Q = dU + P\,dV = C_V\,dT + [(\partial U/\partial V)_T + P]\,dV\). Divide by \(dT\) at constant \(P\):写出 \(U(T,V)\)：\(dU = C_V\,dT + (\partial U/\partial V)_T\,dV\)。于是 \(\text{đ}Q = dU + P\,dV = C_V\,dT + [(\partial U/\partial V)_T + P]\,dV\)。定 \(P\) 下除以 \(dT\)：

\[ C_P - C_V = \left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P}\left[\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T} + P\right] \]

For an ideal gas \((\partial U/\partial V)_T = 0\) and \((\partial V/\partial T)_P = nR/P\), giving \(nR\). 3.5  Adiabatic processes3.5 绝热过程

In a quasi-static adiabatic process (\(\text{đ}Q = 0\)), the work done on the gas all goes into \(U\): \(C_V\,dT = -P\,dV\). Compress a gas without letting heat out and it heats up; let it expand and it cools. For an ideal gas this integrates to:准静态绝热过程里（\(\text{đ}Q = 0\)），对气体做的功全变成了 \(U\)：\(C_V\,dT = -P\,dV\)。所以不散热地压它，它就变热；让它自己膨胀，它就变冷。对理想气体，积分一下就是：

Adiabatic relations (ideal gas, quasi-static)

\[ PV^\gamma = \text{const}, \qquad TV^{\gamma-1} = \text{const}, \qquad TP^{(1-\gamma)/\gamma} = \text{const} \]

Useful forms: \(T_2 = T_1(V_1/V_2)^{\gamma-1}\) and \(T_2 = T_1(P_2/P_1)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}\). On a \(P\)–\(V\) diagram an adiabat is steeper than an isotherm (slope \(-\gamma P/V\) vs \(-P/V\)): during compression the temperature rises too, adding to the pressure rise. Equivalently, a gas is harder to compress adiabatically:常用变形：\(T_2 = T_1(V_1/V_2)^{\gamma-1}\) 和 \(T_2 = T_1(P_2/P_1)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}\)。在 \(P\)–\(V\) 图上，绝热线比等温线更陡（斜率 \(-\gamma P/V\) 对 \(-P/V\)）：因为压缩时温度也在升，压强就涨得更狠。换句话说，气体更"抗拒"绝热压缩：

\[ \frac{\kappa_T}{\kappa_S} = \gamma \gt 1, \qquad \kappa_S = -\frac1V\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial P}\right)_{\!S} \]

\(C_V\,dT = -P\,dV\). From \(PV = nRT\), \(dT = (P\,dV + V\,dP)/nR\). Substitute and use \(nR = C_P - C_V\):\(C_V\,dT = -P\,dV\)。由 \(PV = nRT\) 得 \(dT = (P\,dV + V\,dP)/nR\)。代进去，再用 \(nR = C_P - C_V\)：

\[ C_V(P\,dV + V\,dP) = -(C_P - C_V)P\,dV \;\Rightarrow\; C_PP\,dV + C_VV\,dP = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \gamma\frac{dV}{V} + \frac{dP}{P} = 0 \]

Integrate: \(\gamma\ln V + \ln P = \text{const}\), i.e. \(PV^\gamma = \text{const}\). Substituting \(P = nRT/V\) gives the other two forms.积分：\(\gamma\ln V + \ln P = \text{const}\)，也就是 \(PV^\gamma = \text{const}\)。再代入 \(P = nRT/V\) 就得到另外两个形式。

Watch out: adiabatic ≠ \(PV^\gamma\). \(PV^\gamma = \text{const}\) needs the process to be quasi-static. A free expansion is adiabatic but follows no curve at all (and an ideal gas's \(T\) doesn't change). 3.7  Heat engines and the Carnot cycle3.7 热机与卡诺循环

A heat engine runs in a cycle: it absorbs heat \(Q_H\) from a hot reservoir, delivers work \(W\), and dumps heat \(Q_C\) into a cold reservoir. Over a full cycle the working gas returns to its starting state, so \(\Delta U = 0\) and \(W = Q_H - Q_C\) (all quantities as magnitudes). A refrigerator runs the same cycle backwards, using work to pump heat from cold to hot.热机就是一直在循环干活：从高温热库吸热 \(Q_H\)，输出功 \(W\)，再往低温热库排热 \(Q_C\)。一整个循环下来，工作物质回到原点，所以 \(\Delta U = 0\)、\(W = Q_H - Q_C\)（这里都取大小）。冰箱就是把同一个循环倒着开，用功把热从冷处搬到热处。

Figures of merit

\[ \eta = \frac{W}{Q_H} = 1 - \frac{Q_C}{Q_H} \quad(\text{engine}), \qquad \text{COP} = \frac{Q_C}{W} = \frac{Q_C}{Q_H - Q_C} \quad(\text{refrigerator}) \]

The Carnot cycle is the ideal reversible engine: two isotherms joined by two adiabats.卡诺循环是最理想的可逆热机：两条等温线，用两条绝热线连起来。

Carnot efficiency

\[ \eta_{\text{Carnot}} = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}, \qquad \frac{Q_C}{Q_H} = \frac{T_C}{T_H}, \qquad \text{COP}_{\text{Carnot}} = \frac{T_C}{T_H - T_C} \]

The efficiency depends only on the two temperatures and is always below 1. A refrigerator's COP can exceed 1: it moves more heat than the work it uses, which is fine because the heat dumped is \(Q_H = Q_C + W\). Chapter 4 shows that no engine between \(T_H\) and \(T_C\) can beat \(\eta_{\text{Carnot}}\).效率只看两个温度，而且永远小于 1。冰箱的 COP 倒是可以超过 1：它搬的热比它花的功还多，这不奇怪，因为往热端排的总量是 \(Q_H = Q_C + W\)。第 4 章会证明，\(T_H\) 和 \(T_C\) 之间的任何热机都打不过 \(\eta_{\text{Carnot}}\)。

Proof: \(\eta = 1 - T_C/T_H\)证明：\(\eta = 1 - T_C/T_H\)

Entropy and the Second Law熵与第二定律

What this chapter does

The First Law would allow a hot cup of tea to get hotter by drawing heat from a cold room, as long as energy is conserved. That never happens. The Second Law captures which way things actually go, and this chapter turns it into a number, the entropy \(S\): in any process that happens by itself, the total entropy goes up. The main practical skill is computing \(\Delta S\), which the exams test heavily.按第一定律，一杯热茶从冷房间里"吸热"变得更热，只要能量守恒就没问题。可现实里这从来没发生过。第二定律管的就是"事情到底往哪边走"，这一章把它变成能算的数——熵 \(S\)：只要是自发的，总熵一定增加。考卷上最常考的就是怎么算 \(\Delta S\)。

4.1  Reversible processes and reservoirs4.1 可逆过程与热库

Two idealisations make the theory work, in the way frictionless planes make mechanics work:有两个理想化撑起了整套理论，就像力学里的"无摩擦斜面"一样：

  • A reversible process is quasi-static with no dissipation (§1.2). Run it backwards and both the system and the surroundings return to exactly where they were. Real processes only approximate this.可逆过程就是没耗散的准静态过程（§1.2）。倒着放一遍，系统和环境都能精确回到原处。真实过程只能做到"差不多"。
  • A heat reservoir has such a large heat capacity that taking heat from it or adding heat to it doesn't change its temperature (the atmosphere, a lake, a big water bath). Heat exchange is always reversible from the reservoir's point of view.热库的热容大到离谱，吸点热、放点热，温度几乎不变（大气、湖水、大浴缸）。从热库自己的角度看，跟它换多少热都算可逆。

4.2  The Second Law4.2 第二定律

Two everyday observations, stated carefully, turn out to be the same law:有两件日常都看得见的事，仔细一表述，其实是同一条定律：

Kelvin statement

No process can have as its only effect the complete conversion of heat into work.没有任何过程能"唯一的后果"就是把热全变成功。

Clausius statement

Heat cannot flow from a colder body to a hotter one with no other effect.热不可能"没有其他影响"地从冷物体跑到热物体。

The words "only" and "no other effect" matter. An ideal gas expanding isothermally converts heat fully into work, but its volume has changed (another effect), so it can't be repeated in a cycle. A refrigerator moves heat from cold to hot, but it consumes work. What is forbidden is a cyclic machine that turns heat from a single reservoir entirely into work, or heat flowing uphill by itself."唯一""没有其他影响"这几个字很关键。理想气体等温膨胀能把热全变成功，但它的体积变了（这就是"其他影响"），所以没法反复循环着用。冰箱能把热从冷搬到热，但它得耗功。真正被禁的是：单靠一个热库的热，循环着全变成功；或者热自己往高处流。

Proof: the two statements are equivalent证明：两种说法是一回事

Violate Clausius ⇒ violate Kelvin. If heat could flow from cold to hot by itself, run a Carnot engine between the reservoirs and send the heat it dumps into the cold reservoir straight back up to the hot one. Net effect: heat taken from the hot reservoir and turned entirely into work. Kelvin is violated.违反克劳修斯 ⇒ 违反开尔文。假如热能自己从冷跑到热，那就让一台卡诺热机在两个热库间工作，再把它排到冷库的热原封不动送回热库。净效果：从热库取了热，全变成功。开尔文被违反。

Violate Kelvin ⇒ violate Clausius. If a perfect engine could turn heat fully into work, use that work to run a refrigerator. Net effect: heat moved from cold to hot with nothing else changed. Clausius is violated.违反开尔文 ⇒ 违反克劳修斯。假如有台完美热机能热全变功，拿这个功去开冰箱。净效果：热从冷搬到热，别的啥也没变。克劳修斯被违反。

4.3  Carnot's theorem and absolute temperature4.3 卡诺定理与绝对温度

Carnot's theorem

No engine working between two reservoirs is more efficient than a reversible one, and all reversible engines between the same two temperatures have the same efficiency:两个热库之间，没有哪台热机能比可逆热机更高效；而且同样两个温度之间，所有可逆热机效率一样：

\[ \eta \le \eta_{\text{rev}} = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H} \]

This is the ultimate speed limit for turning heat into work, and it doesn't depend on the working substance (gas, steam, rubber band) or the details of the engine. Because it is universal, it can be used to define temperature without reference to any material: the thermodynamic temperature is defined by \(Q_C/Q_H = T_C/T_H\) for a reversible engine. It agrees with the ideal-gas scale of §1.5 (both give the Carnot result).这是"热变功"的天花板，而且跟工作物质是啥（气体、蒸汽、橡皮筋）、热机长什么样都没关系。正因为这么普适，它可以直接拿来定温度，不用管什么材料：热力学温度就定义为可逆热机的 \(Q_C/Q_H = T_C/T_H\)。它跟 §1.5 的理想气体温标是一回事（两边算出来的卡诺结果一样）。

Proof sketch证明思路

Suppose an engine X beat a reversible engine R between the same reservoirs. Run R backwards as a refrigerator, driven by part of X's work, and let it put back into the hot reservoir the heat X took out. Because X is more efficient, there is work left over, and the only net effect is heat taken from the cold reservoir and turned into work, or heat moved from cold to hot. That contradicts the Second Law. Applying this argument both ways to two reversible engines shows their efficiencies must be equal.假设热机 X 在同一对热库之间比可逆热机 R 还厉害。让 R 倒着开当冰箱，由 X 出的一部分功驱动，再把 X 从热库取走的热原样还回去。既然 X 效率更高，就会多出一点功，而唯一的净效果就是从冷库取热变成功、或者把热从冷搬到热。这违反第二定律。两种方式都套一遍，就说明两台可逆热机效率必须相等。

For the temperature scale: if \(Q_C/Q_H = f(\theta_C, \theta_H)\) for every reversible engine, chaining two engines through an intermediate reservoir forces \(f(\theta_C, \theta_H) = g(\theta_C)/g(\theta_H)\). Choosing \(g(\theta) = \theta\) defines the absolute scale.至于温标：如果所有可逆热机都满足 \(Q_C/Q_H = f(\theta_C, \theta_H)\)，中间插一个热库串两台，就能推出 \(f(\theta_C, \theta_H) = g(\theta_C)/g(\theta_H)\)。选 \(g(\theta) = \theta\) 就定出了绝对温标。

4.4  Entropy4.4 熵

For a Carnot cycle, \(Q_H/T_H = Q_C/T_C\); with signs (heat absorbed positive), \(Q_H/T_H + Q_C/T_C = 0\). This generalises to any reversible cycle broken into many small heat exchanges: \(\oint\text{đ}Q_{\text{rev}}/T = 0\). An integral that vanishes around every closed loop doesn't depend on the path between two points, so it defines a state function:对卡诺循环，\(Q_H/T_H = Q_C/T_C\)；带上符号（吸热为正），就是 \(Q_H/T_H + Q_C/T_C = 0\)。把它拆成很多小段热交换，对任意可逆循环都成立：\(\oint\text{đ}Q_{\text{rev}}/T = 0\)。这种"绕一圈积分为零"的积分，说明它跟路径无关，于是可以定义一个状态函数：

Definition of entropy

\[ \Delta S = S_B - S_A = \int_A^B\frac{\text{đ}Q_{\text{rev}}}{T} \]

Two things to hold on to:有两件事要咬住：

  1. The integral must be taken along a reversible path. For a real (irreversible) process, \(\int\text{đ}Q/T\) is not \(\Delta S\).积分必须沿可逆路径走。真实（不可逆）过程中，\(\int\text{đ}Q/T\) 并不等于 \(\Delta S\)。
  2. But \(\Delta S\) itself only depends on the end states. So for an irreversible process, invent any convenient reversible path between the same start and end, and integrate along that. This is the single most important trick in the chapter.但 \(\Delta S\) 本身只看初末状态。所以遇到不可逆过程，就在相同的初末之间"造"一条方便的可逆路径，沿着它积。这是这一章最值钱的一招。
Proof: Clausius's theorem \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)证明：克劳修斯定理 \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)

Let a system go round any cycle, exchanging heat \(\text{đ}Q\) at temperatures \(T\). Supply each bit of heat with a small Carnot engine running from a single big reservoir at \(T_0\); each engine draws \(T_0\,\text{đ}Q/T\) from that reservoir. After one cycle the system and all the small engines are back to their start, and the net heat drawn from the single reservoir, \(T_0\oint\text{đ}Q/T\), has become work. Kelvin forbids this from being positive, so \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\), with equality for a reversible cycle (run it backwards and the inequality flips).让系统绕任意循环走一圈，在温度 \(T\) 处交换热 \(\text{đ}Q\)。每一小份热，都配一台小卡诺热机，它们统一从温度为 \(T_0\) 的大热库取热；每台热机从大热库取 \(T_0\,\text{đ}Q/T\)。一个循环下来，系统和所有小热机都回到起点，从大热库净取的热 \(T_0\oint\text{đ}Q/T\) 全变成了功。开尔文说这不能是正的，所以 \(\oint\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)；可逆循环取等号（倒过来走一遍，不等号就翻）。

4.5  The entropy principle4.5 熵原理

Compare a real process with a reversible one between the same states and Clausius's theorem gives把真实过程和相同状态间的可逆过程一比，克劳修斯定理给出

Clausius inequality and the entropy principle

\[ dS \ge \frac{\text{đ}Q}{T} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \Delta S_{\text{isolated}} \ge 0, \qquad \Delta S_{\text{univ}} = \Delta S_{\text{sys}} + \Delta S_{\text{surr}} \ge 0 \]

Equality holds only for reversible processes. In words: the entropy of an isolated system never decreases; treating "system + surroundings" as one isolated whole, the entropy of the universe never decreases. This gives a mechanical test:只有可逆过程才取等号。大白话：孤立系统的熵只增不减；把"系统 + 环境"当成一个孤立整体，那就是宇宙的熵只增不减。于是有了一个能做判断的判据：

Reversibility test: compute \(\Delta S_{\text{sys}}\) and \(\Delta S_{\text{surr}}\) separately and add them. \(\Delta S_{\text{univ}} = 0\) ⇒ reversible; \(\gt 0\) ⇒ irreversible; \(\lt 0\) ⇒ impossible (check your signs).
Proof证明

Go from A to B by an irreversible path I, and come back by a reversible path II. For the cycle, \(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T + \int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\). The return leg is reversible, so \(\int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T = S_A - S_B\). Hence \(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T \le S_B - S_A\). For an isolated system \(\text{đ}Q = 0\), so \(S_B \ge S_A\).从 A 沿不可逆路径 I 到 B，再沿可逆路径 II 回来。对整圈，\(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T + \int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T \le 0\)。回来的那段可逆，所以 \(\int_{II}\text{đ}Q/T = S_A - S_B\)。于是 \(\int_I\text{đ}Q/T \le S_B - S_A\)。对孤立系统 \(\text{đ}Q = 0\)，所以 \(S_B \ge S_A\)。

4.7  The fundamental relation and ideal-gas entropy4.7 基本关系式与理想气体熵

For a reversible change, \(\text{đ}Q = T\,dS\) and \(\text{đ}W = -P\,dV\), so the First Law becomes可逆变化时，\(\text{đ}Q = T\,dS\) 和 \(\text{đ}W = -P\,dV\)，第一定律就变成

Fundamental relation

\[ dU = T\,dS - P\,dV \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad dS = \frac{1}{T}\,dU + \frac{P}{T}\,dV \]

It contains only state functions, so although derived for a reversible change, it holds between any two nearby equilibrium states. Reading off coefficients (§2.6, tool ①):这里头全是状态函数，所以虽然是从可逆变化推出来的，却在任意两个邻近的平衡态之间都成立。按 §2.6 那个"读系数"的招：

\[ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial U}\right)_{\!V} = \frac1T, \qquad \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_{\!U} = \frac PT \]
Where it's used later

This one line is the starting point of Chapters 5–9. Chapter 5 rewrites it with \(T\) and \(P\) as variables (the potentials \(F\) and \(G\)); Chapter 6 uses \(\partial S/\partial U = 1/T\) to show that heat flows from hot to cold.这一行就是第 5–9 章的老祖宗。第 5 章把它改写成以 \(T\)、\(P\) 当变量（就是势 \(F\) 和 \(G\)）；第 6 章用 \(\partial S/\partial U = 1/T\) 说明热为啥从热往冷流。

Ideal-gas entropy change

\[ \Delta S = nc_V\ln\frac{T_2}{T_1} + nR\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1} = nc_P\ln\frac{T_2}{T_1} - nR\ln\frac{P_2}{P_1} \]

Proof of the \((T,P)\) form证明 \((T,P)\) 那个形式

From \(PV = nRT\): \(dV/V = dT/T - dP/P\). Substituting into \(dS = nc_V\,dT/T + nR\,dV/V\) and using \(c_V + R = c_P\) gives \(dS = nc_P\,dT/T - nR\,dP/P\).由 \(PV = nRT\)：\(dV/V = dT/T - dP/P\)。代进 \(dS = nc_V\,dT/T + nR\,dV/V\)，再用 \(c_V + R = c_P\)，就得到 \(dS = nc_P\,dT/T - nR\,dP/P\)。

Beyond the ideal gas. For any substance with equation of state \(P(T,V)\):推广到理想气体以外。对任意状态方程为 \(P(T,V)\) 的物质：

\[ dS = \frac{C_V}{T}\,dT + \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V}dV \]

(proved neatly in §5.6 with a Maxwell relation). For a van der Waals gas, \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = nR/(V - nb)\), so \(\Delta S = C_V\ln(T_2/T_1) + nR\ln\frac{V_2 - nb}{V_1 - nb}\). 4.8  What entropy measures: \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\)4.8 熵到底在衡量什么：\(S = k_B\ln\Omega\)

Flip 100 coins. A microstate is the exact sequence (there are \(2^{100}\)); a macrostate is what you'd actually report, say "\(n\) heads". The number of microstates in a macrostate is the multiplicity \(\Omega(N,n) = \binom{N}{n}\). "All heads" has \(\Omega = 1\); "50 heads" has \(\Omega \approx 10^{29}\). If the coins are shaken randomly, you will essentially always see something near 50, simply because overwhelmingly more microstates look like that.抛 100 枚硬币。一个"微观态"就是精确的序列（一共 \(2^{100}\) 种）；"宏观态"是你真正会报告的东西，比如"\(n\) 个正面"。一个宏观态里包含多少个微观态，就叫多重度 \(\Omega(N,n) = \binom{N}{n}\)。"全是正面"只有 \(\Omega = 1\) 种；"50 个正面"有 \(\Omega \approx 10^{29}\) 种。你要是真去摇，几乎每次都接近 50，纯粹因为长得像 50 的微观态多到离谱。

Boltzmann entropy

\[ S = k_B\ln\Omega \]

Equilibrium is the macrostate with the largest \(\Omega\). The Second Law becomes a statement about probability: systems drift towards macrostates with more microstates because those are vastly more likely. For a macroscopic system (\(N \sim 10^{23}\)) "vastly" is so extreme that a decrease in entropy is never observed.平衡态就是 \(\Omega\) 最大的那个宏观态。第二定律于是变成一句概率话：系统会往微观态更多的地方漂，因为那种地方可能性大得多。对一个宏观系统（\(N \sim 10^{23}\)）来说，这个"大得多"夸张到熵减少压根看不到。

Sackur–Tetrode equation (monatomic ideal gas)

\[ S = Nk_B\left[\ln\left(\frac VN\left(\frac{4\pi mU}{3Nh^2}\right)^{3/2}\right) + \frac52\right] \]

Differentiate and the familiar results drop out: \(1/T = \partial S/\partial U = \tfrac32Nk_B/U\) gives \(U = \tfrac32Nk_BT\); \(P/T = \partial S/\partial V = Nk_B/V\) gives \(PV = Nk_BT\). Microscopic counting and macroscopic thermodynamics agree.对熵求个导，熟悉的结论就自动蹦出来了：\(1/T = \partial S/\partial U = \tfrac32Nk_B/U\) 给出 \(U = \tfrac32Nk_BT\)；\(P/T = \partial S/\partial V = Nk_B/V\) 给出 \(PV = Nk_BT\)。微观数数和宏观热力学完全对得上。

Where Sackur–Tetrode comes from (sketch)萨克–特策罗德公式是咋来的（说个大概）

\(\Omega\) is proportional to the volume of phase space available: positions contribute \(V^N\); momenta lie on a \(3N\)-dimensional sphere of radius \(\sqrt{2mU}\), contributing \(\propto U^{3N/2}\); and dividing by \(N!\) accounts for identical particles being indistinguishable. So \(\Omega \propto V^NU^{3N/2}/[N!\,(3N/2)!]\). Taking the log with Stirling's approximation \(\ln N! \approx N\ln N - N\) gives the formula. The \(1/N!\) is what makes \(S\) depend on \(V/N\) rather than \(V\); see the Gibbs paradox in §8.1.\(\Omega\) 正比于能到达的相空间体积：位置贡献 \(V^N\)；动量落在半径为 \(\sqrt{2mU}\) 的 \(3N\) 维球面上，贡献 \(\propto U^{3N/2}\)；再除以 \(N!\) 就是为了说明全同粒子不可分辨。于是 \(\Omega \propto V^NU^{3N/2}/[N!\,(3N/2)!]\)。取对数、用斯特林近似 \(\ln N! \approx N\ln N - N\)，公式就出来了。那个 \(1/N!\) 正是让 \(S\) 依赖 \(V/N\) 而不是 \(V\) 的关键；详见 §8.1 的吉布斯佯谬。

5.1  Natural variables: what a differential tells you5.1 自然变量：从一个微分能读出啥

Look at the structure of \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\): each term on the right is "some quantity × d(another quantity)". The quantities after the \(d\), here \(S\) and \(V\), are the natural variables of \(U\). Treat \(U\) as \(U(S,V)\), compare with the total differential \(dU = (\partial U/\partial S)_V dS + (\partial U/\partial V)_S dV\), and read off:看 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\) 长什么样：右边每一项都是"某个量 × d(另一个量)"。\(d\) 后面跟着的那玩意儿——这儿是 \(S\) 和 \(V\)——就叫 \(U\) 的自然变量。把 \(U\) 当成 \(U(S,V)\)，跟全微分 \(dU = (\partial U/\partial S)_V dS + (\partial U/\partial V)_S dV\) 对着看，系数直接读出来：

Reading \(T\) and \(P\) off \(U(S,V)\)

\[ T = \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial S}\right)_{\!V}, \qquad P = -\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{\!S} \]

This says something important: if you know \(U\) as a function of its natural variables \(S\) and \(V\), you can find the temperature, the pressure and hence the equation of state. All the thermodynamics of the system is contained in that one function. That is where the word "potential" comes from: just as differentiating a potential energy gives a force, differentiating a thermodynamic potential gives \(T\), \(P\) and so on.这句话挺重要：只要知道 \(U\) 作为它自然变量 \(S\)、\(V\) 的函数，温度、压强、连状态方程都全有了。整个系统的热力学，就藏在这一个函数里。"势"这个叫法也是这么来的：跟"势能求导得力"一个道理，热力学势求导就得到 \(T\)、\(P\) 这些。

Example · The whole ideal gas from \(U(S,V)\)

The entropy of a monatomic ideal gas (Chapter 4) can be inverted to give \(U(S,V) = c\,V^{-2/3}\,e^{2S/3Nk_B}\), with \(c\) a constant. Find \(T\) and \(P\) and check that you recover familiar results.单原子理想气体的熵（第 4 章）能反解出 \(U(S,V) = c\,V^{-2/3}\,e^{2S/3Nk_B}\)，其中 \(c\) 是常数。求 \(T\) 和 \(P\)，再看看能不能拿回熟悉的结果。

Show solution显示解答

\[ T = \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial S}\right)_{\!V} = \frac{2}{3Nk_B}\,U \quad\Longrightarrow\quad U = \tfrac{3}{2}Nk_BT \]

\[ P = -\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{\!S} = \frac{2}{3}\,\frac{U}{V} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad PV = \tfrac{2}{3}U = Nk_BT \]

Equipartition and the ideal gas law both come out of a single function.一个函数，就把能量均分和理想气体定律都包了。

5.2  Changing variables: enthalpy, Helmholtz and Gibbs free energies5.2 换变量：焓、亥姆霍兹自由能和吉布斯自由能

To swap the natural variable \(S\) for \(T\), there is a standard trick using the product rule \(d(TS) = T\,dS + S\,dT\). Subtract \(d(TS)\) from \(dU\):想把自然变量从 \(S\) 换成 \(T\)，有个标准套路，用乘积法则 \(d(TS) = T\,dS + S\,dT\)。拿 \(dU\) 减去 \(d(TS)\)：

\[ d(U - TS) = T\,dS - P\,dV - T\,dS - S\,dT = -S\,dT - P\,dV \]

The \(T\,dS\) term cancels and is replaced by \(-S\,dT\). The new function \(U - TS\) has natural variables \(T\) and \(V\). In the same way, adding \(d(PV)\) swaps \(V\) for \(P\). This gives four functions in total (the operation is called a Legendre transformation):\(T\,dS\) 那一项就消了，换成了 \(-S\,dT\)。新函数 \(U - TS\) 的自然变量是 \(T\) 和 \(V\)。同理，加上 \(d(PV)\) 就是把 \(V\) 换成 \(P\)。这么一来一共四个函数（这一手叫勒让德变换）：

The four thermodynamic potentials

\[ \begin{aligned} &U &&dU = T\,dS - P\,dV &&\text{natural variables } S, V \\ &H = U + PV &&dH = T\,dS + V\,dP &&\text{natural variables } S, P \\ &F = U - TS &&dF = -S\,dT - P\,dV &&\text{natural variables } T, V \\ &G = U - TS + PV &&dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP &&\text{natural variables } T, P \end{aligned} \]

No need to memorise four differentials, just the pattern: adding \(PV\) turns \(-P\,dV\) into \(+V\,dP\); subtracting \(TS\) turns \(T\,dS\) into \(-S\,dT\).四个微分不用死记，记住套路就行：加 \(PV\)，就把 \(-P\,dV\) 换成 \(+V\,dP\)；减 \(TS\)，就把 \(T\,dS\) 换成 \(-S\,dT\)。

Reading off coefficients works for every potential:读系数这一招，每个势都好使：

\[ \begin{aligned} &T = \left(\frac{\partial H}{\partial S}\right)_P, &&V = \left(\frac{\partial H}{\partial P}\right)_S \\ &S = -\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial T}\right)_V, &&P = -\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial V}\right)_T \\ &S = -\left(\frac{\partial G}{\partial T}\right)_P, &&V = \left(\frac{\partial G}{\partial P}\right)_T \end{aligned} \]

The last line gets used constantly in Chapters 7 and 8: the \(T\)-derivative of \(G\) is \(-S\), the \(P\)-derivative is \(V\).最后一行在第 7、8 章简直是常客：\(G\) 对 \(T\) 求导得 \(-S\)，对 \(P\) 求导得 \(V\)。

Write the four variables at the corners and the four potentials on the sides ("Valid Facts and Theoretical Understandings Generate Solutions to Hard Problems", read clockwise from top-left):把四个变量放四角，四个势放四条边（"Very Famous Teachers Understand Great Subjects, Have Proper Principles"，从左上顺时针读）：

\[ \begin{array}{ccc} V & F & T \\ U & & G \\ S & H & P \end{array} \]

Add two diagonal arrows, one from \(S\) to \(T\) and one from \(P\) to \(V\). Then:再加两个斜箭头，一个从 \(S\) 指到 \(T\)，一个从 \(P\) 指到 \(V\)。然后：

  • Each potential's natural variables are the two corners next to it (\(F\): \(V\) and \(T\)).每个势的自然变量，就是挨着它的两个角（\(F\)：\(V\) 和 \(T\)）。
  • In its differential, each natural variable is multiplied by the corner diagonally opposite.在微分里，每个自然变量旁边乘的，就是对角的那个角。
  • The sign is \(+\) if the arrow points away from the natural variable, \(-\) if it points towards it.箭头"离开"自然变量的，符号取 \(+\)；箭头"指向"它的，符号取 \(-\)。

Check with \(G\): natural variables \(T, P\). Opposite \(T\) is \(S\), and the arrow points towards \(T\), giving \(-S\,dT\). Opposite \(P\) is \(V\), and the arrow points away from \(P\), giving \(+V\,dP\). If this feels fiddly, the "add \(PV\) / subtract \(TS\)" rule above is more direct.拿 \(G\) 验一下：自然变量 \(T, P\)。对角的 \(T\) 是 \(S\)，箭头指向 \(T\)，给 \(-S\,dT\)。对角的 \(P\) 是 \(V\)，箭头离开 \(P\)，给 \(+V\,dP\)。要是觉得绕，上面那个"加 \(PV\) / 减 \(TS\)"其实更直给。

5.3  What the new functions mean physically5.3 这几个新函数到底啥意思

\(H\), \(F\) and \(G\) are not just a change of variables; each one is the useful quantity in a particular experimental situation.\(H\)、\(F\)、\(G\) 可不是单纯换个变量玩；每一个都对应一种实际场景里"最有用的那个量"。

Enthalpy \(H\): the heat absorbed at constant pressure焓 \(H\)：恒压时吸的热

At constant pressure \(dP = 0\), so \(dH = T\,dS = \text{đ}Q\):恒压时 \(dP = 0\)，所以 \(dH = T\,dS = \text{đ}Q\)：

Heat at constant pressure

\[ Q_P = \Delta H \]

Chemical reactions, melting and boiling almost always happen at atmospheric pressure, so the "heat of reaction" or "latent heat" you find in tables is really a \(\Delta H\).化学反应、熔化、沸腾，基本都在大气压下发生，所以你在表里查到的"反应热""潜热"，其实就是个 \(\Delta H\)。

Where it's used later

The latent heat \(L = \Delta H = T\Delta S\) in Chapter 7; the reaction heat \(\Delta h_0\) in the van 't Hoff equation of Chapter 8; and throttling (Joule–Thomson, Chapter 3) is a constant-\(H\) process.第 7 章的潜热 \(L = \Delta H = T\Delta S\)；第 8 章范特霍夫方程里的反应热 \(\Delta h_0\)；节流（焦耳–汤姆孙，第 3 章）就是恒 \(H\) 过程。

Helmholtz free energy \(F\): the maximum work at constant temperature亥姆霍兹自由能 \(F\)：恒温下能做的最大功

For a system in contact with a reservoir at temperature \(T\), the work it can deliver is at most \(-\Delta F\) (proved in Chapter 6). In a reversible isothermal process, the work done on the system equals \(\Delta F\) exactly:系统跟温度为 \(T\) 的热库贴在一起，它能对外做的功最多 \(-\Delta F\)（第 6 章证）。如果是可逆等温过程，外界对它做的功正好等于 \(\Delta F\)：

Reversible isothermal process

\[ W_{\text{on}} = \Delta F, \qquad W_{\text{by, max}} = -\Delta F \]

Why not \(-\Delta U\)? Because during an isothermal process the system also exchanges heat \(T\Delta S\) with the reservoir. Only the part \(F = U - TS\) is "free" to become work; the rest, \(TS\), can only be exchanged as heat.那为什么不是 \(-\Delta U\)？因为等温过程里系统还在跟热库换热 \(T\Delta S\)。只有 \(F = U - TS\) 这部分是"自由"的、能变成功；剩下的 \(TS\) 只能以热的形式换掉。

Example · Isothermal expansion of an ideal gas

Gibbs free energy \(G\): which way a process goes at constant \(T\) and \(P\)吉布斯自由能 \(G\)：恒 \(T\)、恒 \(P\) 下，事情往哪边走

Most real processes (reactions, phase changes, dissolving) take place at constant temperature and pressure. Chapter 6 shows that under these conditions a process can only go in the direction that lowers \(G\), and equilibrium is the minimum of \(G\). That makes \(G\) the central quantity of Chapters 6–8.真实过程（反应、相变、溶解）大多在恒温恒压下进行。第 6 章会说，这时候过程只能往让 \(G\) 变小的方向走，平衡就是 \(G\) 最小。所以 \(G\) 成了第 6–8 章的绝对主角。

Why it exists

Questions like "does ice melt at −5 °C?" or "does this reaction run by itself?" are answered by the sign of \(\Delta G\). The form \(G = H - TS\) shows the competition: lowering \(H\) (releasing heat) favours one direction, raising \(S\) (more disorder) may favour the other, and the temperature \(T\) sets which one wins.像"冰在 −5 °C 会化吗？""这反应能自己跑吗？"这种问题，看 \(\Delta G\) 的正负就知道。写成 \(G = H - TS\) 更好懂：降低 \(H\)（放热）推一个方向，升高 \(S\)（变得更乱）可能推另一个方向，最后温度 \(T\) 来决定谁赢。

5.4  Maxwell relations: trading entropy for measurable quantities5.4 麦克斯韦关系：把熵换成能测的量

The problem: many questions ask for an entropy change, e.g. "how much does the entropy of a copper block change when it is compressed at constant temperature?" That needs \((\partial S/\partial P)_T\), but there is no "entropy meter". The Maxwell relations replace such entropy derivatives by derivatives of \(P, V, T\), which come from the equation of state or from tabulated data.麻烦就出在这：好多题问的是熵变，比如「铜块恒温压缩时熵变多少」。这得用到 \((\partial S/\partial P)_T\)，可世上没有「熵表」。麦克斯韦关系就是干这个的——把这种熵的导数，换成 \(P, V, T\) 的导数，而后者能从状态方程或数据表里查到。

Where they come from is simple. Each potential is a state function, so its mixed second derivative does not depend on the order of differentiation (the toolkit in §2.6). Take \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\): the coefficients are \(-S\) and \(V\), and cross-differentiating gives它们哪来的？其实特简单。每个势都是状态函数，所以混合二阶导跟求导先后顺序无关（§2.6 的工具箱）。拿 \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\) 来说：系数是 \(-S\) 和 \(V\)，交叉一求导：

\[ \left.\frac{\partial(-S)}{\partial P}\right|_T = \left.\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right|_P \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} = -\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} \]

Doing this once for each potential:每个势都来一遍：

Maxwell relations

\[ \begin{aligned} \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial V}\right)_{\!S} &= -\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial S}\right)_{\!V} &&(U) \\ \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_{\!S} &= \left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial S}\right)_{\!P} &&(H) \\ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T} &= \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V} &&(F) \\ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} &= -\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} &&(G) \end{aligned} \]

The ones you will actually use are the last two (from \(F\) and \(G\)): their left sides ask "how does entropy change with \(V\) or \(P\) at constant \(T\)?", and their right sides come straight from the equation of state. You don't need to memorise them: write the relevant differential, cross-differentiate, and the signs take care of themselves.真正天天用的就最后两条（来自 \(F\) 和 \(G\)）：左边问的是"恒 \(T\) 时，熵随 \(V\) 或 \(P\) 怎么变"，右边直接从状态方程就来。千万别背：把相应的微分写出来，交叉求导，正负号自己就出来了。

Example 1 · Sanity check: isothermal expansion of an ideal gas

At constant \(T\), \(dS = (\partial S/\partial V)_T\,dV\). \[ \Delta S = \int_{V_1}^{V_2}\frac{nR}{V}\,dV = nR\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1} \]

Same answer as Chapter 4's \(\int\text{đ}Q/T\), but without ever asking how much heat flowed.跟第 4 章得到的 \(\int\text{đ}Q/T\) 一模一样，但全程压根没问流了多少热。

Example 2 · Compressing a litre of water

5.5  The relation between \(C_P\) and \(C_V\)5.5 \(C_P\) 和 \(C_V\) 的关系

For solids and liquids, heating at constant volume is practically impossible: the material wants to expand, and holding its volume fixed takes enormous pressure. So experiments measure \(C_P\). When theory needs \(C_V\), it is calculated from \(C_P\), \(\alpha\) and \(\kappa_T\) using:对固体液体来说，恒容加热基本没法做：材料想着撑开，你要按住它体积，得加巨大压强。所以实验测的都是 \(C_P\)。当理论要用 \(C_V\) 时，就靠 \(C_P\)、\(\alpha\)、\(\kappa_T\) 现算：

\(C_P - C_V\)

\[ C_P - C_V = \frac{TV\alpha^2}{\kappa_T} \]

  • Every factor on the right is \(\geq 0\) (\(\kappa_T \gt 0\) is the stability condition of Chapter 6), so always \(C_P \geq C_V\): at constant pressure some of the heat goes into expansion work.右边每个因子都是 \(\geq 0\)（\(\kappa_T \gt 0\) 就是第 6 章的稳定性条件），所以永远有 \(C_P \geq C_V\)：恒压下，总有一部分热跑去做了膨胀功。
  • The two are equal when \(\alpha = 0\), for example water at 4 °C.只有 \(\alpha = 0\) 时两者才相等，比如 4 °C 的水。
  • Check: for an ideal gas \(\alpha = 1/T\) and \(\kappa_T = 1/P\), giving \(TV\cdot\frac{1}{T^2}\cdot P = \frac{PV}{T} = nR\), the familiar \(C_P - C_V = nR\).验一下：理想气体 \(\alpha = 1/T\)、\(\kappa_T = 1/P\)，得到 \(TV\cdot\frac{1}{T^2}\cdot P = \frac{PV}{T} = nR\)，就是熟悉的 \(C_P - C_V = nR\)。
Proof证明

Treat \(S\) as \(S(T,V)\): \(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V\,dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T\,dV\). Divide by \(dT\) at constant \(P\):把 \(S\) 看成 \(S(T,V)\)：\(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V\,dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T\,dV\)。恒 \(P\) 下除以 \(dT\)：

\[ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} = \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V} + \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} \]

Multiply by \(T\): \(C_P = C_V + T(\partial S/\partial V)_T(\partial V/\partial T)_P\). Use the Maxwell relation \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\), then the cyclic relation \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P/(\partial V/\partial P)_T = \alpha/\kappa_T\), and \((\partial V/\partial T)_P = V\alpha\):再乘 \(T\)：\(C_P = C_V + T(\partial S/\partial V)_T(\partial V/\partial T)_P\)。用麦克斯韦关系 \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\)，再上循环关系 \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P/(\partial V/\partial P)_T = \alpha/\kappa_T\)，以及 \((\partial V/\partial T)_P = V\alpha\)：

\[ C_P - C_V = T\cdot\frac{\alpha}{\kappa_T}\cdot V\alpha = \frac{TV\alpha^2}{\kappa_T} \]

5.6  Maxwell relations at work5.6 麦克斯韦关系实战

The following results appear in homework and in later chapters. Each uses the same three steps: write the derivative you want → use a Maxwell relation to eliminate \(\partial S\) → substitute the equation of state.下面这些结果，作业里、后面章节里都会碰到。套路就三步：写出你想要的导数 → 用麦克斯韦关系把 \(\partial S\) 消掉 → 代入状态方程。

(a) Entropy change of any substance(a) 任何物质的熵变

Two general entropy differentials

\[ dS = \frac{C_V}{T}\,dT + \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V}dV, \qquad dS = \frac{C_P}{T}\,dT - \left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P}dP \]

Given an equation of state and a heat capacity, you can now find the entropy change of any substance between any two states: integrate along a convenient path (change \(T\) at fixed \(V\) or \(P\), then change \(V\) or \(P\) at fixed \(T\)). For an ideal gas this reproduces Chapter 4's \(\Delta S = nC_V\ln(T_2/T_1) + nR\ln(V_2/V_1)\).有了状态方程和热容，任何物质在任意两个状态之间的熵变你都能算：沿一条方便的路径积分（先在固定 \(V\) 或 \(P\) 下改 \(T\)，再在固定 \(T\) 下改 \(V\) 或 \(P\)）。对理想气体，这就回到第 4 章的 \(\Delta S = nC_V\ln(T_2/T_1) + nR\ln(V_2/V_1)\)。

Proof证明

The total differential of \(S(T,V)\) is \(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T dV\). The first coefficient is \(C_V/T\) by definition of heat capacity; the second becomes \((\partial P/\partial T)_V\) by the \(F\) relation. The second formula is the same argument with \(S(T,P)\) and the \(G\) relation.\(S(T,V)\) 的全微分是 \(dS = (\partial S/\partial T)_V dT + (\partial S/\partial V)_T dV\)。第一个系数按热容定义就是 \(C_V/T\)；第二个借 \(F\) 关系变成 \((\partial P/\partial T)_V\)。第二个式子是一样的套路，换成 \(S(T,P)\) 和 \(G\) 关系。

(b) How internal energy depends on volume(b) 内能跟体积有什么关系

Energy equation

\[ \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{\!T} = T\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_{\!V} - P \]

This settles a question left open in Chapter 3. For an ideal gas, \(P = nRT/V\) gives \(T\cdot nR/V - P = 0\). So the ideal gas's \(U\) depends only on \(T\): it is a consequence, not an extra assumption.这就把第 3 章留的一个坑填上了。对理想气体，\(P = nRT/V\) 给出 \(T\cdot nR/V - P = 0\)。所以理想气体的 \(U\) 只跟 \(T\) 有关：这是推出来的结论，不是硬加的假设。

Proof证明

Divide \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\) by \(dV\) at constant \(T\): \((\partial U/\partial V)_T = T(\partial S/\partial V)_T - P\). Then use \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\).恒 \(T\) 下把 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV\) 除以 \(dV\)：\((\partial U/\partial V)_T = T(\partial S/\partial V)_T - P\)。然后上 \((\partial S/\partial V)_T = (\partial P/\partial T)_V\)。

Example · A van der Waals gas cools in free expansion

A van der Waals gas, \(P = \frac{nRT}{V - nb} - \frac{an^2}{V^2}\) with constant \(C_V\), expands freely into a vacuum (no heat, no work) from \(V_1\) to \(V_2\). Show solution显示解答

\((\partial P/\partial T)_V = nR/(V - nb)\), so \((\partial U/\partial V)_T = \frac{nRT}{V-nb} - P = \frac{an^2}{V^2}\). Integrating, \(U = C_VT - \frac{an^2}{V} + \text{const}\).\((\partial P/\partial T)_V = nR/(V - nb)\)，所以 \((\partial U/\partial V)_T = \frac{nRT}{V-nb} - P = \frac{an^2}{V^2}\)。积分得 \(U = C_VT - \frac{an^2}{V} + \text{const}\)。

Free expansion has \(W = 0\) and \(Q = 0\), so \(U\) is unchanged:自由膨胀时 \(W = 0\)、\(Q = 0\)，所以 \(U\) 不变：

\[ C_V\Delta T = an^2\left(\frac{1}{V_2} - \frac{1}{V_1}\right) \lt 0 \]

The gas cools. As the molecules spread out they work against their mutual attraction (the \(a\) term), and that energy can only come from their thermal motion.气体会变冷。分子散开时得克服相互吸引（就是 \(a\) 那项）做点功，这能量只能从它们自己的热运动里抽。

(c) Why adiabatic expansion cools(c) 绝热膨胀为啥会变冷

\[ \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_{\!S} = \frac{TV\alpha}{C_P} \gt 0 \quad(\text{when } \alpha \gt 0) \]

Lowering the pressure reversibly and adiabatically (isentropically) lowers the temperature. For an ideal gas, \(\alpha = 1/T\) gives \(dT/T = \frac{nR}{C_P}\,dP/P = \frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}\,dP/P\), which is the differential form of Chapter 3's adiabatic relation \(TP^{(1-\gamma)/\gamma} = \text{const}\).可逆、绝热（等熵）地降压，温度就往下掉。对理想气体，\(\alpha = 1/T\) 给出 \(dT/T = \frac{nR}{C_P}\,dP/P = \frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}\,dP/P\)，这正好是第 3 章绝热关系 \(TP^{(1-\gamma)/\gamma} = \text{const}\) 的微分形式。

Proof证明

By the cyclic relation, \((\partial T/\partial P)_S = -(\partial S/\partial P)_T/(\partial S/\partial T)_P\). The numerator is \(-V\alpha\) by the \(G\) relation; the denominator is \(C_P/T\).用循环关系，\((\partial T/\partial P)_S = -(\partial S/\partial P)_T/(\partial S/\partial T)_P\)。分子按 \(G\) 关系是 \(-V\alpha\)；分母是 \(C_P/T\)。

(d) The Joule–Thomson (throttling) coefficient(d) 焦耳–汤姆孙（节流）系数

\[ \mu_{JT} = \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_{\!H} = \frac{V}{C_P}\,(T\alpha - 1) \]

For an ideal gas \(T\alpha = 1\), so throttling leaves the temperature unchanged. A real gas with \(T\alpha \gt 1\) cools on throttling (the principle behind gas liquefaction and refrigerators); with \(T\alpha \lt 1\) it warms. The boundary \(T\alpha = 1\) is the inversion curve.理想气体 \(T\alpha = 1\)，所以节流温度不变。实际气体如果 \(T\alpha \gt 1\)，节流就变冷（气体液化、冰箱就靠这个）；如果 \(T\alpha \lt 1\)，反而变热。分界线 \(T\alpha = 1\) 就是反转曲线。

Proof证明

\(\mu_{JT} = -(\partial H/\partial P)_T/(\partial H/\partial T)_P\). The denominator is \(C_P\). From \(dH = T\,dS + V\,dP\), \((\partial H/\partial P)_T = T(\partial S/\partial P)_T + V = -TV\alpha + V\). Substitute.\(\mu_{JT} = -(\partial H/\partial P)_T/(\partial H/\partial T)_P\)。分母是 \(C_P\)。由 \(dH = T\,dS + V\,dP\) 得 \((\partial H/\partial P)_T = T(\partial S/\partial P)_T + V = -TV\alpha + V\)。代进去就完了。

Extension: cooling by adiabatic demagnetisation (for interest)加餐：绝热去磁致冷（看看就好）

For a magnetic system, replace \(-P\,dV\) by \(H\,dM\) (applied field \(H\), magnetisation \(M\)); the same steps give magnetic Maxwell relations. For a paramagnet obeying Curie's law \(M = aVH/T\):磁系统就把 \(-P\,dV\) 换成 \(H\,dM\)（外加场 \(H\)，磁化强度 \(M\)），同样的步骤就得到磁性版麦克斯韦关系。遵守居里定律 \(M = aVH/T\) 的顺磁体：

\[ \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial H}\right)_{\!S} = \frac{aV}{C_H\,T}\,H \gt 0 \]

Reducing the field adiabatically lowers the temperature. This is a standard way to cool samples below 1 K, and the "isothermal step + adiabatic step" idea returns in Chapter 9 when we ask whether 0 K can be reached.绝热地把场减小，温度就降。这是把样品冷到 1 K 以下的常用手段，而"等温一步 + 绝热一步"这套思路，到第 9 章讨论"能不能到 0 K"时还会再出现。

5.7  When particle number changes: the chemical potential5.7 粒子数会变的时候：化学势

So far the particle number \(N\) has been fixed. But in a phase change (water molecules leaving the liquid for the vapour), a chemical reaction, or dissolving, the number of particles in each part changes. The fundamental relation then needs one more term:前面一直假设粒子数 \(N\) 不变。但相变（水分子从液体跑进蒸汽）、化学反应、溶解，这些过程里各部分的粒子数都在变。这时候基本关系式就得多加一项：

Fundamental relation for open systems

\[ dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN, \qquad dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP + \mu\,dN \]

\(\mu\) is the chemical potential. Reading off coefficients:\(\mu\) 就是化学势。读系数：

\[ \mu = \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial N}\right)_{\!S,V} = \left(\frac{\partial G}{\partial N}\right)_{\!T,P} \]

In words: how much \(G\) increases when one particle is added at fixed \(T\) and \(P\).大白话：固定 \(T\)、\(P\)，再塞进去一个粒子，\(G\) 涨多少。

Why it exists · where it's used later

\(T\) decides which way heat flows (hot to cold). \(P\) decides which side expands (high to low pressure). \(\mu\) decides which way particles move: from high \(\mu\) to low \(\mu\) (proved in Chapter 6). So:\(T\) 决定热往哪流（热→冷）。\(P\) 决定哪边膨胀（高压→低压）。\(\mu\) 决定粒子往哪跑：从高 \(\mu\) 跑向低 \(\mu\)（第 6 章证）。所以：

  • Chapter 7: two phases coexist ⟺ their chemical potentials are equal. Phase diagrams and the Clausius–Clapeyron equation follow from this single statement.第 7 章：两相共存 ⟺ 化学势相等。相图、克劳修斯–克拉珀龙方程，全是这一句话变出来的。
  • Chapter 8: chemical equilibrium, solutions and mixtures are all "set the \(\mu\)'s equal".第 8 章：化学平衡、溶液、混合物，统统都是"让 \(\mu\) 相等"。

\(G = \mu N\): the chemical potential is the Gibbs energy per particle\(G = \mu N\)：化学势就是每个粒子的吉布斯能量

\(G\) is extensive (doubling the system doubles \(G\)), but its natural variables \(T\) and \(P\) do not change with system size. So at fixed \(T, P\), \(G\) can only be proportional to \(N\), and the proportionality constant is \(\mu\):\(G\) 是广延量（系统翻倍，\(G\) 也翻倍），可它的自然变量 \(T\)、\(P\) 却跟系统大小无关。所以在固定 \(T, P\) 下，\(G\) 只能正比于 \(N\)，比例系数就是 \(\mu\)：

Euler relation

\[ G = \mu N \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mu = \frac{G}{N} \equiv g(T,P) \]

For a single substance, \(\mu\) is just the Gibbs energy per molecule (or per mole). Dividing \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\) by \(N\) gives the most important formula of Chapter 7:对单一物质，\(\mu\) 就是每个分子（或每摩尔）的吉布斯能量。拿 \(dG = -S\,dT + V\,dP\) 除以 \(N\)，就得到第 7 章最核心的公式：

\(d\mu\) for a single substance

\[ d\mu = -s\,dT + v\,dP \qquad (s = S/N,\ v = V/N) \]

Proof: the Euler and Gibbs–Duhem relations证明：欧拉关系与吉布斯–杜安关系

Scale the system by a factor \(\lambda\): \(U(\lambda S, \lambda V, \lambda N) = \lambda U(S, V, N)\). Differentiate with respect to \(\lambda\) and set \(\lambda = 1\):把系统放大 \(\lambda\) 倍：\(U(\lambda S, \lambda V, \lambda N) = \lambda U(S, V, N)\)。对 \(\lambda\) 求导，再令 \(\lambda = 1\)：

\[ U = TS - PV + \mu N \]

Hence \(G = U - TS + PV = \mu N\), and similarly \(H = TS + \mu N\), \(F = -PV + \mu N\).于是 \(G = U - TS + PV = \mu N\)，同理 \(H = TS + \mu N\)、\(F = -PV + \mu N\)。

Take the total differential of \(U = TS - PV + \mu N\) and subtract \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\). What remains is the Gibbs–Duhem relation:取 \(U = TS - PV + \mu N\) 的全微分，再减掉 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\)，剩下的就是吉布斯–杜安关系：

\[ S\,dT - V\,dP + N\,d\mu = 0 \]

It says \(T\), \(P\) and \(\mu\) cannot all vary independently: fix two and the third is determined. With several components, \(S\,dT - V\,dP + \sum_i N_i\,d\mu_i = 0\); this is the origin of the Gibbs phase rule in Chapter 7.它说的是：\(T\)、\(P\)、\(\mu\) 不能各变各的，钉住两个，第三个就定了。多组分的话 \(S\,dT - V\,dP + \sum_i N_i\,d\mu_i = 0\)；第 7 章吉布斯相律就是从这儿来的。

Chemical potential of an ideal gas理想气体的化学势

At constant temperature \(d\mu = v\,dP = \frac{k_BT}{P}\,dP\). Integrate from a reference pressure \(P_0\) to \(P\):恒温 \(d\mu = v\,dP = \frac{k_BT}{P}\,dP\) 下，从参考压强 \(P_0\) 积到 \(P\)：

Ideal-gas chemical potential

\[ \mu(T,P) = \mu_0(T) + k_BT\ln\frac{P}{P_0} \qquad(\text{per mole: } RT\ln\tfrac{P}{P_0}) \]

Higher pressure means higher chemical potential: the molecules are more "eager" to leave. In a gas mixture, each component uses its own partial pressure \(P_i\). This formula is the starting point for chemical equilibrium constants in Chapter 8.压强越大，化学势越高：分子越"想"跑。气体混合物里，每个组分用它自己的分压 \(P_i\)。第 8 章那些平衡常数，起点就是这个式子。

6.1  Which way does a process go?6.1 过程往哪边走？

For an isolated system the Second Law already answers this: entropy goes up, and equilibrium is maximum entropy. But a beaker on a lab bench is not isolated; it exchanges heat with the room. Tracking the room's entropy as well is tedious. What we want is a rule stated only in terms of the system. The potentials provide exactly that:孤立系统的事，第二定律早说清了：熵只增不减，平衡就是熵最大。可实验台上的烧杯不孤立啊，它要跟房间换热。要连房间的熵一起算，太累。我们想要一条只盯着系统自己的规则。势就是干这个的：

Direction of spontaneous change and equilibrium

\[ \begin{aligned} &\text{fixed } T, V: && dF \le 0, &&\text{equilibrium at minimum } F \\ &\text{fixed } T, P: && dG \le 0, &&\text{equilibrium at minimum } G \end{aligned} \]

The constant-\(T\), constant-\(P\) case is by far the most common (anything open to the air at room temperature), so the working rule for the rest of the course is: a process at fixed \(T\) and \(P\) happens by itself only if it lowers \(G\).恒 \(T\)、恒 \(P\) 最常见（凡是敞在室温空气里的都是），所以后面整门课的发力点就是：固定 \(T\)、\(P\) 时，过程只有当它让 \(G\) 变小，才会自己发生。

Proof: where the minimum principles come from证明：极小值原理是从哪来的

Combine the Clausius inequality \(dS \geq \text{đ}Q/T\) with the First Law \(\text{đ}Q = dU - \text{đ}W\):把克劳修斯不等式 \(dS \geq \text{đ}Q/T\) 跟第一定律 \(\text{đ}Q = dU - \text{đ}W\) 拼一块：

\[ T\,dS \geq dU - \text{đ}W \]

Fixed \(T\). Let \(\text{đ}W' = -\text{đ}W\) be the work done by the system. Then \(\text{đ}W' \le -dU + T\,dS = -d(U - TS) = -dF\), using \(T\,dS = d(TS)\) at constant \(T\). If no work is done (fixed \(V\)), \(dF \le 0\).固定 \(T\)。设 \(\text{đ}W' = -\text{đ}W\) 是系统做的功。那 \(\text{đ}W' \le -dU + T\,dS = -d(U - TS) = -dF\)，这里用了恒 \(T\) 下的 \(T\,dS = d(TS)\)。要是不做功（固定 \(V\)），就 \(dF \le 0\)。

Fixed \(T\) and \(P\). The system must at least push back the surroundings, \(\text{đ}W' = P\,dV\) if that is its only work. Then \(P\,dV \le -dF\), and since \(P\) is constant, \(d(F + PV) = dG \le 0\).固定 \(T\)、\(P\)。系统至少得把环境顶开，\(\text{đ}W' = P\,dV\)（如果这是它唯一的功）。那 \(P\,dV \le -dF\)，又因为 \(P\) 不变，\(d(F + PV) = dG \le 0\)。

Equality holds for reversible changes. At equilibrium nothing can lower the potential further, so it sits at a minimum: \(dF = 0, d^2F \gt 0\) or \(dG = 0, d^2G \gt 0\).可逆变化取等号。平衡时没人能把势压得更低，所以它待在最低处：\(dF = 0, d^2F \gt 0\) 或 \(dG = 0, d^2G \gt 0\)。

Example · Does ice melt at −10 °C? At +10 °C?

For melting ice, \(\Delta H = 6.01\ \text{kJ/mol}\), and at 0 °C the process is in equilibrium. Assuming \(\Delta H\) and \(\Delta S\) don't change much with temperature, find the sign of \(\Delta G\) for melting at 263 K and at 283 K.拿冰块说事：\(\Delta H = 6.01\ \text{kJ/mol}\)，0 °C 时正好平衡。假设 \(\Delta H\)、\(\Delta S\) 随温度变化不大，求 263 K 和 283 K 下融化的 \(\Delta G\) 的正负。

Show solution显示解答

At the melting point \(\Delta G = 0\), so \(\Delta S = \Delta H/T_m = 6010/273.15 = 22.0\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\). Then \(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\):熔点时 \(\Delta G = 0\)，所以 \(\Delta S = \Delta H/T_m = 6010/273.15 = 22.0\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\)。于是 \(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\)：

\[ T = 263\ \text{K}: \ \Delta G = 6010 - 263(22.0) \approx +220\ \text{J/mol} \gt 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{ice does not melt} \]

\[ T = 283\ \text{K}: \ \Delta G = 6010 - 283(22.0) \approx -220\ \text{J/mol} \lt 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{ice melts} \]

Melting costs energy (\(\Delta H \gt 0\)) but gains entropy (\(\Delta S \gt 0\)). The \(-T\Delta S\) term grows with temperature, so above 0 °C entropy wins and below it energy wins. That tug of war inside \(G = H - TS\) is the reason phase transitions happen at a definite temperature.融化要花能量（\(\Delta H \gt 0\)），但换来熵（\(\Delta S \gt 0\)）。\(-T\Delta S\) 这项随温度涨，所以 0 °C 以上熵赢，以下能量赢。\(G = H - TS\) 里这个"拔河"，正是相变卡在一个确定温度的原因。

Maximum useful work最大有用功

The proof above also shows that at fixed \(T\) the work a system can deliver is at most \(-\Delta F\), and at fixed \(T\) and \(P\) the work beyond pushing back the atmosphere (e.g. electrical work) is at most \(-\Delta G\).上面那个证明还顺带说明：固定 \(T\) 时，系统最多能做 \(-\Delta F\) 的功；固定 \(T\)、\(P\) 时，除了把大气顶开之外能做的功（比如电功），最多是 \(-\Delta G\)。

Example · A hydrogen fuel cell

For \(\mathrm{H_2} + \tfrac12\mathrm{O_2} \to \mathrm{H_2O(l)}\) at 298 K and 1 bar: \(\Delta H = -286\ \text{kJ/mol}\), \(\Delta G = -237\ \text{kJ/mol}\). How much electrical work can a fuel cell deliver per mole of water, and where does the rest of the energy go?298 K、1 bar 下的 \(\mathrm{H_2} + \tfrac12\mathrm{O_2} \to \mathrm{H_2O(l)}\)：\(\Delta H = -286\ \text{kJ/mol}\)、\(\Delta G = -237\ \text{kJ/mol}\)。燃料电池每摩尔水最多能做多少电功？剩下的能量跑哪去了？

Show solution显示解答

Maximum non-expansion work \(= -\Delta G = 237\ \text{kJ}\). Burning the hydrogen would release \(-\Delta H = 286\ \text{kJ}\) of heat, but even a perfect fuel cell can turn only 237 kJ into electrical work. The remaining \(\Delta H - \Delta G = T\Delta S = -49\ \text{kJ}\) must be released as heat, because the products have lower entropy than the reactants (three molecules of gas become one of liquid), and that entropy has to be dumped into the surroundings.最大非膨胀功 \(= -\Delta G = 237\ \text{kJ}\)。要是直接烧氢，能放出 \(-\Delta H = 286\ \text{kJ}\) 的热，可就算完美的燃料电池，也只能把 237 kJ 变成电。剩下 \(\Delta H - \Delta G = T\Delta S = -49\ \text{kJ}\) 非得当热放掉，因为产物的熵比反应物低（三分子气体变成一分子液体），这部分熵得甩给环境。

6.2  Equilibrium between two parts: equal \(T\), \(P\), \(\mu\)6.2 两部分之间的平衡：\(T\)、\(P\)、\(\mu\) 都相等

Now split a system into two parts that can exchange something. The rule is easy to state:现在把系统劈成两半，让它俩能交换东西。规则好记：

Equilibrium conditions

Whatever the two parts can exchange, the matching intensive variable must be equal on both sides:能交换什么，对应的那个强度量就得两边一样：

\[ \text{energy} \Rightarrow T_1 = T_2, \qquad \text{volume} \Rightarrow P_1 = P_2, \qquad \text{particles} \Rightarrow \mu_1 = \mu_2 \]

When a condition is not met, the system moves to increase its total entropy, which fixes the direction of flow:条件不满足时，系统就往增加总熵的方向走，这也就定了流动方向：

If the parts can exchange两部分能交换Equilibrium needs平衡要求Otherwise不然的话
Energy (heat)能量（热）\(T_1 = T_2\)Heat flows from high \(T\) to low \(T\)热从高 \(T\) 流向低 \(T\)
Volume (movable wall)体积（可动壁）\(P_1 = P_2\)The high-\(P\) side expands\(P\) 高的那边膨胀
Particles (permeable wall)粒子（可渗透壁）\(\mu_1 = \mu_2\)Particles move from high \(\mu\) to low \(\mu\)粒子从高 \(\mu\) 流向低 \(\mu\)

The first two rows are familiar. The third is the new one, and it is what Chapters 7 and 8 run on: whenever particles can move between two places (two phases, a solution and pure solvent, reactants and products), equilibrium means their chemical potentials are equal.前两行都很熟。第三行是新的，也是第 7、8 章的饭碗：只要粒子能在两处之间跑（两个相、溶液和纯溶剂、反应物和产物），平衡就是它们的化学势相等。

Proof: maximise the total entropy证明：总熵取最大

Entropy is extensive, so \(S = S_1(U_1, V_1, N_1) + S_2(U_2, V_2, N_2)\). The whole system is isolated, so \(dU_2 = -dU_1\), \(dV_2 = -dV_1\), \(dN_2 = -dN_1\). From \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\), the entropy derivatives are \(\partial S/\partial U = 1/T\), \(\partial S/\partial V = P/T\), \(\partial S/\partial N = -\mu/T\). So熵是广延量，所以 \(S = S_1(U_1, V_1, N_1) + S_2(U_2, V_2, N_2)\)。整个系统孤立，所以 \(dU_2 = -dU_1\)、\(dV_2 = -dV_1\)、\(dN_2 = -dN_1\)。由 \(dU = T\,dS - P\,dV + \mu\,dN\)，熵的导数是 \(\partial S/\partial U = 1/T\)、\(\partial S/\partial V = P/T\)、\(\partial S/\partial N = -\mu/T\)。于是

\[ dS = \left(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2}\right)dU_1 + \left(\frac{P_1}{T_1} - \frac{P_2}{T_2}\right)dV_1 - \left(\frac{\mu_1}{T_1} - \frac{\mu_2}{T_2}\right)dN_1 \]

At equilibrium \(dS = 0\) for every allowed \(dU_1, dV_1, dN_1\), so each bracket vanishes: \(T_1 = T_2\), then \(P_1 = P_2\), then \(\mu_1 = \mu_2\).平衡时，对每个允许的 \(dU_1, dV_1, dN_1\)，\(dS = 0\) 都得为零，所以每个括号都消失：\(T_1 = T_2\)，然后 \(P_1 = P_2\)，然后 \(\mu_1 = \mu_2\)。

Away from equilibrium a spontaneous change has \(dS \gt 0\). Take just the first term: if \(T_1 \lt T_2\) the bracket is positive, so \(dU_1 \gt 0\): energy flows into the colder part. The other two rows of the table follow the same way.不在平衡时，自发变化满足 \(dS \gt 0\)。单看第一项：如果 \(T_1 \lt T_2\)，括号是正的，那么 \(dU_1 \gt 0\)：能量往冷的那边跑。表格另外两行同理。

6.3  Stability: will it stay there?6.3 稳定性：它会老老实实待着吗？

"Minimum of \(G\)" (or maximum of \(S\)) contains two pieces of information. The first derivative being zero gives the equilibrium conditions above. The second derivative having the right sign says the state is a true minimum, not a maximum or a saddle, so that small random fluctuations get pushed back. That requirement constrains the material itself:"\(G\) 取最小"（或者说 \(S\) 取最大），其实包含了两层意思。一阶导数为零，给出上面那些平衡条件。二阶导数符号对，才说明这是个真·极小值，不是极大值也不是鞍点，小的随机涨落才会被推回去。这一条对材料本身是有要求的：

Stability conditions

\[ C_V \gt 0, \qquad \kappa_T = -\frac{1}{V}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} \gt 0 \]

Both conditions have a direct physical meaning:两个条件都有大白话解释：

  • \(C_V \gt 0\) (thermal stability). If a small region randomly gains some energy, its temperature rises, so heat flows back out of it. With a negative heat capacity, gaining energy would make it colder; it would pull in even more heat and the fluctuation would run away.\(C_V \gt 0\)（热稳定性）。一小块区域要是莫名多了一点能量，温度就升，热就往外流回去。要是热容为负，多能量反而更冷，那它会越吸越多热，涨落直接失控。
  • \(\kappa_T \gt 0\) (mechanical stability). If a small region gets squeezed, its pressure goes up and it pushes back. If squeezing lowered its pressure, it would keep collapsing.\(\kappa_T \gt 0\)（力学稳定性）。一小块区域被一挤，压强升高就顶回去。要是越挤压强越低，它就会一路塌下去。
Where it's used later
  • Chapter 7: below the critical temperature the van der Waals equation has a stretch with \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\), i.e. \(\kappa_T \lt 0\). That uniform fluid is unstable and splits into liquid + gas; this is the liquid–gas transition.第 7 章：临界温度以下，范德瓦尔斯方程有一段 \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\)，也就是 \(\kappa_T \lt 0\)。这样的均匀流体不稳，会裂成液 + 气；这就是液–气相变。
  • Chapter 8: a mixture whose \(G(x)\) curves the wrong way is unstable in the same sense and separates into two phases (oil and water).第 8 章：\(G(x)\) 弯错方向的混合物，也一样不稳，会分成两相（油和水）。
  • Chapter 5: \(\kappa_T \gt 0\) is why \(C_P - C_V = TV\alpha^2/\kappa_T \geq 0\).第 5 章：\(C_P - C_V = TV\alpha^2/\kappa_T \geq 0\) 之所以成立，就是因为 \(\kappa_T \gt 0\)。
Proof: the second variation of entropy证明：熵的二阶变分

\[ d^2S = \tfrac{1}{2}\left[S_{UU}(dU)^2 + 2S_{UV}\,dU\,dV + S_{VV}(dV)^2\right] \]

6.4  Le Chatelier's principle6.4 勒夏特列原理

The stability conditions have a qualitative version that needs no calculation:稳定性条件有个不用算的"定性版"：

Le Chatelier's principle

When a system in equilibrium is disturbed (temperature, pressure, volume or concentration changed), it shifts to a new equilibrium in the direction that partly undoes the disturbance.一个平衡中的系统被扰动（温度、压强、体积、浓度变了），它会移动到一个新平衡，而移动方向恰好部分抵消掉这个扰动。

This is the same logic as \(C_V \gt 0\) and \(\kappa_T \gt 0\): a stable equilibrium pushes back. If it amplified the disturbance instead, it would not have been stable to begin with.这跟 \(C_V \gt 0\)、\(\kappa_T \gt 0\) 是一个逻辑：稳定的平衡会"顶回来"。要是它反而放大扰动，那它一开始就不稳定。

7.1  The key picture: lowest \(\mu\) wins7.1 核心图景：谁的 \(\mu\) 最低谁赢

A phase is a region of matter that is uniform throughout: same density, composition and so on everywhere. Ice, liquid water and steam are three phases of one substance. (A uniform mix of He and N₂ gas is one phase; ice cubes in water are two.)相，就是一块处处均匀的物质：密度、组分啥的都一样。冰、液态水、水蒸气是同一种物质的三个相。（He 和 N₂ 的均匀混合物是一个相；水里的冰块是两个相。）

At given \(T\) and \(P\), each phase has its own chemical potential \(\mu(T,P)\), the Gibbs energy per molecule. Molecules drift from high \(\mu\) to low \(\mu\) (§6.2), so at any \((T,P)\) the substance ends up entirely in the phase with the lowest \(\mu\). Now recall from §5.7:给定 \(T\)、\(P\) 时，每个相都有自己的化学势 \(\mu(T,P)\)，也就是每个分子的吉布斯能量。分子从高 \(\mu\) 往低 \(\mu\) 跑（§6.2），所以无论 \((T,P)\) 是多少，物质最后会整个待在 \(\mu\) 最低的那个相。现在回忆 §5.7：

\[ \left(\frac{\partial\mu}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} = -s, \qquad \left(\frac{\partial\mu}{\partial P}\right)_{\!T} = v \]

So on a plot of \(\mu\) against \(T\), each phase is a line sloping down with slope \(-s\). A gas has much more entropy per molecule than a liquid, which has more than a solid. So the gas line is the steepest and the solid line the flattest:所以在 \(\mu\) 对 \(T\) 的图上，每个相就是一条往下斜的直线，斜率 \(-s\)。气体每分子的熵比液体大得多，液体又比固体大。所以气体线最陡，固体线最平：

Read the picture from left to right:从左往右看这张图：

  • At low \(T\) the solid has the lowest \(\mu\): it is stable.在低的 \(T\) 下，固体的 \(\mu\) 最低：它稳。
  • The steeper liquid line overtakes it at \(T_m\): the substance melts.更陡的液体线在 \(T_m\) 处反超：化了。
  • The even steeper gas line overtakes the liquid at \(T_b\): it boils.最陡的气体线又在 \(T_b\) 处反超液体：沸了。

The transitions are sharp because the lowest line switches abruptly at a crossing. At the crossing itself the two \(\mu\) are equal and both phases can coexist:转变之所以"咔嚓"一下，是因为最低的那条线在交点处突然换人。就在交点上，两个 \(\mu\) 相等，两相共存：

Two phases coexist when

\[ T^{\text{I}} = T^{\text{II}}, \qquad P^{\text{I}} = P^{\text{II}}, \qquad \mu^{\text{I}}(T,P) = \mu^{\text{II}}(T,P) \]

The picture also explains pressure: raising \(P\) raises each \(\mu\) by \(v\,\Delta P\). The gas has by far the largest \(v\), so its line rises most and its crossing with the liquid moves to higher temperature. That is why water boils above 100 °C in a pressure cooker. §7.3 makes this quantitative.这张图还顺便解释了压强：升高 \(P\) 让每条 \(\mu\) 都抬高 \(v\,\Delta P\)。气体 \(v\) 最大，所以它抬得最多，跟液体的交点就往高温挪。所以高压锅里水能在一百多度才沸。§7.3 会把它算成具体的数。

Why a uniform phase can split in two (the entropy argument)均匀相为啥会一裂为二（熵的说法）

Suppose the entropy of a uniform substance, as a function of its energy, has a stretch where \(S(U)\) curves upward. Split the system into two halves with energies \(U + \Delta U\) and \(U - \Delta U\). The total energy is the same, but the average entropy is假如一个均匀物质的熵，作为能量的函数，有一段满足 \(S(U)\) 是往上弯的。把系统劈成两半，能量分别是 \(U + \Delta U\)、\(U - \Delta U\)。总能量不变，但平均熵变成

\[ \tfrac{1}{2}\left[S(U + \Delta U) + S(U - \Delta U)\right] \gt S(U) \]

because a chord lies above an upward-curving curve. Splitting raises the entropy, so the uniform state is not the equilibrium state: the system separates into two parts with different energy densities, i.e. two phases. This is exactly the violation of the stability condition \(\partial^2S/\partial U^2 \lt 0\) from §6.3.因为弦总在往上弯的曲线上方。这么一劈，熵就更大，所以均匀态根本不是平衡态：系统会分成能量密度不同的两块，也就是两个相。这跟 §6.3 的稳定性条件 \(\partial^2S/\partial U^2 \lt 0\) 失效是一回事。

7.3  The Clausius–Clapeyron equation7.3 克劳修斯–克拉珀龙方程

A coexistence line has a definite slope, and we can predict it from two measurable numbers: the latent heat and the volume change.共存线的斜率是定死的，而且能拿两个能测的数算出来：潜热和体积变化。

Clausius–Clapeyron equation

\[ \frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta v} = \frac{L}{T\,\Delta v} \]

Here \(L = T\Delta s\) is the latent heat (heat absorbed going from phase I to phase II) and \(\Delta v\) the change in volume, both for the same amount of substance (per molecule, per mole, or per kilogram).这里 \(L = T\Delta s\) 是潜热（从相 I 到相 II 吸收的热），\(\Delta v\) 是体积变化，都指同一份物质（每分子、每摩尔或每千克都行）。

What it tells you
  • Boiling: \(L \gt 0\) and the gas takes up far more volume (\(\Delta v \gt 0\)), so \(dP/dT \gt 0\): higher pressure → higher boiling point.沸腾：\(L \gt 0\)，而且气体占的体积大得多（\(\Delta v \gt 0\)），所以 \(dP/dT \gt 0\)：压强越大，沸点越高。
  • Melting of ice: \(L \gt 0\), but liquid water is denser than ice, so \(\Delta v \lt 0\) and \(dP/dT \lt 0\). Squeezing ice lowers its melting point. Almost every other substance behaves the opposite way.冰融化：\(L \gt 0\)，可液态水比冰还密，所以 \(\Delta v \lt 0\)、\(dP/dT \lt 0\)。一挤冰，熔点反而降。别的物质几乎全是反过来的。
Proof (three lines)证明（就三行）

Along the coexistence line, \(\mu^{\text{I}} = \mu^{\text{II}}\) at every point, so moving along the line their changes are equal: \(d\mu^{\text{I}} = d\mu^{\text{II}}\). With \(d\mu = -s\,dT + v\,dP\):共存线上每一点 \(\mu^{\text{I}} = \mu^{\text{II}}\)，所以沿线走的时候两边变化一样：\(d\mu^{\text{I}} = d\mu^{\text{II}}\)。用 \(d\mu = -s\,dT + v\,dP\)：

\[ -s^{\text{I}}\,dT + v^{\text{I}}\,dP = -s^{\text{II}}\,dT + v^{\text{II}}\,dP \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{s^{\text{II}} - s^{\text{I}}}{v^{\text{II}} - v^{\text{I}}} \]

The phase change happens at constant \(T\) and is reversible, so the heat absorbed is \(L = T(s^{\text{II}} - s^{\text{I}})\).相变是恒 \(T\) 下可逆发生的，所以吸的热就是 \(L = T(s^{\text{II}} - s^{\text{I}})\)。

Vapour pressure: the integrated form蒸气压：积分形式

For liquid → gas (or solid → gas), three approximations make the equation easy to integrate: the gas volume dominates (\(\Delta V \approx V_g\)); the vapour is ideal (\(V_g = RT/P\) per mole); and \(L\) is roughly constant. Then \(dP/dT = LP/(RT^2)\), which integrates to液 → 气（或固 → 气）时，三个近似让方程好积：气体体积占绝对大头（\(\Delta V \approx V_g\)）；蒸汽当理想气体（每摩尔 \(V_g = RT/P\)）；还有 \(L\) 差不多不变。于是 \(dP/dT = LP/(RT^2)\)，积出来：

Vapour-pressure curve

\[ \ln P = -\frac{L}{RT} + \text{const}, \qquad \ln\frac{P_2}{P_1} = -\frac{L}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}\right) \]

(\(L\) per mole.) Plotting \(\ln P\) against \(1/T\) gives a straight line of slope \(-L/R\); reading \(L\) off such a slope is a standard exam step.（每摩尔 \(L\)。）把 \(\ln P\) 对 \(1/T\) 作图，就是一条斜率为 \(-L/R\) 的直线；从斜率读 \(L\)，这是考试标准操作。

So far we have taken the existence of liquid and gas for granted. The van der Waals equation shows how a single equation of state produces both. Per molecule (\(v = V/N\)):前面我们一直默认"液和气是现成的"。范德瓦尔斯方程厉害的地方，就是用一个状态方程把两者都变出来。按每个分子算是（\(v = V/N\)）：

\[ P = \frac{k_BT}{v - b} - \frac{a}{v^2} \]

\(b\) accounts for the finite size of molecules, \(a\) for their mutual attraction. At high temperature the isotherms look like an ideal gas. Below a critical temperature \(T_c\) each isotherm develops a wiggle containing a stretch where \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\), i.e. \(\kappa_T \lt 0\). By §6.3 that is unstable: a uniform fluid there splits into two phases, liquid and gas.\(b\) 管分子占地方，\(a\) 管它们互相吸引。高温下等温线跟理想气体差不多。一低于某个临界温度 \(T_c\)，每条等温线就开始"打摆子"，其中有一段 \((\partial P/\partial V)_T \gt 0\)，也就是 \(\kappa_T \lt 0\)。§6.3 说了，这就不稳：均匀流体在那儿会裂成液相和气相。

Van der Waals critical point

\[ v_c = 3b, \qquad k_BT_c = \frac{8a}{27b}, \qquad P_c = \frac{a}{27b^2}, \qquad \frac{P_cv_c}{k_BT_c} = \frac{3}{8} \]

\[ -\frac{k_BT}{(v-b)^2} + \frac{2a}{v^3} = 0, \qquad \frac{2k_BT}{(v-b)^3} - \frac{6a}{v^4} = 0 \]

7.5  First-order and continuous transitions7.5 一级相变和连续相变

In melting and boiling, \(\mu\) is continuous across the transition (the two lines cross), but its slopes jump: \(\partial\mu/\partial T = -s\) and \(\partial\mu/\partial P = v\) are different for the two phases. In some transitions the two phases become identical at the transition point: \(\Delta s = 0\), \(\Delta v = 0\), no latent heat. 8.1  The entropy of mixing8.1 混合熵

Put gas A in the left half of a box and gas B in the right half, at the same \(T\) and \(P\). Remove the partition. The gases mix and never unmix by themselves: mixing is irreversible, so it must create entropy. For ideal gases nothing else changes (no interactions, so \(\Delta U = 0\) and \(T\) stays the same); each gas simply spreads into a larger volume, like a free expansion.左边放 A 气、右边放 B 气，温度 \(T\)、压强 \(P\) 都一样。抽掉隔板，两种气体混在一起，再也不会自己分开：混合是不可逆的，所以必然产生熵。理想气体别的啥都没变（没相互作用，所以 \(\Delta U = 0\)、\(T\) 都不变），每种气只是扩到了更大的体积，跟自由膨胀一个样。

Entropy of mixing (ideal)

\[ \Delta S_{\text{mix}} = -Nk_B\sum_i x_i\ln x_i \;\gt\; 0, \qquad x_i = \frac{N_i}{N} \]

Since every \(x_i\) is between 0 and 1, every \(\ln x_i \lt 0\) and the result is positive. Equal amounts of two gases (\(x = \tfrac12\)) give \(Nk_B\ln 2\), where \(N\) is the total number of molecules.因为每个 \(x_i\) 都在 0 和 1 之间，所以每个 \(\ln x_i \lt 0\) 都……结果一定是正的。两种气体各来一份（\(x = \tfrac12\)），得到 \(Nk_B\ln 2\)，其中 \(N\) 是分子总数。

Why this matters for the rest of the chapter

Mixing adds \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}}\) to \(G\), which lowers it. Differentiating that term gives each component a chemical potential lowered by a \(k_BT\ln x\) term:混合给 \(G\) 加了 \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}}\) 这一项，把它压低了。对这一项求个导，就得到每个组分的化学势都被一个 \(k_BT\ln x\) 项拉低：

\[ \mu_i = \mu_i^{0} + k_BT\ln x_i \]

where \(\mu_i^0\) is the pure substance's value. This single fact drives everything below: a solvent with something dissolved in it has a lower \(\mu\) than the pure solvent, so water "prefers" to be in the solution.其中 \(\mu_i^0\) 是纯物质的值。就这一条，撑起了下面所有内容：溶剂里溶了东西，它的 \(\mu\) 就比纯溶剂低，所以水"更愿意"待在溶液里。

Proof of the formula, and the Gibbs paradox公式的证明，以及吉布斯佯谬

Let species \(i\) start with \(N_i\) molecules in its own compartment \(V_i\), all at the same density, so \(V_i/V = x_i\). An ideal gas's entropy depends on volume through \(Nk_B\ln V\). Before mixing: \(k_B\sum_i N_i\ln V_i = k_BN\sum_i x_i\ln x_i + k_BN\ln V\). After mixing every species fills \(V\): \(k_BN\ln V\). The difference is \(-Nk_B\sum_i x_i\ln x_i\).让物种 \(i\) 一开始有 \(N_i\) 个分子待在自己的隔间 \(V_i\) 里，大家密度都一样，所以 \(V_i/V = x_i\)。理想气体的熵是通过 \(Nk_B\ln V\) 依赖体积的。混合前：\(k_B\sum_i N_i\ln V_i = k_BN\sum_i x_i\ln x_i + k_BN\ln V\)。混合后每个物种都铺满 \(V\)：\(k_BN\ln V\)。差值是 \(-Nk_B\sum_i x_i\ln x_i\)。

Gibbs paradox. Repeat with the same gas on both sides. The formula would still predict \(2Nk_B\ln 2\), yet nothing physical has happened; reinserting the partition restores the original state. The resolution is that identical molecules are indistinguishable: the correct volume dependence is \(Nk_B\ln(V/N)\) (as in the Sackur–Tetrode equation), and joining two identical gases at the same density leaves \(V/N\) unchanged, so \(\Delta S = 0\). For different gases, each species' own \(V/N_i\) still grows, so the mixing entropy survives.吉布斯佯谬。换成两边是同一种气体再试一次。公式还是会预测出 \(2Nk_B\ln 2\)，可物理上什么都没变，把隔板插回去就恢复原样了。问题在于：全同分子是不可分辨的。正确的体积依赖是 \(Nk_B\ln(V/N)\)（见萨克–特策罗德方程），把两份等密度的同种气体合起来，\(V/N\) 不变，所以 \(\Delta S = 0\)。对不同的气体，每个物种自己的 \(V/N_i\) 照样变大，混合熵就还在。

8.2  Chemical equilibrium: the law of mass action8.2 化学平衡：质量作用定律

Take \(\mathrm{N_2} + 3\,\mathrm{H_2} \rightleftharpoons 2\,\mathrm{NH_3}\) at fixed \(T\) and \(P\). If the reaction advances a little, N₂ drops by \(\varepsilon\), H₂ by \(3\varepsilon\), and NH₃ rises by \(2\varepsilon\). At equilibrium \(G\) is a minimum, so \(dG = \sum\mu_i\,dN_i = 0\) for this change:拿 \(\mathrm{N_2} + 3\,\mathrm{H_2} \rightleftharpoons 2\,\mathrm{NH_3}\) 来说，固定 \(T\)、\(P\)。反应稍微往前走一点，N₂ 少 \(\varepsilon\)，H₂ 少 \(3\varepsilon\)，NH₃ 多 \(2\varepsilon\)。平衡时 \(G\) 取最小，所以这个变化下 \(dG = \sum\mu_i\,dN_i = 0\)：

Condition for chemical equilibrium

\[ \mu_{\mathrm{N_2}} + 3\,\mu_{\mathrm{H_2}} = 2\,\mu_{\mathrm{NH_3}} \]

The chemical potentials balance with the same coefficients as the reaction equation. Now substitute the ideal-gas form \(\mu_i = g_i^0 + RT\ln(P_i/P_0)\) per mole (§5.7), with \(P_i\) the partial pressure:化学势按反应式同样的系数平衡。现在代入理想气体形式 \(\mu_i = g_i^0 + RT\ln(P_i/P_0)\)（每摩尔，§5.7），其中 \(P_i\) 是分压：

Law of mass action

\[ \frac{(P_{\mathrm{NH_3}}/P_0)^2}{(P_{\mathrm{N_2}}/P_0)(P_{\mathrm{H_2}}/P_0)^3} = K(T), \qquad K(T) = e^{-\Delta g_0/RT} \]

\(\Delta g_0 = 2g^0_{\mathrm{NH_3}} - g^0_{\mathrm{N_2}} - 3g^0_{\mathrm{H_2}}\) (products minus reactants, at the reference pressure \(P_0\)). The equilibrium constant \(K\) depends only on temperature: whatever amounts you start with, the partial pressures adjust until this ratio equals \(K(T)\). Large \(K\) (negative \(\Delta g_0\)) means mostly products.\(\Delta g_0 = 2g^0_{\mathrm{NH_3}} - g^0_{\mathrm{N_2}} - 3g^0_{\mathrm{H_2}}\)（产物减反应物，在参考压强 \(P_0\) 下）。平衡常数 \(K\) 只看温度：不管你一开始放多少，分压都会自己调，直到这个比值等于 \(K(T)\)。\(K\) 很大（\(\Delta g_0\) 为负），说明产物占大头。

Proof: from equal \(\mu\)'s to \(K\)证明：从 \(\mu\) 相等推出 \(K\)

\[ g^0_{\mathrm{N_2}} + RT\ln\tfrac{P_{\mathrm{N_2}}}{P_0} + 3g^0_{\mathrm{H_2}} + 3RT\ln\tfrac{P_{\mathrm{H_2}}}{P_0} = 2g^0_{\mathrm{NH_3}} + 2RT\ln\tfrac{P_{\mathrm{NH_3}}}{P_0} \]

Move the \(g^0\) terms to one side, combine the logarithms, and divide by \(RT\): \(\ln[\ldots] = -\Delta g_0/RT\). Exponentiate.把 \(g^0\) 的项挪到一边，对数合并，再除以 \(RT\)：\(\ln[\ldots] = -\Delta g_0/RT\)。两边取指数。

Pressure effect (Le Chatelier, quantitatively). With mole fractions \(x_i\) and total pressure \(P\), \(P_i = x_iP\), so \(\frac{x_{\mathrm{NH_3}}^2}{x_{\mathrm{N_2}}x_{\mathrm{H_2}}^3} = K(T)\,(P/P_0)^2\). The power 2 is (gas moles on the left) − (gas moles on the right). Raising \(P\) increases the fraction of NH₃, as §6.4 predicted.

How \(K\) changes with temperature\(K\) 怎么随温度变

van 't Hoff equation

\[ \frac{d\ln K}{dT} = \frac{\Delta h_0}{RT^2} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \ln\frac{K_2}{K_1} = -\frac{\Delta h_0}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}\right) \]

\(\Delta h_0\) is the heat absorbed by the reaction. Endothermic (\(\Delta h_0 \gt 0\)): \(K\) grows with \(T\). Exothermic (\(\Delta h_0 \lt 0\)): \(K\) shrinks with \(T\). This is the quantitative form of Le Chatelier. Notice it has exactly the same shape as the vapour-pressure law \(d\ln P/dT = L/RT^2\) from §7.3, because evaporation is just the "reaction" liquid ⇌ vapour.\(\Delta h_0\) 是反应吸收的热。吸热（\(\Delta h_0 \gt 0\)）：\(K\) 随 \(T\) 增大。放热（\(\Delta h_0 \lt 0\)）：\(K\) 随 \(T\) 减小。这就是勒夏特列原理的定量版。注意它跟 §7.3 的蒸气压公式 \(d\ln P/dT = L/RT^2\) 长得一模一样，因为蒸发不过是"反应"液 ⇌ 气。

Proof证明

\(\frac{d}{dT}\left(-\frac{\Delta g_0}{RT}\right) = -\frac{1}{R}\,\frac{T\,(d\Delta g_0/dT) - \Delta g_0}{T^2}\). Use \(d\Delta g_0/dT = -\Delta s_0\) and \(\Delta g_0 = \Delta h_0 - T\Delta s_0\): the numerator becomes \(-T\Delta s_0 - \Delta h_0 + T\Delta s_0 = -\Delta h_0\).\(\frac{d}{dT}\left(-\frac{\Delta g_0}{RT}\right) = -\frac{1}{R}\,\frac{T\,(d\Delta g_0/dT) - \Delta g_0}{T^2}\)。用 \(d\Delta g_0/dT = -\Delta s_0\)、\(\Delta g_0 = \Delta h_0 - T\Delta s_0\)：分子变成 \(-T\Delta s_0 - \Delta h_0 + T\Delta s_0 = -\Delta h_0\)。

8.3  Dilute solutions8.3 稀溶液

A dilute solution has a little solute B (salt, sugar) dissolved in a lot of solvent A (water): \(N_B \ll N_A\). Solute molecules are far apart, so they interact only with the solvent, never with each other. Write \(x = N_B/N_A\) for the (small) solute fraction. The one result we need is how dissolving the solute changes the solvent's chemical potential:稀溶液就是一点溶质 B（盐、糖）溶在一大堆溶剂 A（水）里：\(N_B \ll N_A\)。溶质分子离得老远，只跟溶剂打交道，彼此不搭理。用 \(x = N_B/N_A\) 表示（很小的）溶质分数。我们只要一个结论：溶进去溶质，溶剂的化学势怎么变。

Solvent and solute in a dilute solution

\[ \mu_A = \mu_0(T,P) - x\,k_BT, \qquad \mu_B = f(T,P) + k_BT\ln x \]

\(\mu_0\) is the chemical potential of the pure solvent; \(f\) describes solute–solvent interactions and never appears in the results. The first formula is the important one: dissolving anything lowers the solvent's \(\mu\) by \(xk_BT\), regardless of what the solute is. It is the \(k_BT\ln x_A\) term from §8.1 with \(\ln(1-x) \approx -x\).\(\mu_0\) 是纯溶剂的化学势；\(f\) 描述溶质–溶剂相互作用，后面结果里压根不出现。第一个式子最关键：不管溶质是啥，只要溶进去，溶剂的 \(\mu\) 就降低 \(xk_BT\)。它其实就是 §8.1 里那个 \(k_BT\ln x_A\) 项，取 \(\ln(1-x) \approx -x\)。

Why this matters

Because only the number of dissolved particles enters (not their identity), the effects in §8.4 are called colligative properties. One mole of sugar and one mole of urea shift the freezing point of water by the same amount. A salt like NaCl splits into two ions, so it counts double.因为只看溶进去多少粒子（不管是什么），所以 §8.4 那些效应叫"依数性"。一摩尔糖和一摩尔尿素，把水的凝固点压低得一样多。而 NaCl 这种盐会拆成两个离子，所以得算双份。

Proof: the Gibbs energy of a dilute solution证明：稀溶液的吉布斯能量

Start from pure solvent, \(G = N_A\mu_0\). Adding \(N_B\) solute molecules adds an interaction term \(N_Bf(T,P)\) (proportional to \(N_B\), since solute molecules don't meet each other) and the mixing term \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}} = k_BT[N_B\ln(N_B/N) + N_A\ln(N_A/N)]\). For \(N_B \ll N_A\), \(N \approx N_A\) and \(N_A\ln(N_A/N) \approx -N_B\), so从纯溶剂 \(G = N_A\mu_0\) 出发。加进 \(N_B\) 个溶质分子，多了一个相互作用项 \(N_Bf(T,P)\)（正比于 \(N_B\)，因为溶质之间碰不到），再加混合项 \(-T\Delta S_{\text{mix}} = k_BT[N_B\ln(N_B/N) + N_A\ln(N_A/N)]\)。对 \(N_B \ll N_A\)，\(N \approx N_A\)、\(N_A\ln(N_A/N) \approx -N_B\)，所以

\[ G \approx N_A\mu_0 + N_Bf - N_Bk_BT\ln N_A + N_Bk_BT\ln N_B - N_Bk_BT \]

Differentiating: \(\mu_A = \partial G/\partial N_A = \mu_0 - N_Bk_BT/N_A\) and \(\mu_B = \partial G/\partial N_B = f + k_BT\ln(N_B/N_A)\).求导：\(\mu_A = \partial G/\partial N_A = \mu_0 - N_Bk_BT/N_A\)、\(\mu_B = \partial G/\partial N_B = f + k_BT\ln(N_B/N_A)\)。

8.4  Colligative properties8.4 依数性

All four results below come from the three-step recipe in the chapter introduction. The pure solvent's own equilibrium point (its boiling point, freezing point, or vapour pressure) is the point we expand around.下面四个结论，全都照章开头那个三步套路来。纯溶剂自己的平衡点（沸点、凝固点、蒸气压）就是展开的地方。

Raoult's law: vapour pressure is lowered拉乌尔定律：蒸气压下降

Raoult's law

\[ P \approx P_0(1 - x) \]

\(P_0\) is the vapour pressure of the pure solvent. With solute dissolved, the solvent's \(\mu\) in the liquid is lower, so fewer molecules "want" to escape and the equilibrium vapour pressure drops by the fraction \(x\).\(P_0\) 是纯溶剂的蒸气压。溶了东西以后，液相里溶剂的 \(\mu\) 变低，想跑出来的分子就少了，平衡蒸气压按比例下降 \(x\)。

Proof证明

Step 2: the vapour is pure solvent, so \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P) = \mu_0(T,P) - xk_BT\). For pure solvent, \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P_0) = \mu_0(T,P_0)\).第 2 步：气相是纯溶剂，所以 \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P) = \mu_0(T,P) - xk_BT\)。纯溶剂时 \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T,P_0) = \mu_0(T,P_0)\)。

Step 3: expand both sides about \(P_0\) using \(\partial\mu/\partial P = v\): \(v_{\text{gas}}(P - P_0) = v_{\text{liq}}(P - P_0) - xk_BT\). Drop \(v_{\text{liq}} \ll v_{\text{gas}}\) and use \(v_{\text{gas}} = k_BT/P_0\): \(P - P_0 = -xP_0\).第 3 步：用 \(\partial\mu/\partial P = v\) 把两边在 \(P_0\) 附近展开：\(v_{\text{gas}}(P - P_0) = v_{\text{liq}}(P - P_0) - xk_BT\)。舍掉 \(v_{\text{liq}} \ll v_{\text{gas}}\)，用 \(v_{\text{gas}} = k_BT/P_0\)：\(P - P_0 = -xP_0\)。

Boiling point goes up, freezing point goes down沸点升高，凝固点降低

Boiling-point elevation and freezing-point depression

\[ \Delta T_b \approx +\frac{x\,RT_b^2}{L_{\text{vap}}}, \qquad \Delta T_m \approx -\frac{x\,RT_m^2}{L_{\text{fus}}} \]

(\(L\) per mole; per molecule replace \(R\) by \(k_B\).) The solute stays in the liquid; the vapour and the ice are pure solvent. Dissolving lowers \(\mu\) of the liquid only. In the \(\mu(T)\) picture of §7.1, the liquid line moves down, so it crosses the gas line later (higher \(T_b\)) and the solid line earlier (lower \(T_m\)). The liquid range widens at both ends. This is why salted roads don't ice over and why antifreeze is added to car radiators.（每摩尔 \(L\)；按每分子算就把 \(R\) 换成 \(k_B\)。）溶质留在液体里，蒸气和冰都是纯溶剂。溶解只把液相的 \(\mu\) 压低了。放到 §7.1 那张 \(\mu(T)\) 图上看，液相线往下移，于是它跟气相线相交得更晚（沸点更高 \(T_b\)）、跟固相线相交得更早（凝固点更低 \(T_m\)）。液相能稳定存在的温度范围两头都变宽了。所以撒了盐的路不容易结冰，汽车水箱里要加防冻液。

Proof (boiling; freezing is identical)证明（沸腾；凝固一模一样）

At fixed \(P\), the solution boils where \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T) = \mu_0(T) - xk_BT\). Pure solvent: \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T_b) = \mu_0(T_b)\). Expand about \(T_b\) with \(\partial\mu/\partial T = -s\):固定 \(P\)，溶液在哪沸腾？在 \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T) = \mu_0(T) - xk_BT\) 处。纯溶剂是 \(\mu_{\text{gas}}(T_b) = \mu_0(T_b)\)。用 \(\partial\mu/\partial T = -s\) 在 \(T_b\) 附近展开：

\[ -s_{\text{gas}}\,\Delta T = -s_{\text{liq}}\,\Delta T - xk_BT \;\Rightarrow\; \Delta T = \frac{xk_BT_b}{s_{\text{gas}} - s_{\text{liq}}} = \frac{xk_BT_b^2}{L_{\text{vap}}} \]

For freezing, replace the gas by ice: \((s_{\text{ice}} - s_{\text{liq}})\Delta T = xk_BT\), and since \(s_{\text{ice}} \lt s_{\text{liq}}\) the shift is negative: \(\Delta T = -xk_BT_m^2/L_{\text{fus}}\).凝固的话，把气相换成冰：\((s_{\text{ice}} - s_{\text{liq}})\Delta T = xk_BT\)，因为 \(s_{\text{ice}} \lt s_{\text{liq}}\)，所以偏移是负的：\(\Delta T = -xk_BT_m^2/L_{\text{fus}}\)。

Osmotic pressure渗透压

A semi-permeable membrane lets water through but not the solute. Put pure water on one side and solution on the other. Water flows into the solution (its \(\mu\) is lower there) until the solution side is at a higher pressure that stops the flow, e.g. a column of height \(h\) with \(\Pi = \rho gh\).半透膜只放水过去，不放溶质。一边纯水，一边溶液。水会往溶液这边跑（那边水的 \(\mu\) 更低），直到溶液这边压强够高、把水挡住，比如高出一根 \(h\) 的液柱，\(\Pi = \rho gh\)。

Osmotic pressure

\[ \Pi = P_2 - P_1 = \frac{N_Bk_BT}{V} = \frac{n_BRT}{V} \]

It has exactly the form of the ideal gas law: the dissolved particles push as if they were a gas filling the solution's volume.这跟理想气体定律长得一模一样：溶质粒子推出来的压强，就像它们是一团占满溶液体积的气体。

Proof证明

Water's \(\mu\) must match across the membrane: \(\mu_0(T,P_1) = \mu_0(T,P_2) - xk_BT\). Expand \(\mu_0(T,P_2) \approx \mu_0(T,P_1) + v_w(P_2 - P_1)\) with \(v_w\) the volume per water molecule: \(v_w(P_2 - P_1) = xk_BT = N_Bk_BT/N_A\). Since \(N_Av_w \approx V\), \(P_2 - P_1 = N_Bk_BT/V\).膜两边水的 \(\mu\) 得相等：\(\mu_0(T,P_1) = \mu_0(T,P_2) - xk_BT\)。用 \(v_w\)（每个水分子的体积）把 \(\mu_0(T,P_2) \approx \mu_0(T,P_1) + v_w(P_2 - P_1)\) 展开：\(v_w(P_2 - P_1) = xk_BT = N_Bk_BT/N_A\)。又因为 \(N_Av_w \approx V\)，所以 \(P_2 - P_1 = N_Bk_BT/V\)。

8.5  Binary mixtures: mix or separate?8.5 二元混合物：混还是分？

Now drop "dilute": mix two components A and B in any proportion, \(x = N_B/(N_A + N_B)\) from 0 (pure A) to 1 (pure B). Will they form one uniform mixture, or separate like oil and water? Compare Gibbs energies.现在不管"稀"了：两种组分 A、B 随便什么比例，\(x = N_B/(N_A + N_B)\) 从 0（纯 A）到 1（纯 B）。它们会混成均匀一锅，还是像油和水一样分开？比吉布斯能量。

Unmixed, \(G\) is just a weighted average of the pure substances — a straight line in \(x\). Mixing ideal components adds only the mixing entropy:不混的话，\(G\) 就是两个纯物质的加权平均——在 \(x\) 里是一条直线。理想组分一混，只多出混合熵：

Ideal mixture

\[ G = (1-x)G_A^0 + xG_B^0 + Nk_BT\left[(1-x)\ln(1-x) + x\ln x\right] \]

The bracket is negative and curves upward, so \(G(x)\) sags below the straight line everywhere: ideal components always mix, in any proportion. (In fact the slope of \(x\ln x\) is infinite at \(x \to 0\), so adding the first trace of B always lowers \(G\): no substance in contact with another stays perfectly pure.)那个括号是负的，而且是往上弯的，所以 \(G(x)\) 在直线下方，哪儿都是：理想组分永远会混，什么比例都混。（其实 \(x\ln x\) 在 \(x \to 0\) 处的斜率是无穷大，所以哪怕只加一丁点 B，也会让 \(G\) 降下来：没有哪样东西跟别的东西挨着还能保持绝对纯。）

When mixing costs energy当混合要耗能时

In real mixtures, unlike molecules may attract each other less than like ones (oil and water), so mixing raises the energy. Now two terms compete: the energy term makes \(G(x)\) bulge upward in the middle, while the entropy term pulls it down. At low temperature the energy wins and \(G(x)\) develops a stretch that curves the wrong way. A uniform mixture there is unstable and lowers its \(G\) by splitting into an A-rich phase and a B-rich phase.真实混合物里，异种分子之间的吸引可能比同种弱（油和水），于是混合要耗能。现在两项打架：能量项让 \(G(x)\) 中间鼓起来，熵项往下拉。低温时能量赢，\(G(x)\) 就出现一段弯错方向的。这儿的均匀混合物不稳，会裂成一个富 A 相和一个富 B 相，从而把 \(G\) 降下来。

Compositions between \(x_a\) and \(x_b\) are a solubility gap: the two components are immiscible there. Raising \(T\) strengthens the entropy term, the gap narrows, and above a certain temperature it disappears. Plotting \(x_a\) and \(x_b\) against \(T\) gives a dome, with two phases inside and one uniform mixture outside, just like the liquid–gas dome in §7.4.\(x_a\) 到 \(x_b\) 之间叫溶解度间隙：在这段里两种组分互不相溶。升高 \(T\)，熵项增强，间隙变窄，超过某个温度就没了。把 \(x_a\)、\(x_b\) 对 \(T\) 画出来，是个穹顶：里面两相，外面一相，跟 §7.4 的液–气穹顶一模一样。

8.6  The common-tangent rule and the lever rule8.6 公切线法则与杠杆定则

Two practical tools for reading binary phase diagrams:读二元相图的两个实用工具：

Common-tangent rule

Two phases \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) coexist at the compositions where a single straight line is tangent to both \(G(x)\) curves (or both parts of one curve).两个相 \(\alpha\)、\(\beta\) 共存，就发生在一条直线同时跟两条 \(G(x)\) 曲线（或者一条曲线的两段）相切的组分处。

Why: equilibrium needs \(\mu_A\) equal in both phases and \(\mu_B\) equal in both phases. The tangent line to \(g(x) = G/N\) at composition \(x\) hits the \(x = 0\) axis at \(\mu_A\) and the \(x = 1\) axis at \(\mu_B\). Equal \(\mu\)'s in two phases means equal intercepts, which means the same tangent line.为啥？平衡要求两个相里 \(\mu_A\) 相等、\(\mu_B\) 也相等。组分 \(x\) 处 \(g(x) = G/N\) 的切线，在 \(x = 0\) 轴上是 \(\mu_A\)，在 \(x = 1\) 轴上是 \(\mu_B\)。两相里 \(\mu\) 相等，就是截距相等，也就是同一条切线。

Proof: chemical potentials are tangent intercepts证明：化学势就是切线的截距

With \(G = Ng(x)\), \(N = N_A + N_B\), \(x = N_B/N\): \(\partial x/\partial N_A = -x/N\), \(\partial x/\partial N_B = (1-x)/N\). So有 \(G = Ng(x)\)、\(N = N_A + N_B\)、\(x = N_B/N\)：\(\partial x/\partial N_A = -x/N\)、\(\partial x/\partial N_B = (1-x)/N\)。于是

\[ \mu_A = \frac{\partial G}{\partial N_A} = g - x\,g'(x), \qquad \mu_B = \frac{\partial G}{\partial N_B} = g + (1-x)\,g'(x) \]

The tangent at \(x\) is \(\ell(y) = g(x) + g'(x)(y - x)\); \(\ell(0) = \mu_A\) and \(\ell(1) = \mu_B\).在 \(x\) 处的切线是 \(\ell(y) = g(x) + g'(x)(y - x)\)；\(\ell(0) = \mu_A\)、\(\ell(1) = \mu_B\)。

Once the coexisting compositions \(x^\alpha\) and \(x^\beta\) are known, conservation of B fixes how much of each phase there is. If a fraction \(\mathcal{P}\) of the particles is in phase \(\beta\) and the overall composition is \(x_0\), then \((1-\mathcal{P})x^\alpha + \mathcal{P}x^\beta = x_0\):共存组分 \(x^\alpha\)、\(x^\beta\) 求出来以后，B 的守恒就定下了每个相有多少。如果粒子中有比例 \(\mathcal{P}\) 在相 \(\beta\) 里，总组分是 \(x_0\)，那么 \((1-\mathcal{P})x^\alpha + \mathcal{P}x^\beta = x_0\)：

Lever rule

\[ \mathcal{P}_\beta = \frac{x_0 - x^\alpha}{x^\beta - x^\alpha}, \qquad \mathcal{P}_\alpha = \frac{x^\beta - x_0}{x^\beta - x^\alpha} \]

On the diagram: the amount of each phase is proportional to the length of the segment on the opposite side of \(x_0\). If \(x_0\) is close to \(x^\alpha\), most of the system is phase \(\alpha\).图上：每个相的量，正比于 \(x_0\) 另一侧那段长度。\(x_0\) 越靠近 \(x^\alpha\)，系统就越多是相 \(\alpha\)。

Building a phase diagram构建相图

Given \(G(x)\) curves for, say, the liquid and gas at many temperatures: wherever the curves cross, draw the common tangent and mark its two touching points; then plot those compositions against \(T\). For two liquids with boiling points \(T_A \lt T_B\) this gives a lens-shaped two-phase region between \(T_A\) and \(T_B\). The gas is always richer in the more volatile component (the lower boiling point).假设你有一堆温度下液相、气相的 \(G(x)\) 曲线：哪里曲线相交，就在那儿画公切线、标出两个切点；然后把这些组分对 \(T\) 画出来。沸点 \(T_A \lt T_B\) 的两种液体，会得到 \(T_A\) 到 \(T_B\) 之间一个透镜状的两相区。气相永远更富含更易挥发（沸点更低）的那个组分。

Example · Liquefying air (course example)

Air (21% O₂, 79% N₂) is cooled to 80.5 K. The phase diagram shows gas with oxygen fraction \(x_g = 0.15\) coexisting with liquid at \(x_l = 0.40\). What fraction of the molecules is still gas?空气（21% O₂、79% N₂）冷到 80.5 K。相图显示氧分数 \(x_g = 0.15\) 的气相，跟 \(x_l = 0.40\) 的液相共存。还有多少比例是气体？

\[ \mathcal{P}_{\text{gas}} = \frac{x_l - x_0}{x_l - x_g} = \frac{0.40 - 0.21}{0.40 - 0.15} = 0.76 \]

76% is still gas; 24% has condensed. The liquid is oxygen-rich because oxygen boils at a higher temperature (90 K) than nitrogen (77 K). (The slide labels this temperature range "°C"; it is kelvin.)76% 还是气体，24% 凝结了。液相富氧，因为氧的沸点（90 K）比氮（77 K）高。（幻灯片把这段温度标成"°C"，其实是开尔文。）

Eutectic mixtures共晶混合物

Some pairs mix completely as liquids but form two different solid phases: \(\alpha\) (mostly A) and \(\beta\) (mostly B). On cooling, solid \(\alpha\) or \(\beta\) starts to separate out, and the melting curves fall from the pure melting points \(T_A\) and \(T_B\) towards each other. They meet at the eutectic point, the composition with the lowest melting temperature of all, lower than either pure substance. This is freezing-point depression pushed to large concentrations. Example from the slides: water + ethylene glycol (car coolant) has its eutectic near −49 °C at about 56% glycol; a 50% mixture freezes at about −31 °C.有些搭配液态时完全互溶，但会形成两种不同的固相：\(\alpha\)（基本是 A）和 \(\beta\)（基本是 B）。降温时，固体 \(\alpha\) 或 \(\beta\) 先析出来，两条熔化曲线从纯物质的熔点 \(T_A\) 和 \(T_B\) 互相靠拢，最后在共晶点碰头——熔点最低的那个组分，比两个纯物质都低。这就是把凝固点降低推到高浓度。幻灯片上的例子：水 + 乙二醇（汽车冷却液），共晶点大约 −49 °C、乙二醇约 56%；50% 的混合物约 −31 °C 结冰。

Chapter 9

The Third Law of Thermodynamics热力学第三定律

What this chapter does

The First and Second Laws only ever talk about changes, \(\Delta U\) and \(\Delta S\). The Third Law is about the bottom of the temperature scale: it gives entropy an absolute zero, and it explains why 0 K can be approached but never reached. It is a short chapter that reuses tools from Chapters 4, 5 and 7.第一、第二定律讲来讲去都是"变化"，\(\Delta U\)、\(\Delta S\)。第三定律管的是温标的最底下：它给熵定了个绝对零点，还解释了为啥 0 K 只能无限逼近、永远到不了。这章很短，用的全是第 4、5、7 章的旧工具。

After this chapter you should be able to
  • state the Third Law (Nernst and Planck forms) and explain it with \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\);说清第三定律（能斯特版和普朗克版），并用 \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\) 解释它；
  • compute an absolute entropy from heat capacities and latent heats;用热容和潜热算绝对熵；
  • derive the consequences \(C \to 0\), \(\alpha \to 0\), flat phase boundaries, and the unattainability of 0 K.推出那些推论：\(C \to 0\)、\(\alpha \to 0\)、相界变平、0 K 到不了。

9.1  Why worry about absolute zero?9.1 为啥要在意绝对零度？

A reversible refrigerator removing heat \(Q_C\) from a cold body at \(T_C\) and dumping it at \(T_H\) needs work (§3.9, §4.4)一台可逆冰箱，从 \(T_C\) 的冷物体里抽走热 \(Q_C\)，再排到 \(T_H\) 去，要花的功是（§3.9、§4.4）

\[ W = Q_C\left(\frac{T_H}{T_C} - 1\right) \]

As \(T_C \to 0\) this blows up: extracting even a little heat from something near 0 K costs enormous work. So refrigeration alone cannot reach absolute zero. The Third Law turns this observation into a general principle (§9.4).当 \(T_C \to 0\) 时，这数字直接爆炸：从接近 0 K 的东西里抽一点点热，就得花海量的功。所以光靠制冷到不了绝对零度。第三定律把这个观察变成了一条普遍原理（§9.4）。

9.2  Statements of the Third Law9.2 第三定律的几种说法

Nernst statement

For any isothermal process between equilibrium (condensed) states, \(\displaystyle\lim_{T\to0}\Delta S = 0\).对任何在两个平衡（凝聚）态之间的等温过程，\(\displaystyle\lim_{T\to0}\Delta S = 0\)。

Planck statement

The entropy of a perfect crystal is zero at absolute zero: \(S(T = 0) = 0\).完美晶体在绝对零度时熵为零：\(S(T = 0) = 0\)。

Nernst says all entropy differences vanish at 0 K, so every state of a substance approaches the same entropy there. Planck sets that common value to zero. Unlike energy, whose zero is a convention, entropy then has an absolute value.能斯特说的是：0 K 时所有熵差都消失，所以一种物质的每个态到那儿都趋于同一个熵。普朗克再把这个共同值定成 0。于是熵就有了绝对值——不像能量，零点只是人为约定的。

The microscopic reason is \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\) (§4.11). At \(T = 0\) a perfect crystal sits in its single lowest-energy arrangement: \(\Omega = 1\), so \(S = 0\). There is no remaining uncertainty about the microstate.微观原因就是 \(S = k_B\ln\Omega\)（§4.11）。在 \(T = 0\)，完美晶体待在自己独一无二的最低能量排列里：\(\Omega = 1\)，所以 \(S = 0\)。微观态上再没有任何不确定。

Residual entropy. If the lowest-energy state is not unique, \(\Omega_0 \gt 1\) and \(S(0) = k_B\ln\Omega_0 \gt 0\). In a crystal of CO, each molecule can point either way (CO or OC) at almost the same energy. If the orientations freeze in at random, \(\Omega_0 = 2^N\), and the crystal keeps \(Nk_B\ln 2\), i.e. \(R\ln 2\) per mole.
How Nernst's statement shows up in chemistry data能斯特那句话在化学数据里长啥样

For an isothermal reaction, \(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\), so \(\Delta S = (\Delta H - \Delta G)/T\). At \(T = 0\), \(\Delta G = \Delta H\), and the limit is \(0/0\). By L'Hôpital's rule, \(\lim\Delta S = (\partial\Delta H/\partial T)_0 - (\partial\Delta G/\partial T)_0\). Measured \(\Delta G(T)\) and \(\Delta H(T)\) curves meet at \(T = 0\) and both become flat there: \(\partial\Delta G/\partial T = -\Delta S \to 0\) and \(\partial\Delta H/\partial T = \Delta C_P \to 0\).对等温反应，\(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\)，所以 \(\Delta S = (\Delta H - \Delta G)/T\)。在 \(T = 0\) 处，\(\Delta G = \Delta H\)，极限是 \(0/0\) 型。洛必达一下：\(\lim\Delta S = (\partial\Delta H/\partial T)_0 - (\partial\Delta G/\partial T)_0\)。实测的 \(\Delta G(T)\) 和 \(\Delta H(T)\) 曲线在 \(T = 0\) 处相交，而且都在那儿变平：\(\partial\Delta G/\partial T = -\Delta S \to 0\)、\(\partial\Delta H/\partial T = \Delta C_P \to 0\)。

9.3  Absolute entropy9.3 绝对熵

With \(S(0) = 0\) as the reference, the entropy at any temperature is obtained by adding up \(\text{đ}Q/T\) for all the heat needed to bring the substance up from 0 K:以 \(S(0) = 0\) 为参考，任何温度下的熵，就是把物质从 0 K 加热到该温度所需的所有热累加起来 \(\text{đ}Q/T\)：

Absolute (Third-Law) entropy

\[ S(T) = \int_0^T\frac{C(T')}{T'}\,dT' + \sum_{\text{transitions}}\frac{\Delta H_{\text{trans}}}{T_{\text{trans}}} \]

The integral uses each phase's own heat capacity; each melting or boiling on the way adds its latent heat divided by its temperature. Tabulated entropies (like the diamond and graphite values in Chapter 7) are made this way. In problems, though, you usually need entropy changes, and the same two pieces (\(\int C\,dT/T\) and \(L/T\)) are what you add up.积分时要分段用每个相自己的热容；路上每次熔化、沸腾，还要加上"潜热除以温度"。数据表里的熵（比如第 7 章金刚石和石墨的值）就是这么来的。不过做题时，通常要的是熵变，而你累加的还是那两块（\(\int C\,dT/T\) 和 \(L/T\)）。

Example · An ice cube melting in the kitchen (course example)

A 30 g ice cube at 0 °C melts on a table in a 25 °C kitchen and warms to 25 °C. Find the entropy change of (a) the ice/water, (b) the kitchen, (c) the universe. (\(L = 333\ \text{J/g}\), \(c = 4.186\ \text{J/(g·K)}\).)一块 30 g、0 °C 的冰，在 25 °C 的厨房桌子上化掉，再升到 25 °C。求 (a) 冰/水、(b) 厨房、(c) 宇宙的熵变。（\(L = 333\ \text{J/g}\)、\(c = 4.186\ \text{J/(g·K)}\)。）

Show solution显示解答

(a) Melting at 273 K: \(\Delta S_1 = mL/T = (30)(333)/273 = 36.6\ \text{J/K}\). Warming 273 → 298 K: \(\Delta S_2 = mc\ln(T_f/T_i) = (30)(4.186)\ln(298/273) = 11.0\ \text{J/K}\). Total \(47.6\ \text{J/K}\).(a) 273 K 下融化：\(\Delta S_1 = mL/T = (30)(333)/273 = 36.6\ \text{J/K}\)。273 → 298 K 升温：\(\Delta S_2 = mc\ln(T_f/T_i) = (30)(4.186)\ln(298/273) = 11.0\ \text{J/K}\)。一共 \(47.6\ \text{J/K}\)。

(b) The kitchen is a reservoir at 298 K giving up all that heat: \(Q = (30)(333) + (30)(4.186)(25) = 13\,130\ \text{J}\), so \(\Delta S = -13\,130/298 = -44.1\ \text{J/K}\).(b) 厨房是 298 K 的热库，把这些热全给出去：\(Q = (30)(333) + (30)(4.186)(25) = 13\,130\ \text{J}\)，所以 \(\Delta S = -13\,130/298 = -44.1\ \text{J/K}\)。

(c) \(47.6 - 44.1 = +3.5\ \text{J/K} \gt 0\): irreversible, as heat flowed across a finite temperature difference. The ice gains more entropy than the kitchen loses because it receives the heat at a lower temperature, and \(\Delta S = \int\text{đ}Q/T\).(c) \(47.6 - 44.1 = +3.5\ \text{J/K} \gt 0\)：不可逆，因为热跨过了有限温差。冰得到的熵比厨房失去的多，因为它是在更低的温度下"收货"，\(\Delta S = \int\text{đ}Q/T\)。

9.4  Consequences of the Third Law9.4 第三定律的推论

Each consequence below uses one tool from earlier chapters together with "\(\Delta S \to 0\) as \(T \to 0\)".下面每条，都是把前面章节的一个工具，配上"\(\Delta S \to 0\) 当 \(T \to 0\)"得到的。

(i) Heat capacities vanish(i) 热容趋于零

As \(T \to 0\)

\[ C_P \to 0, \qquad C_V \to 0 \]

Reason: \(S(T) = \int_0^T C\,dT'/T'\) must be finite. If \(C\) stayed at a nonzero value, \(\int dT'/T'\) would diverge logarithmically. So the classical ideal gas, with constant \(C_V = \tfrac32Nk_B\), must fail at very low temperature; real matter is quantum there, and its heat capacity falls to zero (for example as \(T^3\) for vibrations in a solid).理由：\(S(T) = \int_0^T C\,dT'/T'\) 必须是有限的。要是 \(C\) 一直是非零常数，\(\int dT'/T'\) 就会对数发散。所以热容为常数 \(C_V = \tfrac32Nk_B\) 的经典理想气体，在极低温一定失效；真实物质在低温下是量子的，热容会掉到零（比如固体振动按 \(T^3\) 掉）。

(ii) Thermal expansion vanishes(ii) 热膨胀趋于零

As \(T \to 0\)

\[ \alpha = \frac{1}{V}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_{\!P} \to 0 \]

Reason: by Nernst, compressing isothermally at \(T \to 0\) changes no entropy, \((\partial S/\partial P)_T \to 0\). The Maxwell relation \((\partial S/\partial P)_T = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P\) (§5.4) then gives \(V\alpha \to 0\). Likewise \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = (\partial S/\partial V)_T \to 0\).理由：能斯特定理说，\(T \to 0\) 下等温压缩不改变熵，\((\partial S/\partial P)_T \to 0\)。再用麦克斯韦关系 \((\partial S/\partial P)_T = -(\partial V/\partial T)_P\)（§5.4）就得到 \(V\alpha \to 0\)。同理 \((\partial P/\partial T)_V = (\partial S/\partial V)_T \to 0\)。

(iii) Phase boundaries become flat(iii) 相界变平

As \(T \to 0\)

\[ \frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta v} \to 0 \]

Reason: Clausius–Clapeyron (§7.3) with \(\Delta s \to 0\) and \(\Delta v\) finite. Any coexistence line that reaches 0 K must meet the axis horizontally, as the melting curve of helium does.理由：克劳修斯–克拉珀龙方程（§7.3），因为 \(\Delta s \to 0\)、\(\Delta v\) 都有限。任何一直延伸到 0 K 的共存线，必须水平地碰到轴——氦的熔化曲线就是这样。

(iv) Absolute zero cannot be reached(iv) 绝对零度到不了

Cooling is done in alternating steps between two values of a control parameter (volume, pressure, or magnetic field as in adiabatic demagnetisation, §5.6). An isothermal step (in contact with the coldest reservoir available) lowers \(S\) at fixed \(T\); an isentropic step (isolated) then lowers \(T\) at fixed \(S\). The final step can't be isothermal, since that would need a reservoir already at 0 K.制冷是这样干的：在两个控制参数（体积、压强或者磁场，比如绝热去磁，§5.6）取值之间来回切换。等温一步（贴着目前最冷的热库）在固定 \(T\) 下把 \(S\) 降下来；然后绝热一步（孤立），在固定 \(S\) 下把 \(T\) 降下来。最后一步不可能是等温的，那需要一个本来就在 0 K 的热库。

Because \(\Delta S \to 0\), the two curves meet at \(T = 0\). Each vertical drop gets smaller, and so does each move to the left. (In the figure each cycle exactly halves \(T\).) If the curves did not meet, a single isentropic step from a low enough temperature could land exactly on 0 K.因为 \(\Delta S \to 0\)，两条曲线在 \(T = 0\) 处相交。于是每次竖直往下掉的幅度越掉越小，每次往左挪的幅度也是。（图里每个循环正好把 \(T\) 减半。）要是两条曲线不相交，从一个足够低的温度出发，一次绝热步骤就能正好踩到 0 K。

Unattainability statement

Absolute zero cannot be reached by any process in a finite number of steps or in a finite time.任何过程，不管多少步、花多少时间，都到不了绝对零度。
